Tà Xùa or Sapa: the two places are **not close to each other**. Tà Xùa is in Sơn La, Sapa is in Lào Cai, and they don't sit on the same road axis, so combining them in one short trip is very hard. Tà Xùa is closer to Hanoi (about 215km versus about 320km for Sapa); which one to pick should depend on whether you want cloud hunting right from your room, need town-style comforts, or are looking for somewhere quiet like Y Tý.

**Quick summary:**

- **Are they close:** no. Tà Xùa (Sơn La) and Sapa (Lào Cai) are in two different provinces; the road between them is long and winds over many passes. Tà Xùa Booking has no bus route running directly between the two.
- **Which is farther from Hanoi:** Sapa. Hanoi to Tà Xùa is about 210 to 215km, and the overnight sleeper cabin bus reaches the centre around 6am; Hanoi to Sapa is about 320km, 5 to 6 hours by expressway; Y Tý is the farthest, about 370km on difficult roads.
- **Cloud hunting from your room:** Tà Xùa has the edge. 17 of the 38 homestays on Tà Xùa Booking have rooms with a cloud view, and the cloud season usually runs from October to April.
- **Comfort, travelling with older people:** Sapa has more hotels, restaurants and services, plus an expressway, but it is busier.
- **Love quiet places and rice terraces:** Y Tý is remote and quiet, strong on rice terraces and clouds; allow 3 days or more.
- **Tà Xùa costs:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi is 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person, and homestays have a weekday listed price from 200.000đ per night.

## Is Tà Xùa close to Sapa, and how far apart are they?

The short answer is **no**. Both get called "the Northwest", but they belong to two different provinces:

- **Tà Xùa** is in **Sơn La** province; the last stretch is a 15km steep mountain pass up from Bắc Yên.
- **Sapa** and **Y Tý** are in **Lào Cai** province.

The two are not on the same road axis from Hanoi. The road from Hanoi to Tà Xùa heads to Bắc Yên and then climbs the pass, while Sapa is reached by the expressway towards Lào Cai. To get from one to the other you have to wind over many passes and spend a very long time on the road. We don't give a specific distance or travel time between the two because we haven't been able to verify it; ask a local transport operator before you go.

A tip when using maps: type "Tà Xùa, Sơn La". There is another village called Tà Xùa in Trạm Tấu (Yên Bái), which can easily send the map the wrong way. The exact location and the route from Hanoi are in our article [Where is Tà Xùa and how far is it from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).

## Should you combine Tà Xùa and Sapa in one trip?

With a 2 to 4 day schedule, most travellers **do them as separate trips**. The reasons are very practical:

- The transfer between the two is very long and easily eats into the best hours at both places.
- There is no direct bus route between Tà Xùa and Sapa on our system, so you'd have to hire a vehicle or drive yourself over the passes.
- Cloud hunting means sleeping up on the mountain and getting up before 6am. Losing a night to travel means losing a morning of cloud hunting.

If you have 4 days or more and enjoy long rides, a Northwest loop is still a sensible choice. The route more people pair with Tà Xùa is Trạm Tấu and Mù Cang Chải; the detailed itinerary is in [the Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải 4-day 3-night loop](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai). If you want to add a place in the same province of Sơn La, Mộc Châu is closer; see [from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua).

## Which is farther from Hanoi, and how long does it take?

Ranked by distance from Hanoi: **Tà Xùa is the closest, Sapa is in the middle, Y Tý is the farthest**.

### Tà Xùa: about 215km, by overnight bus

Most travellers take a sleeper cabin bus. The common schedule across operators: leave Hanoi at 9pm to 10pm, reach Bắc Yên around 4am, and arrive in central Tà Xùa around 6am. On the way back, buses leave Tà Xùa at 12pm and reach Hanoi around 7pm. You sleep on the bus and arrive right in the morning window when clouds are at their best.

Sapa is about 100km farther, but the road is mostly expressway and takes around 5 to 6 hours. That is a big advantage for families with young children or older people who aren't used to mountain passes.

Y Tý is the farthest and the final stretch is hard going. A 2-day trip is almost all spent on the road; plan at least 3 days.

## Tà Xùa or Sapa: choose by what you want

No place is "the most beautiful" for everyone. Each one is strong for a different kind of trip.

### For cloud hunting: Tà Xùa is easier

What sets Tà Xùa apart is how close the clouds are to where you sleep. Of the 38 homestays on Tà Xùa Booking, 17 have rooms with a cloud view; for example, [Thác Mây Tà Xùa](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua) has 13 rooms, all with a bathtub and cloud view (weekday listed price 1.300.000đ to 1.600.000đ), and [Phỏng Lài Homestay](/homestay/phong-lai-homstay) has 11 rooms that all come with a bathtub, cloud view, breakfast, air conditioning and a heated mattress, at a weekday listed price of 650.000đ. The full list is in [Tà Xùa homestays with a sea of clouds view](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-view-may-dep).

The viewpoints are close too: Đỉnh Gió is about 2km from the centre and free for cloud hunting; a motorbike taxi from the centre up to Đỉnh Gió is 100.000đ one way for 1 passenger. Sống lưng khủng long is farther (12 to 15km) and harder to reach, with a motorbike taxi costing 300.000đ to 350.000đ one way for 1 passenger. In Sapa, going by common experience, you usually need to go up high, such as to Ô Quy Hồ or take the cable car up Fansipan, to see good clouds.

Clouds are weather, and no place can guarantee them. How to forecast clouds and when to get up are covered in [our Tà Xùa cloud hunting guide](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

![Sea of clouds seen through a gap in the rocks at Đỉnh Gió, Tà Xùa, about 2km from the centre](/uploads/1794461465526-bf73c7253a18.jpg)

### Love rice terraces: consider Y Tý or Sapa

Rice terraces are Y Tý's strong point. Tà Xùa also has one spot worth the trip: the Mâm Xôi hill in Xím Vàng, 15 to 20km from the centre (about 40 minutes by motorbike), with around 320 ha of rice terraces across the commune. The hill is only beautiful from mid-September to mid-October; after the harvest only stubble remains. Details are in [Xím Vàng in the ripe rice season](/kinh-nghiem/xim-vang-mua-lua-chin).

![Mâm Xôi rice terrace hill in Xím Vàng near Tà Xùa in the ripe rice season](/uploads/1794462912490-55dc3ecd6669.jpg)

### Need comfort, travelling with grandparents: Sapa is easier

Sapa has plenty of hotels, restaurants and services, plus an expressway, which suits people who need easy travel. If you still want to bring your parents to Tà Xùa, choose a homestay in the centre labelled "Easy road" with air conditioning and a heated mattress; specific suggestions are in [taking your parents to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cho-bo-me).

### Prefer quiet: Y Tý, or Tà Xùa on weekdays

Y Tý is the quietest of the three. Tà Xùa gets busy at weekends in cloud season, sometimes with a queue for photos at Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree); going on weekdays is more relaxed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa close to Sapa?

Tà Xùa is not close to Sapa. Tà Xùa is in Sơn La and Sapa is in Lào Cai; they are not on the same road axis, and the road between them is long and winds over many passes.

### Which is farther from Hanoi, Tà Xùa or Sapa?

Sapa is farther from Hanoi, about 320km versus about 210 to 215km for Tà Xùa. On the other hand, the road to Sapa is an expressway and takes about 5 to 6 hours.

### Is there a direct bus from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

Tà Xùa Booking has no direct bus route between Sapa and Tà Xùa. People doing a multi-destination loop usually hire a vehicle or drive themselves, and should ask a local transport operator about the route before setting off.

### Tà Xùa or Sapa for cloud hunting?

People who want to hunt clouds close to where they stay usually choose Tà Xùa, because many homestays have rooms with a cloud view and the overnight sleeper cabin bus arrives around 6am. Clouds at both places depend on the weather, and neither can guarantee them.

### Which is more beautiful, Tà Xùa or Sapa?

There is no single answer to whether Tà Xùa or Sapa is more beautiful. Tà Xùa is strong on the sea of clouds and homestays where you can watch clouds from your room; Sapa is strong on comfort; Y Tý is strong on rice terraces, clouds and solitude.

### When should you go to Tà Xùa to see clouds?

The cloud season in Tà Xùa usually runs from October to April, and clouds tend to be thickest just as the sun comes up. A month-by-month table is in [the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

### How many days do you need for Y Tý?

Y Tý is about 370km from Hanoi on difficult roads, so you need at least 3 days. On a 2-day trip most of your time is spent on the bus.
