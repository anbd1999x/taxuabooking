From Sapa to Tà Xùa, the cross-country route is about 287km by car, with no need to loop back through Hanoi; as the crow flies it is only about 130km. By car, take the Nội Bài – Lào Cai expressway toward the former Yên Bái, then National Roads 32 and 37 through Phù Yên: about 6.5 hours of driving. Motorbikes are not allowed on the expressway, so they go via Than Uyên and Mường La, about 305km and 8 hours on the road. There is no direct bus, so travelers who don't drive themselves usually return to Hanoi and take the overnight cabin bus.

**Quick summary:**

- **Cross-country distance:** about 287km by car via the expressway, about 305km by motorbike via Mường La, about 310km on the mountain pass route through Mù Cang Chải and Trạm Tấu.
- **Looping through Hanoi:** about 520 to 560km, but both legs have frequent buses.
- **Direct bus:** none. No bus company on Tà Xùa Booking runs a Sapa to Tà Xùa route.
- **The most important time:** reach Bắc Yên before 15h so you can climb the last 14km of pass before 16h, before the fog rolls in.
- **Split it into 2 days** if you ride the Mù Cang Chải route by motorbike, sleeping one night in Mù Cang Chải or Nghĩa Lộ.
- **Not driving yourself:** take a bus from Sapa to Hanoi, then the overnight cabin bus at 21h to 22h, round-trip ticket 500.000đ to 650.000đ.

## How far is Sapa from Tà Xùa, and how many hours does it take?

The two places are in different provinces: Sapa is in Lào Cai, Tà Xùa is in Sơn La. They sit at opposite ends of the Hoàng Liên Sơn range, and the roads between them wind over many passes. Four ways to go, sorted by distance:

| Way to go | Main route | Distance | Driving time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car via the expressway | National Road 4D, Nội Bài – Lào Cai expressway, National Roads 32 and 37 through Phù Yên, Bắc Yên | about 287km | about 6.5 hours |
| Motorbike cross-country | Ô Quy Hồ, Than Uyên, Mường La, Cò Nòi, National Road 37, Bắc Yên | about 305km | about 8 hours |
| Scenic pass route | Ô Quy Hồ, Than Uyên, Mù Cang Chải, Tú Lệ, Nghĩa Lộ, Trạm Tấu | about 310km | 8.5 to 9 hours, best split over 2 days |
| Back to Hanoi, then cabin bus | Bus from Sapa to Hanoi, overnight cabin bus up to Tà Xùa | about 520 to 560km | about 16 to 17 hours including waiting |

The car distance is the figure both navigation services OSRM and Valhalla measure (286.7km and 287.5km), matching the article [Tà Xùa or Sapa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-hay-sapa-y-ty). We measured the two motorbike routes with Valhalla in motorbike mode; they are longer because they must avoid the expressway. Times are moving times, not counting meals or rest. Northwest mountain roads often have repair sections and landslides after rain, so add 1 to 2 hours as a buffer.

A tip when searching the map: type **"Tà Xùa, Sơn La"**. There is also a village named Tà Xùa on the Trạm Tấu side, and a short search may send you there. The explanation is in the article [Where is Tà Xùa, how far from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).

## Is there a bus from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

There is no direct bus. All five cabin bus companies serving Tà Xùa depart from Hanoi and the lowland provinces, and none of them pick up in Lào Cai or Sapa.

Two ways to go by public transport:

- **Via Hanoi (the most reliable):** a bus or limousine from Sapa to Hanoi during the day, then the cabin bus in the evening. Timing details are in the last section.
- **Via Sơn La city:** there are buses from Lào Cai and Sapa to Sơn La city sold on booking apps. From Sơn La to Bắc Yên there are still about 100km plus 14km of pass, and you must arrange your own transport for that leg. This suits long backpacking trips, not short schedules.

Hiring a private car with a driver from Sapa is also an option for groups of 4 to 7. When asking for a price, state clearly that the destination is central Tà Xùa, Sơn La province, and give the name of your booked homestay.

## Driving from Sapa: where to stop along the way?

The car route in brief: from Sapa down to Lào Cai on National Road 4D, onto the Nội Bài – Lào Cai expressway, leave it toward the former Yên Bái, follow National Roads 32 and 37 to Phù Yên and Bắc Yên, then climb 14km of Provincial Road 112. The character of each stretch from Phù Yên upward is described in the article [the road up to Tà Xùa from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/duong-di-ta-xua-tu-ha-noi).

A suggested stop plan for one day of driving:

- **7h to 8h:** leave Sapa after breakfast and drive down to Lào Cai while the pass is still quiet.
- **Mid-morning:** rest at an expressway service stop and refuel before leaving the expressway.
- **Noon:** eat in Phù Yên, the last big town before the mountains.
- **Before 15h:** pass Bắc Yên, fill the tank and withdraw cash at the ATM.
- **Around 15h30:** arrive in central Tà Xùa, check in, and head to Đỉnh Gió (Wind Peak) in the afternoon if you still have energy.

Parking, homestays with car access and the spots where you must switch to a motorbike taxi are covered in the article [driving to Tà Xùa by car](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai). On Tà Xùa Booking, 23 of 38 homestays carry the "Easy road access" label.

## Which way to ride a motorbike from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

Motorbikes are not allowed on the expressway, so the most sensible route cuts across Lai Châu and Sơn La:

1. **From Sapa over the Ô Quy Hồ pass** down toward Lai Châu, along National Road 4D then National Road 32 to Than Uyên, about 95km.
2. **National Road 279D** from Than Uyên to Mường La (Sơn La), following the hydropower reservoir area.
3. **From Mường La down to Mai Sơn**, joining National Road 6 at Cò Nòi.
4. **National Road 37 from Cò Nòi** across the Tạ Khoa bridge up to Bắc Yên, then 14km of pass up to Tà Xùa.

About 305km in total, roughly 8 hours of riding. It can be done in one day if you leave Sapa at 6h, but it will be very tiring. National Road 279D has had landslides many times after heavy rain, so check road news before your trip, especially in the rainy season.

If you don't bring a bike, rent one right in central Tà Xùa: Honda Wave 180.000đ, Yamaha PG1 280.000đ per day; compare rental shops in the article [renting a motorbike in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/thue-xe-may-ta-xua). Tips on riding mountain passes, refuelling and parking are in our guide to [riding a motorbike from Hanoi to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/phuot-ta-xua-bang-xe-may-tu-ha-noi).

## Should you take the pass route through Mù Cang Chải and Trạm Tấu?

This is the most beautiful route but also the hardest, only suitable for riders used to long days on mountain passes:

| Point | Cumulative from Sapa | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Than Uyên | about 95km | Over the Ô Quy Hồ pass |
| Mù Cang Chải | about 135km | Rice terraces, best in rice season |
| Tú Lệ | about 185km | Over the Khau Phạ pass |
| Nghĩa Lộ | about 230km | A large town, good for an overnight stop |
| Trạm Tấu | about 260km | Refuel, have lunch |
| Central Tà Xùa | about 310km | 45 to 50km of small mountain road from Trạm Tấu |

Distances are estimates from navigation data. Why do we only suggest this route to experienced riders?

- **The Trạm Tấu to Tà Xùa stretch is narrow, steep and full of bends,** with few houses and few petrol stations. Landslides can happen in the rainy season. Ask your homestay about road conditions right before you go.
- **Three big passes in one leg:** Ô Quy Hồ, Khau Phạ and the mountain crossing at Trạm Tấu. Doing it in one day is too much.
- **Don't cross the mountain after 16h.** In the late afternoon the fog comes down fast and visibility drops sharply.

If the Trạm Tấu road is not in good shape, from Nghĩa Lộ go back down to National Road 37 over the Lũng Lô pass to Phù Yên, then up to Bắc Yên. It is longer, but it is a national road. A 4-day plan in the opposite direction is in the article [the Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải loop](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai).

## Not driving: how to go back to Hanoi and take the cabin bus?

This is what many travelers already in Sapa choose, because both legs have daily buses:

1. **Morning or early afternoon:** a bus or limousine from Sapa to Hanoi, about 320km, 5.5 to 6 hours via the expressway. Leave Sapa before around 13h and pick a bus that drops off near Mỹ Đình.
2. **Evening:** board the overnight cabin bus in western Hanoi. Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành depart at 21h00, Xuân Trường at 21h30, Thành Mỹ and Quang Nghĩa at 22h00.
3. **Around 4h:** the bus reaches Bắc Yên, and passengers switch to the company's shuttle to climb the 14km pass.
4. **Around 6h:** arrive in central Tà Xùa, in time for the first cloud hunt.

## Combining Sapa and Tà Xùa: a 3, 4 or 5-day plan?

Visiting Sapa first and ending in Tà Xùa makes the most sense: you get a morning of cloud hunting at the end of the trip, and the ride back to Hanoi is only about 210km. Three plans depending on the number of days:

| Days | How to travel | Where to sleep | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 days 2 nights | Sapa to Hanoi by day, overnight cabin bus up to Tà Xùa | Night 1 on the cabin bus, night 2 in Tà Xùa | Not driving, want 2 mornings of cloud hunting |
| 4 days 3 nights | Car from Sapa to Tà Xùa on the expressway route in 1 day | Sapa, Tà Xùa 2 nights | Groups with a car and experienced drivers |
| 5 days 4 nights | Motorbike or car via Mù Cang Chải and Nghĩa Lộ | Sapa, Mù Cang Chải, Tà Xùa 2 nights | Mountain pass lovers, rice season trips |

The 5-day plan in detail:

1. **Day 1:** Sapa.
2. **Day 2:** Sapa to Mù Cang Chải via Ô Quy Hồ and Than Uyên, sleep in Mù Cang Chải.
3. **Day 3:** over the Khau Phạ pass, Tú Lệ, Nghĩa Lộ, then on to Tà Xùa via Trạm Tấu or Phù Yên, arriving before 16h.
4. **Day 4:** early morning cloud hunting, Dinosaur Spine, Đỉnh Gió (Wind Peak) in the afternoon.
5. **Day 5:** a second cloud hunt, then head down the mountain to Hanoi at noon.

The 3-day plan by cabin bus: on day 1 leave Sapa before 13h and board the bus in Hanoi in the evening; on day 2 reach Tà Xùa at 6h, hunt clouds and sleep at a homestay; on day 3 hunt clouds a second time, take the 12h bus back and reach Hanoi around 19h.

For the first night after the long ride, choose a homestay in the center, with easy road access and heated mattresses, for example [Tà Xùa Hill](/homestay/ta-xua-hill) (all 21 rooms have heated mattresses, 375.000đ to 1.150.000đ) or [Thơ Garden](/homestay/tho-garden-ta-xua) (450.000đ to 750.000đ). A detailed mountain plan is in the article [Tà Xùa itinerary, 3 days 2 nights](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many km is Tà Xùa from Sapa?

Tà Xùa is about 287km from Sapa on the shortest car route; by motorbike, avoiding the expressway, it is about 305 to 310km. Looping through Hanoi, it is about 520 to 560km.

### How long does it take from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

By car via the expressway it takes about 6.5 hours of driving, by motorbike about 8 hours. Taking a bus to Hanoi and then the overnight cabin bus takes about 16 to 17 hours including waiting.

### Is there a direct bus from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

No. All cabin bus companies serving Tà Xùa depart from Hanoi and the lowland provinces. If you are not driving, go back to Hanoi and take the overnight cabin bus at 21h to 22h.

### Should you ride a motorbike from Sapa to Tà Xùa?

Yes, if you are used to mountain passes, ride in daylight and split it into 2 days on the Mù Cang Chải route. Check road news before you go, since National Road 279D and the Trạm Tấu stretch can have landslides in the rainy season.

### Is Tà Xùa close to Sapa?

No. The two places are in different provinces, about 287km apart by road even though it is only about 130km as the crow flies. Tà Xùa is closer to Hanoi than Sapa is, about 210km versus about 320km.

### Should you visit Sapa or Tà Xùa first?

Visit Sapa first and finish in Tà Xùa. That gives you a morning of cloud hunting at the end of the trip and a shorter ride back to Hanoi.
