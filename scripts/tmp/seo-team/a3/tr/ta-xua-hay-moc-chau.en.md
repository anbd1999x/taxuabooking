Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu? Both are in Sơn La province and roughly the same distance from Hanoi: Mộc Châu is about 190km via National Road 6, Tà Xùa about 210km. The difference is the kind of trip: Tà Xùa sits at about 1,500m, is best for cloud hunting, and the last stretch is a 14km mountain pass; Mộc Châu sits at about 1,050m, is best for tea hills and flower seasons, with easier roads and more services.

**Quick summary:**

- **Distance:** Hanoi to Mộc Châu is about 190km, roughly 4.5 hours by car; Hanoi to Tà Xùa is about 210km, 6 to 7 hours self-driving or one night on a cabin sleeper bus.
- **For cloud hunting:** Tà Xùa has the edge. Cloud season usually runs from October to April, and many homestays let you watch clouds right from your room.
- **For flowers and tea hills:** Mộc Châu is the better fit. Plum and peach blossoms bloom from about mid-January to late February, white mustard flowers from late October.
- **Traveling with elderly people or small children:** Mộc Châu is easier thanks to flat terrain and no final pass.
- **Tà Xùa costs in numbers:** round-trip cabin bus tickets 500.000đ to 650.000đ, rooms on Tà Xùa Booking from 200.000đ a night.
- **Combining both:** possible. Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa is about 135km, so allow at least 4 days.

## How are Tà Xùa and Mộc Châu different?

| Criteria | Tà Xùa | Mộc Châu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Province | Sơn La (formerly Bắc Yên) | Sơn La |
| Distance from Hanoi | About 210km | About 190km via National Road 6 |
| Altitude | Center about 1,500m, peak 2,865m | Plateau averaging about 1,050m |
| Route | National Roads 32 and 37, ending with a steep 14km pass | National Road 6, over Thung Khe Pass, flat plateau terrain |
| Main scenery | Sea of clouds, mountain ridges, Hmong villages, ancient tea trees | Tea hills, plum orchards, mustard fields, Dải Yếm waterfall |
| Best season | October to April (clouds) | October to February (mustard, plum and peach flowers) |
| Accommodation | Homestays, bungalows on the mountainside, many cloud-view rooms | Homestays, resorts, glamping |

| Best for | Cloud hunters, high-mountain lovers, night bus travelers | Families, elderly travelers, flower lovers |

One point often overlooked: Mộc Châu is busy all year because it is close to National Road 6, while Tà Xùa gets crowded mainly on weekends in cloud season. Going to Tà Xùa on weekdays is much quieter.

## Which is closer to Hanoi, Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu, and how do you get there?

Mộc Châu is about 20km closer and the road is easier. From Hanoi you follow Thăng Long Boulevard then National Road 6, through Hoà Bình and Thung Khe Pass, about 4.5 hours by car. Coaches and limousines run many trips a day.

Tà Xùa is a bit farther but has its own way to get there: the overnight cabin bus. Five bus companies leave Hanoi between 21h and 22h, reach Bắc Yên around 4h, and get to central Tà Xùa around 6h in the morning. On the way back, buses leave Tà Xùa at 12h and arrive in Hanoi around 19h.

## Which months are best for Tà Xùa, and which for Mộc Châu?

Flower and cloud seasons both depend on the weather, and some years shift by a few weeks. The table below shows the usual pattern:

| Month | Tà Xùa | Mộc Châu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 2 | Peak cloud season, Bauhinia then plum blossoms, cold | Plum and peach blossoms whiten the valleys, cold, foggy |
| 3 to 4 | End of cloud season, gradually warmer | After the flower season, pleasant weather |
| 5 to 6 | Start of rainy season, few visitors, cool | Plum picking season |
| 7 to 8 | Mid rainy season, green hills | Green tea, full waterfalls, roads may be slippery |
| 9 to 10 | Ripe rice in Xím Vàng, clouds start returning | Wild sunflowers from late October, white mustard begins |
| 11 to 12 | Clouds thicken, nights 5 to 10°C | White mustard flowers, wild sunflowers |

In short: **January and February are the only months when both places are at their best**. If you want reliable clouds, pick November to March in Tà Xùa; if you want reliable flowers, time the plum and mustard seasons in Mộc Châu. A 12-month table for Tà Xùa is in our article [the best season to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

## Cloud hunting, tea hills, flower seasons: what is each place best at?

**Tà Xùa is best for clouds and high mountains.** The center sits at about 1,500m, and many homestays are right on the slopes overlooking the valley. Of the 38 homestays currently listed on Tà Xùa Booking, 17 have cloud-view rooms. Nearby viewpoints: Đỉnh Gió (Wind Peak) about 2km from the center, Dinosaur Spine 12 to 15km. A motorbike taxi from the center up to Đỉnh Gió costs 100.000đ one way for 1 passenger. How to predict clouds and when to wake up is in our [Tà Xùa cloud hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

**Mộc Châu is best for plateau scenery and light activities.** The heart-shaped tea hill, Nà Ka plum orchards, Bản Áng pine forest, Dải Yếm waterfall, and mustard flower and strawberry farms. Most places can be reached by car, with little uphill walking.

**Hmong village culture is found in both.** Mộc Châu has Hang Táu village about 20km from the center. In Tà Xùa, the villages around the cloud hunting area are all Hmong; our article on [Háng Đồng village](/kinh-nghiem/ban-hang-dong-ta-xua) describes daily life in a village in the middle of a cloud valley. New Year customs, dress and village etiquette are covered in [Hmong culture in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/van-hoa-nguoi-hmong-ta-xua).

**Food.** Mộc Châu is famous for bê chao (fried veal), fresh milk, milk cakes and salmon. Tà Xùa has black chicken grilled with mắc khén pepper, black chicken hotpot, Hmong mustard greens and ancient Shan Tuyết tea; the list and prices are in our article [what to eat in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/an-gi-o-ta-xua).

## Who suits Tà Xùa, who suits Mộc Châu?

Choose **Tà Xùa** if you:

- Want to open your door and see clouds, and are willing to get up before 6h.
- Love high mountains, ridges and short walks on trails.
- Only have 2 days and want to take the night bus to save time.
- Travel as a couple, with friends or solo.

Choose **Mộc Châu** if you:

- Travel with grandparents, small children or people prone to bad motion sickness.
- Love plum blossoms, mustard flowers, tea hills and farm photos.
- Want plenty of eateries and amusement areas, and to get around by car.

For families who still want to go to Tà Xùa, choose homestays labeled "Easy road" with heated mattresses; suggestions are in our article on [taking your parents to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cho-bo-me). If you are also weighing Sapa, read [Tà Xùa or Sapa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-hay-sapa-y-ty).

## Can you combine Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa in one trip?

Yes, and this is the easiest pair to combine in the Northwest since they are in the same province. From Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa is about 135km, via National Road 6 to Cò Nòi, then National Road 37 across Tạ Khoa bridge up to Bắc Yên, about 3.5 to 4.5 hours of driving. The full loop Hanoi, Mộc Châu, Tà Xùa, Hanoi is about 535km.

Suggested 4-day, 3-night outline:

1. **Day 1:** Hanoi to Mộc Châu in the morning, tea hills in the afternoon, sleep in Mộc Châu.
2. **Day 2:** plum orchards or Dải Yếm waterfall in the morning, head to Tà Xùa at noon, arrive before 16h.
3. **Day 3:** early morning cloud hunting, visit Dinosaur Spine, sunset at Đỉnh Gió in the afternoon.
4. **Day 4:** second round of cloud hunting, come down the mountain at noon and return to Hanoi.

This direction makes sense because it ends in Tà Xùa: you get 2 mornings of cloud hunting and avoid driving the pass after 16h. If you are not driving, return to Hanoi by [cabin bus](/xe-cabin) at 12h. Detailed route notes are in our article [from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is closer to Hanoi, Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu?

Mộc Châu is closer, about 190km via National Road 6, compared with about 210km for Tà Xùa. The road to Mộc Châu is also easier because there is no final pass like the 14km from Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa.

### Should you go to Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu for cloud hunting?

Tà Xùa has the edge because it is higher, with the center at about 1,500m, and many homestays let you watch clouds from your room. Clouds in both places depend on the weather, and neither can guarantee them.

### Which months are good for both Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa?

January and February. At this time Tà Xùa is in peak cloud season, while Mộc Châu enters plum and peach blossom season. In return, both places are cold and crowded around Tết.

### How many km is it from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa?

About 135km, via National Road 6 to Cò Nòi, then National Road 37 across Tạ Khoa bridge up to Bắc Yên and a final 14km pass. Driving time is about 3.5 to 4.5 hours.

### With small children, should you choose Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu?

Mộc Châu is easier thanks to flat terrain and more services. If you choose Tà Xùa, take a double cabin bus and pick a central homestay with heated mattresses and an easy road.

### Is it worth doing both Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa in 3 days?

It is doable but rushed, since the driving legs alone add up to about 535km. Allow at least 4 days to get at least 2 mornings of cloud hunting in Tà Xùa.
