Riding a motorbike from Hanoi to Tà Xùa is a trip of about 210km: 6 to 7 hours of actual riding, close to 8 hours with meal breaks. The familiar route takes National Road 32 through Sơn Tây, Thanh Sơn and Thu Cúc, switches to National Road 37 towards Phù Yên and Bắc Yên, then climbs the final 14km pass to central Tà Xùa. The one rule to keep: **arrive before 4 pm**, because after that, fog often covers the pass.

**Quick summary:**

- **Distance:** about 207 to 210km each way, about 420km round trip, not counting rides around the cloud-hunting spots.
- **Route:** Hanoi, Sơn Tây, Thanh Sơn (about 90km), Thu Cúc, Phù Yên (about 165km), Bắc Yên (about 195km), central Tà Xùa (207 to 210km).
- **Timing:** leave Hanoi at 5:30 to 6 am, have breakfast in Thanh Sơn, lunch and refuel in Phù Yên, pass Bắc Yên around 1 pm.
- **Hardest stretch:** the 14km of Provincial Road 112 from Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa, steep all the way, with many hairpin bends.
- **Don't want to ride that far:** take the overnight cabin bus (round trip 500.000đ to 650.000đ) and rent a bike on arrival: Honda Wave 180.000đ, Yamaha PG1 280.000đ per day.
- **How long to stay:** 2 nights, because riding there and back takes up almost two full days.

## Which route do you take by motorbike to Tà Xùa, and how far is it?

In short: **National Road 32 through Sơn Tây and Thanh Sơn to Thu Cúc, National Road 32B over the Cón pass to Mường Cơi, National Road 37 through Phù Yên to Bắc Yên, then 14km of Provincial Road 112 up to central Tà Xùa.** The total is about 207 to 210km, as measured by two routing services, OSRM and Valhalla. The features of each leg, which passes are long and which stretches are prone to landslides are covered in the article [the road to Tà Xùa from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/duong-di-ta-xua-tu-ha-noi). This article is only about what matters to the person riding a motorbike.

Three things specific to motorbikes:

- **Motorbikes are not allowed on expressways.** To leave Hanoi, take National Road 32 through Nhổn and Phùng to Sơn Tây.
- **Type exactly "Tà Xùa, Sơn La"** into your map app. There is another Tà Xùa village in Trạm Tấu, and a wrong turn there costs 3 to 4 hours. Explained in the article [Where is Tà Xùa and how far is it from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).
- **Starting from eastern Hanoi,** add 15 to 20km of city streets.

You can come from elsewhere too. From Mộc Châu, take National Road 6, then National Road 37 across the Tạ Khoa bridge, about 135km; how to combine the routes is in the article [from Mộc Châu, Sơn La to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua). From Sapa it is much further, about 287km by road, and motorbikes have to avoid the expressway sections. The Sapa leg is covered in our guide [from Sapa to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-sapa-di-ta-xua).

## How many hours does it take to ride to Tà Xùa, and when should you leave?

Actual riding time is about 6 to 7 hours. Add breakfast, lunch and a few photo stops, and you should plan 8 hours from leaving home. Working backwards from the 4 pm mark:

1. **5:30 to 6 am:** leave Hanoi, avoiding rush hour at the western gateway.
2. **Around 8 am:** breakfast in Thanh Sơn, refuel.
3. **11 am to 12 pm:** reach Phù Yên, have lunch, rest 45 minutes to ease your arms.
4. **Around 1 pm:** pass Bắc Yên, fill the tank, start up the pass.
5. **Around 2 pm:** arrive in central Tà Xùa, right at check-in time for most homestays.

The same rule applies on the way back: leave Tà Xùa before 1 pm so you are off the pass while it is still light. The road back to Hanoi on Sunday afternoons is often busy around Sơn Tây, so allow an extra hour.

If you only have 2 days, you will spend most of the time on the bike. A sensible plan is to go on Friday, return on Sunday, stay 2 nights and get 2 mornings of cloud hunting. A detailed day-by-day schedule is in the article [Tà Xùa itinerary for 3 days 2 nights](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).

## Where to refuel and rest along the way?

The road from Hanoi to Bắc Yên passes many towns, with only 30 to 90km between stops. The safe habit is to **fill the tank at every major town**, without waiting for the needle to hit red. Suggested stops for motorbikes:

| Stop | Cumulative from Hanoi | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thanh Sơn | about 90km | Breakfast, refuel, stretch your back after the busy streets |
| Thu Cúc | about 130km | Fill the tank before the Cón pass, check your brakes |
| Phù Yên | about 165km | Lunch, rest 45 minutes, refuel |
| Bắc Yên | about 195km | Fill the tank one last time, withdraw cash at the ATM, add a layer |
| Central Tà Xùa | 207 to 210km | Check in, park the bike at the homestay |

The nearest ATM to Tà Xùa is also in Bắc Yên, so withdraw cash here.

Once in Tà Xùa, the cloud-hunting spots are only 2 to 15km from the centre. A full tank from Bắc Yên is enough for a whole session of riding around: Đỉnh Gió (Wind Peak) is about 2km, Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur's Spine) 12 to 15km. Phone signal and ATMs on the mountain are covered in the article [Does Tà Xùa have ATMs, wifi and phone signal](/kinh-nghiem/song-dien-thoai-atm-wifi-ta-xua).

## How to ride the 14km pass from Bắc Yên to Tà Xùa safely?

The road is paved and wide enough for two cars to pass on most stretches, but the climb barely lets up. Six things to do:

- **Climb in a low gear with steady throttle.** On a semi-automatic, use 2nd gear, and 1st on the steepest parts. Don't let the bike lose power mid-bend before shifting down.
- **Use engine braking on the descent.** Shift down and brake in short pulses, never drag the brakes. Overheated brakes are a common cause of trouble on long descents.
- **Keep to the right and honk before blind bends.** Local buses and trucks know the pass well and sometimes take bends wide.
- **In fog, turn on low beams and slow down.** Visibility can drop to 10 to 15m. Don't use high beams, they only add glare.
- **Don't stop for photos on a bend.** Only stop where the shoulder is wide and you can see both directions.
- **On wet or gravelly surfaces, slow down before the bend,** and never brake hard while leaning.

The hardest climbs are not on the main road but on the branches to the cloud-hunting spots. The final stretches to Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Sống lưng khủng long are dirt and steep concrete. Less confident riders should leave the bike at the homestay and take a [local motorbike taxi](/xe-om): from the centre to Đỉnh Gió 100.000đ, to Sống lưng khủng long 300.000đ to 350.000đ one way for 1 passenger. General risks are in the article [Is it dangerous to travel to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-co-nguy-hiem-khong).

## Semi-automatic, scooter or manual clutch bike?

All three types can make it up to Tà Xùa. The difference lies in the descent and in the rider.

| Bike type | Strength on the way to Tà Xùa | Watch out for | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semi-automatic (Wave, Future…) | Easy to drop to a low gear, good engine braking on descents | With 2 people on steep climbs you need 1st gear | Most first-timers |
| Scooter (Vision, Air Blade…) | Light handling, storage under the seat | No gears to hold back, brakes overheat easily on the 14km descent | Riders used to scooters on mountain roads, riding solo |
| Manual clutch, off-road bike | Powerful engine, high clearance, suits dirt branches | Heavy, needs clutch experience on slopes | Riders with lots of pass experience |

Three things to do before the trip, whatever the bike: replace brake pads if worn, check tyres and chain, change the oil if it is nearly due. A bike carrying 2 people plus heavy backpacks will struggle on the pass, so pack everything into one main backpack.

If you don't have a suitable bike, don't borrow one you don't know. ## What to bring on a motorbike trip to Tà Xùa?

Documents you must carry for the whole journey:

- A driving licence of the right class, ID card, vehicle registration, compulsory insurance.
- A helmet with a visor or separate goggles. Early winter mornings, the wind on the pass is bitterly cold.

Gear for you and the bike:

- **A two-piece raincoat** (separate jacket and trousers), not a poncho, which can easily get caught in the wheel.
- **Gloves, a windproof jacket, thermal layers.** Winter nights in Tà Xùa are often 5 to 10°C; what to wear is in the article [How cold is Tà Xùa in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi).
- **Luggage straps and waterproof bags** for your phone and documents.
- **A power bank and phone mount.** Save offline maps for the Thu Cúc to Bắc Yên stretch.
- **Cash.** Many places on the mountain only take cash, or bank transfer when there is signal.
- **Motion sickness pills and personal medicine** for the passenger. Tips in the article [motion sickness on the pass to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua).

The full list for both seasons is in the article [What to prepare for a trip to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/chuan-bi-gi-khi-di-ta-xua).

## Where to park in Tà Xùa, and which area is most convenient?

Motorbikes are not hard to park like cars. Most homestays have a yard or porch where guests can leave bikes overnight. Still, ask the owner three questions before paying a deposit: is there covered parking, can the bike get right to the door, is there a steep concrete stretch on the way in.

Suggestions by area for self-riders:

- **Centre:** close to the petrol station, restaurants and repair shops. [Thơ Garden](/homestay/tho-garden-ta-xua) (450.000đ to 750.000đ, all 10 rooms have heated mattresses), [Dê Homestay](/homestay/de-homestay-ta-xua) (475.000đ to 875.000đ, rents motorbikes).
- **Đỉnh Gió:** close to the sunset viewpoint, short road up. [Khèn Coffee House](/homestay/khen-coffee-house-ta-xua) has 12 of its 13 rooms with cloud views, from 300.000đ.
- **Budget:** [A Phủ Home's](/homestay/a-phu-homes) in the centre, from 113.000đ.

To compare the areas in more detail, read the article [Which area to stay in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many km is it by motorbike from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

About 207 to 210km to central Tà Xùa via Sơn Tây, Thanh Sơn, Thu Cúc, the Cón pass, Phù Yên and Bắc Yên. The round trip is about 420km, plus riding around the cloud-hunting spots.

### How long does a motorbike trip to Tà Xùa take?

6 to 7 hours of actual riding each way. Including breakfast, lunch and breaks, allow about 8 hours and leave Hanoi at 5:30 to 6 am.

### Can a scooter make it up to Tà Xùa?

Yes, the road to the centre is paved. What needs care is the 14km descent: a scooter has no gears to hold it back, so brake in short pulses to keep the brakes from overheating.

### Is the road to Tà Xùa dangerous by motorbike?

Not if you ride in daylight, descend in a low gear and don't ride the pass after 4 pm. Those who have never ridden a mountain pass should take the cabin bus and hire a local motorbike taxi.

### Where to refuel before going up to Tà Xùa?

Fill the tank in Bắc Yên, the last town before the 14km pass. Along the way, also top up in Thanh Sơn, Thu Cúc and Phù Yên.

### Should you ride a motorbike to Tà Xùa in the rainy season?

Avoid it if you can. In the rainy season the pass is slippery and local landslides can happen. If you still go, check the weather before the trip and don't ride in heavy rain.
