**Trekking Tà Xùa**, as hikers use the term, means climbing Tà Xùa peak at **2,865m**, a summit usually listed among the 10 highest mountains in Vietnam. The route is normally done in **2 days and 1 night, sleeping in a mountain hut**, with a local porter leading the way. It is a completely different thing from the 1 to 1.5km walk at Sống lưng khủng long (the "Dinosaur Spine"), a cloud-hunting spot 12 to 15km from the centre of Tà Xùa commune.

**Quick summary:**

- **Altitude:** Tà Xùa peak is 2,865m. The centre of Tà Xùa commune, where the homestays and eateries are, sits at only about 1,500m.
- **Duration:** groups usually climb in 2 days and 1 night, with one night in a hut; the porter sets the exact schedule.
- **Porter:** treat it as mandatory for first-timers. Tà Xùa Booking does not sell summit tours and has no listed prices for porters or huts, so you need to ask in advance.
- **Easy to confuse:** Sống lưng khủng long and Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) are cloud-hunting spots with a ticket of about 20.000đ, done in one morning, no porter needed.
- **Getting to Tà Xùa:** overnight cabin sleeper bus from Hanoi, return ticket 500.000đ to 650.000đ per passenger depending on the operator.
- **Where to stay before and after the climb:** the 5 homestays in the centre suggested in this article have weekday list prices from 113.000đ to 1.150.000đ.

## How high is Tà Xùa peak and where is it?

Tà Xùa peak is 2,865m high and lies on the mountain range of the same name, straddling Sơn La province and the Trạm Tấu area (formerly Yên Bái, now part of Lào Cai province). Compared with the centre of Tà Xùa commune at about 1,500m, the summit marker is more than 1,300m higher.

There are two places called Tà Xùa:

- **Tà Xùa commune, Sơn La:** the cloud-hunting tourist area, about 210km from Hanoi via Bắc Yên. The homestays, cabin buses and motorbike taxis on Tà Xùa Booking are all on this side.
- **Tà Xùa village on the Trạm Tấu side:** on the other flank of the range, reached by an entirely different road.

The route from Hanoi to the tourist area is covered in [Where is Tà Xùa and how far is it from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km). As for the trailhead of the summit route, our system has no data. According to information commonly shared by trekking groups, the most frequently mentioned trail starts on the Trạm Tấu side; on the Sơn La side some people mention the direction of Xím Vàng, a commune about 15km from the centre of Tà Xùa. Check again with your porter before booking transport. Xím Vàng is also the start of the [Sa Mu trek](/kinh-nghiem/trekking-sa-mu-u-bo-ta-xua), a 2,756m peak that groups usually do in 2 days and 1 night.

## What is on the Tà Xùa summit route: three peaks and the moss forest

This section follows the usual descriptions from groups who have done the climb; Tà Xùa Booking has not surveyed the route itself. Use it to picture the trek, not to navigate.

- **Three peaks:** the route crosses three connected summits, and the 2,865m marker is on the highest one.
- **Tà Xùa moss forest (rừng rêu):** a stretch of damp old forest where trunks and rocks are thickly covered in moss, on the upper part of the route. It is very slippery when wet.
- **Ridges:** some sections are exposed to the wind and look down into the valley. Clouds are weather, and no day is guaranteed to have them.
- **Huts:** groups spend the night in huts on the mountain. Our system has no listed price or capacity for the huts.

Not planning to summit but still looking for the "Tà Xùa moss forest"? [Mống Vàng Retreat](/homestay/mong-vang-retreat) lists a spot called "Rừng rêu" 3km from the house. Our system has no information on the access path, so ask the host when you book.

## How does a 2-day-1-night Tà Xùa trek go?

The outline that groups usually describe:

1. **Day 1:** set off in the morning, climb through hill fields and forest, reach the hut in the afternoon and sleep there.
2. **Day 2:** get up early, pass through the moss forest to the summit marker, then descend the same day.

Departure time, sleeping place, meals and the turnaround point in bad weather are decided by the porter.

If you want to combine the summit climb with cloud hunting in the tourist area, you need at least 1 more night in a homestay. A sample cloud-hunting plan is on the [itinerary and costs](/lich-trinh) page.

## Tà Xùa porters: where to hire one and what to ask?

A porter is a local who guides the way, carries gear and cooks at the hut. Forest trails are easy to get lost on, so going with a porter is the safest option.

Tà Xùa Booking does not sell summit tours and has no listed prices for porters, tours or huts, so this article gives no figures. Two ways to find a guide:

- Ask the host of your homestay to introduce a porter they know.
- Contact a porter team on the side you plan to start from.

Before sending a deposit, ask all 5 questions:

1. Where and at what time do we start, and how do we get there?
2. Is the price per day, per person or per group?
3. Does the price include meals, the hut and bedding?
4. How much gear will the porter carry?
5. If it rains or the fog is thick, how do rescheduling and deposit refunds work?

## What to pack for Tà Xùa peak and which season to go?

The minimum list for a night on the mountain:

- **Hiking shoes with good grip, already broken in.** Moss and damp soil are very slippery.
- **Three layers of clothing:** thermal base layer, a wool or fleece mid layer, a windproof jacket. Add a beanie, gloves and spare socks.
- **A rain suit and waterproof bags** for clothes and phone.
- **Headlamp and power bank.** Cold weather drains batteries fast.
- **Water and snacks;** ask the porter about main meals.
- **Personal medicine, plasters, cash.** The nearest ATM is in Bắc Yên, 14km of mountain pass from the centre of Tà Xùa.

In the centre at 1,500m, winter nights are usually 5 to 10°C, dropping to 2 to 5°C in severe cold spells. The hut and the summit marker are much higher, so they are colder; our system has no separate measurements for the peak. For how to dress warmly see [How cold is Tà Xùa in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi), and for the full packing list see [What to prepare for a trip to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/chuan-bi-gi-khi-di-ta-xua).

The cloud season in Tà Xùa usually runs from October to April, when the weather is drier and better for climbing. Summer brings a lot of rain and slippery forest trails, so do not push it. A 12-month table is in [Which season is best in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

Three safety rules:

- Do not climb in rain, strong wind or thick fog. Turn back when the porter says so.
- Do not leave the group and do not stand at the edge of rocks for photos.
- Tell your family your schedule, because phone signal on forest trails is unreliable; for signal in the centre see [Phone signal, ATMs and wifi in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/song-dien-thoai-atm-wifi-ta-xua).

General risks from steep passes and fog are covered in [Is going to Tà Xùa dangerous](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-co-nguy-hiem-khong).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many metres high is Tà Xùa peak?

Tà Xùa peak is 2,865m high. The centre of Tà Xùa commune, where visitors stay in homestays and hunt clouds, is only about 1,500m.

### How many days does trekking Tà Xùa take?

Groups usually trek Tà Xùa peak in 2 days and 1 night, with one night in a hut on the mountain. The porter sets the exact schedule.

### Do you need a porter to climb Tà Xùa peak?

You should have a local porter, especially the first time. The route goes through forest where it is easy to get lost, and the porter takes care of the hut and meals.

### How much does a Tà Xùa porter cost?

Tà Xùa Booking has no listed prices for porters, tours or huts, so we give no figures. Ask the porter directly or have your homestay host introduce one, and confirm what the price includes before paying a deposit.

### Where is the Tà Xùa moss forest?

The moss forest that hikers talk about is on the upper part of the Tà Xùa summit route and can only be reached by trekking. It is not a check-in spot you can ride a motorbike to, like Mỏm cá heo.

### Is Sống lưng khủng long the same as trekking Tà Xùa peak?

No. Sống lưng khủng long at Chống Tra village is 12 to 15km from the centre, involves a 1 to 1.5km walk, costs about 20.000đ and is done in one morning. Climbing the 2,865m peak is a separate, much longer route.
