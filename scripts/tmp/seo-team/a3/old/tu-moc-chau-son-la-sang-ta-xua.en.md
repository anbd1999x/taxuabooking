Many people only "hear about" Tà Xùa after reaching Mộc Châu or Sơn La city and want to drop by. That's entirely doable — here's how to get there and combine the routes sensibly.

## From Sơn La city to Tà Xùa

- Route: Sơn La city → QL37 towards Phù Yên/Bắc Yên → Bắc Yên town → 14 km pass up to Tà Xùa.
- About 100–110 km, roughly **3 – 3.5 hours** by motorbike/car.
- QL37 has moderate passes and lovely scenery; the last 14 km are the familiar "challenge".

## From Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa

- Ferry-free route: Mộc Châu → QL6 to Cò Nòi → QL37 across Tạ Khoa bridge → Bắc Yên → 14 km pass up to Tà Xùa. About **135 km, 3.5 – 4.5 hours** depending on the road. The QL43 route via the Vạn Yên ferry to Phù Yên is shorter but depends on ferry times.
- This route is quiet with many small roads — travel by day and fill up in Mộc Châu/Phù Yên.
- Not driving yourself? Going back to Hanoi and catching the [overnight cabin bus](/xe-cabin) up to Tà Xùa is the most comfortable option.

## A suggested 4–5 day North-West route

Hanoi → Mộc Châu (tea hills, Dải Yếm waterfall) 1–2 days → over to Tà Xùa for 2 days of cloud hunting → down the pass back to Hanoi. This direction makes sense because you finish in Tà Xùa, still catching the clouds on the last morning before heading down at noon.

## Notes

- Check the weather before crossing the Mộc Châu – Phù Yên route in the rainy season (local landslides can happen).
- Remember to book your [homestay](/homestay) in Tà Xùa ahead — looking for a room on arrival at a weekend can easily leave you "empty-handed".
- For a detailed schedule in Tà Xùa, see the [2D1N itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).
