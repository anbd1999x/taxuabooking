Where to go in Northwest Vietnam depends first on how many days off you have. With a 2-day weekend, Tà Xùa (about 210–215km, by overnight bus) and Mộc Châu (about 180–200km) are the two easiest choices; only with 3 to 4 days should you consider Hà Giang, Cao Bằng or Y Tý. This article puts Tà Xùa next to 7 places it is often compared with, so you can choose quickly by days off, by month and by who you travel with.

**Quick summary:**

- **2 days off, want to hunt clouds:** Tà Xùa. Cabin buses leave Hà Nội at 21h–22h and reach the centre around 6h, with round-trip tickets from 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person.
- **2 days off, travelling with grandparents or young kids:** Mộc Châu or Pù Luông, easier roads with fewer steep passes.
- **3 to 4 days off, love mountain passes:** Hà Giang, where the Đồng Văn – Mèo Vạc – Mã Pì Lèng loop usually takes 3 days 2 nights or 4 days 3 nights.
- **Rice harvest season (mid-September to early October):** Mù Cang Chải, or Xím Vàng next to Tà Xùa.
- **Want to see a big waterfall:** Cao Bằng with Bản Giốc waterfall. Note that Cao Bằng is in the Northeast, not the Northwest.
- **Need plenty of hotels, restaurants and an expressway:** Sapa.

## How many days off, and where to go?

### Only a weekend (2 days)

The best fits are Tà Xùa and Mộc Châu. Tà Xùa's advantage is the overnight bus: you board on Friday evening, sleep in a berth and arrive around 6h on Saturday, just when clouds are usually thickest. On the way back the bus leaves Tà Xùa at 12h and reaches Hà Nội around 19h, so you use no leave days. An hour-by-hour plan is in the article [Tà Xùa itinerary 2 days 1 night](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

Mộc Châu is a little closer and the road is flatter. In return, Mộc Châu does not offer cloud watching from your room the way Tà Xùa does. Pù Luông also fits 2 days if you want to rest in a valley.

![Mộc Châu tea hills with a lone bare tree on the hilltop at dusk, Sơn La](/uploads/1795799019374-4708db17248d.jpg)

### 3 days

Three days open up Sapa, Mù Cang Chải, Cao Bằng or Y Tý. For Tà Xùa, 3 days 2 nights gives you two cloud-hunting mornings, which raises your chances because clouds are weather: some mornings have them, some do not. See the article [Tà Xùa itinerary 3 days 2 nights](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).

### 4 days or more

Only now is it worth doing the full Hà Giang loop, or linking several places into one route. A route often combined with Tà Xùa is Tà Xùa → Trạm Tấu (about 40km) → Mù Cang Chải (about 100km from Trạm Tấu, Nghĩa Lộ). The detailed route is in the article [Tà Xùa – Trạm Tấu – Mù Cang Chải 4D3N](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai). To combine within Sơn La province, Mộc Châu → Tà Xùa is about 90–110km, 3–4 hours.

## Which month suits which place?

Each place has its own peak season. Going in the right season matters more than picking the right place.

| Time | Places to prioritise | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November to March | Tà Xùa, Y Tý | Cloud season, dry weather, cold nights |
| December to February | Mộc Châu, Tà Xùa | Plum and mustard flowers; Tà Xùa has tớ dày blossoms in January, plum blossoms in February |
| May to June | Mù Cang Chải, Pù Luông | Water season on the terraced fields |
| June to August | Tà Xùa, Sapa, Mộc Châu | Escape the heat, cooler than the lowlands |
| Mid-September to early October | Mù Cang Chải, Xím Vàng (Tà Xùa), Y Tý | Ripe rice |
| Late summer, early autumn | Cao Bằng | Bản Giốc waterfall at full flow |
| October to November | Hà Giang | Buckwheat flowers |

Tà Xùa has a month-by-month guide in the article [Tà Xùa: which season is best](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat). The rice season in Xím Vàng, 15–20km from the centre of Tà Xùa, is only beautiful from mid-September to mid-October; details are in the article [Xím Vàng in rice harvest season](/kinh-nghiem/xim-vang-mua-lua-chin).

## Who are you travelling with?

- **Young groups of friends who like early cloud hunts:** Tà Xùa. Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine), Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Đỉnh Gió are close together and reachable by local xe ôm (motorbike taxi).
- **Couples:** Tà Xùa if you want a cloud-view room with a bathtub (14 of the 38 homestays on the system have bathtubs); Sapa if you want town hotels and restaurants.
- **Families with young kids:** Mộc Châu, Pù Luông or Sapa because the roads are easier. For Tà Xùa, choose a homestay in the centre with an easy road; see the article [Tà Xùa with young kids](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cung-gia-dinh-tre-nho).
- **Elderly parents:** avoid the long Hà Giang loop and Y Tý. If you want to take them to Tà Xùa, read the article [taking your parents to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cho-bo-me).
- **Long-distance self-riders on motorbikes:** Hà Giang, or the Tà Xùa – Trạm Tấu – Mù Cang Chải route.

## How is each place different from Tà Xùa?

### Tà Xùa or Sapa, Y Tý?

Sapa is further from Hà Nội (about 320km) but on an expressway, with dense services. Y Tý is the furthest (about 370km), quiet and strong on terraced fields. Both are in Lào Cai, not on the same road axis as Tà Xùa. The full comparison is in the article [Tà Xùa or Sapa, Y Tý](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-hay-sapa-y-ty).

![Fansipan summit (3,143m) and its temple complex above a sea of clouds, Sa Pa, Lào Cai](/uploads/1795799019274-98e6345e8145.jpg)

### Hà Giang or Tà Xùa?

Hà Giang is a trip of mountain passes: Mã Pì Lèng, the Đồng Văn stone plateau, needing 3–4 days with most of the time on the road. Tà Xùa is a trip where you stay in one place and hunt clouds in the morning. See the article [Hà Giang or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ha-giang-hay-ta-xua).

![A narrow road winding along the rocky mountainside at Mã Pì Lèng pass, Hà Giang](/uploads/1795799017303-7e995119497c.jpg)

### Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa?

Cao Bằng is in the Northeast, known for Bản Giốc waterfall and clear green rivers; Tà Xùa is in the Northwest, known for clouds and high mountains. Cao Bằng needs 3 days because the waterfall is 80–90km from the city. Details are in the article [Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/cao-bang-hay-ta-xua).

![The multi-tiered Bản Giốc waterfall pouring into a green pool amid forest, Cao Bằng](/uploads/1795799017523-2ac56285b201.jpg)

### Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa?

Same Sơn La province, both doable in 2 days. Mộc Châu is easy to reach, with many tea hills and flowers; Tà Xùa is higher, cloudier and the road up is steeper. The two can be combined in 3–4 days. Read the articles [Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-hay-moc-chau) and [from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua).

### Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa?

Mù Cang Chải is at its best in the rice season and the water season. Tà Xùa is at its best in the cloud season. The two connect via Trạm Tấu. See the article [Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/mu-cang-chai-hay-ta-xua).

### What about Pù Luông?

Pù Luông is in Thanh Hóa, about 160–170km away, not in the Northwest but often picked for an easy weekend. It suits people who want to rest among rice fields and forest more than hunt clouds.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where to go in Northwest Vietnam for 2 days?

For 2 days, choose Tà Xùa or Mộc Châu. Tà Xùa has overnight cabin buses from Hà Nội arriving around 6h, so you lose no time; Mộc Châu is closer and the road is easier.

### Is Cao Bằng in the Northwest?

Cao Bằng is not in the Northwest but in the Northeast. The two regions are often lumped together when people look for northern mountain trips, but they lie in different directions from Hà Nội.

### Where is cloud hunting easiest in northern Vietnam?

Tà Xùa has many homestays where you can watch clouds right from the room: 17 of the 38 homestays on Tà Xùa Booking have cloud-view rooms. Clouds still depend on the weather, and no place can guarantee them.

### Can Hà Giang or Tà Xùa be done in a weekend?

Tà Xùa can be done in a weekend thanks to the overnight bus. Hà Giang usually needs 3 days 2 nights to 4 days 3 nights because the Đồng Văn – Mèo Vạc – Mã Pì Lèng loop is long.

### Are there direct buses from Tà Xùa to Mù Cang Chải, Hà Giang or Sapa?

There is no direct bus route on the system linking Tà Xùa with these places. You drive yourself, hire a private car, or go back to Hà Nội and continue from there.

### Which place suits elderly parents?

Mộc Châu, Pù Luông or Sapa are easier because there are fewer steep passes. If you choose Tà Xùa, stay at a homestay in the centre with car access to the door.

### How far is Tà Xùa from Hà Nội?

Tà Xùa is about 210–215km from Hà Nội. Its location and ways to get there are in the article [where is Tà Xùa, how far from Hà Nội](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).
