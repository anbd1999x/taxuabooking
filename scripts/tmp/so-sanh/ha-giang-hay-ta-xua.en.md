Should you go to Hà Giang or Tà Xùa? If you only have a 2-day weekend, choose Tà Xùa: it is about 210 to 215km from Hanoi, you ride an overnight cabin bus and by morning you are standing in cloud-hunting country. If you have 3 to 4 days and want to ride passes through jagged limestone, choose Hà Giang: it is about 300km from Hanoi, and the Đồng Văn, Mèo Vạc, Mã Pì Lèng loop alone usually takes 3 days 2 nights to 4 days 3 nights.

**Quick summary:**

- **Different directions:** Hà Giang lies north of Hanoi (now part of Tuyên Quang province after the July 2025 merger), while Tà Xùa lies to the northwest, in Sơn La. The two are not on the same road axis.
- **Time needed:** Tà Xùa fits neatly into 2 days 1 night without using leave days. Hà Giang needs 3 to 4 days to complete the loop.
- **Scenery:** Hà Giang means stone plateaus, the Mã Pì Lèng pass and the Nho Quế river. Tà Xùa means seas of clouds, narrow ridgelines and homestays where you watch clouds from your room.
- **Best season:** Hà Giang is famous for buckwheat flowers from late October to November. Clouds in Tà Xùa usually appear from October to April.
- **Cost in Tà Xùa:** a round-trip cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person; homestays list from 200.000đ per night.
- **Combining both:** not convenient. To visit both, people usually return to Hanoi and make a separate trip.

## How are Hà Giang and Tà Xùa different?

The two are often weighed against each other because both are "northern mountains", both have Hmong communities and both look best in the dry season. But the experience is very different:

- **Hà Giang is a trip on the road.** The memorable part is riding through Quản Bạ, Yên Minh, Đồng Văn and Mèo Vạc. Each day is a new leg, and each night you sleep in a different town.
- **Tà Xùa is a trip in one place.** You stay at one homestay, get up early to watch the clouds, visit a few spots a few km apart during the day and come back.

In other words, Hà Giang suits people who like moving and changing scenery constantly. Tà Xùa suits people who want an easy trip with little time in vehicles, focused on sunrise and the sea of clouds.

On Google Maps, type the full "Tà Xùa, Sơn La", because Trạm Tấu also has a village with the same name. The exact location is in the article [Where is Tà Xùa and how far is it from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).

## Leaving from Hanoi, which is faster?

### Tà Xùa: sleep on the bus, arrive in the morning

Tà Xùa is about 90km closer and its bus schedule matches cloud time. Cabin buses leave Hanoi between 21h and 22h, pass Bắc Yên around 4h and reach central Tà Xùa around 6h. On the way back, the bus picks up in Tà Xùa at 12h and reaches Hanoi around 19h.

### Hà Giang: the journey starts only when you reach the city

From Hanoi to Hà Giang city is about 300km, around 6 to 7 hours by sleeper bus. But Hà Giang city is only the starting point. Famous spots such as the Lũng Cú flag tower, Đồng Văn old quarter and the Mã Pì Lèng pass lie far further up, after many more mountain legs. That is why the loop usually takes 3 days 2 nights to 4 days 3 nights, plus the time to and from Hanoi.

## Stone plateau or sea of clouds: which scenery suits you?

### Hà Giang: long passes and grey limestone

Hà Giang's highlight is its roads. The Mã Pì Lèng pass runs along a cliff, looking down into the Nho Quế river canyon. The Đồng Văn stone plateau stretches out with corn growing between cracks in the rock. You go to look from the road, with a new view at every bend.

### Tà Xùa: clouds flowing right below you

Tà Xùa's strength is the morning sea of clouds. Of the 38 homestays on the system, 17 have rooms with a cloud view and 14 have bathtubs. Some rooms let you watch clouds from bed, such as [Thác Mây Tà Xùa](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua) in the Đỉnh Gió area, with 13 rooms all having a bathtub and cloud view, listed at 1.300.000đ to 1.600.000đ on weekdays.

The viewpoints are close together. [Đỉnh Gió](/kinh-nghiem/dinh-gio-ta-xua) is about 2km from the center, and a xe ôm up there costs 100.000đ one way for 1 guest. The [Dinosaur Spine](/kinh-nghiem/song-lung-khung-long-ta-xua) is farther and steeper, with xe ôm at 300.000đ to 350.000đ one way for 1 guest. Whether clouds appear depends on each day's weather; how to read forecasts and when to get up is in [cloud hunting tips for Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

## Which months for Hà Giang, which for Tà Xùa?

The two places' seasons are slightly offset, so you can choose by the month you are free.

| Month | Hà Giang | Tà Xùa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January to March | Peach and plum blossoms around Tết | Clouds still thick; tớ dày blossoms until mid-January, plum blossoms in February |
| April to May | Water-pouring season in Hoàng Su Phì | End of cloud season, gradually warmer |
| June to August | Rainy season, landslide risk on passes | Rainy season, cool at about 20°C, few visitors |
| September to October | Ripe rice in Hoàng Su Phì | Ripe rice in Xím Vàng from mid-September to mid-October, clouds start to appear |
| November to December | Buckwheat flowers, dry weather | Cloud season, cold nights |

A few things worth knowing:

- **October and November are the hardest months to choose**, because Hà Giang's buckwheat flowers and Tà Xùa's clouds are both in season. If you only have a weekend, Tà Xùa is still easier.
- **The flower season in Tà Xùa** is not as vivid as Hà Giang at Tết, but there are tớ dày and plum blossoms. Timing for each is in [flower seasons in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/mua-hoa-ta-xua).
- **If you want terraced fields** without going far, Tà Xùa has the Mâm Xôi hill in Xím Vàng, 15 to 20km from the center. See [Xím Vàng in rice harvest season](/kinh-nghiem/xim-vang-mua-lua-chin).

A detailed 12-month table for Tà Xùa is in [the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

## Who should choose Hà Giang, who should choose Tà Xùa?

**Choose Tà Xùa if you:**

- Only have 2 days and don't want to take leave.
- Want to watch clouds from your room or the homestay yard.
- Don't ride a motorbike on long mountain passes; in Tà Xùa you can call a [local xe ôm](/xe-om) to each spot.
- Travel with friends or as a couple and want more rest than riding.

**Choose Hà Giang if you:**

- Have 3 to 4 days and enjoy trips on the road.
- Ride a motorbike confidently and are used to long passes and cliffside roads.
- Want to see buckwheat flowers, the Lũng Cú flag tower and the Nho Quế river.

For first-timers in the mountains, Tà Xùa is an easier introduction. The hardest part is the last 15km of mountain road, and on a cabin bus you don't have to drive it yourself. Safety notes are in [Is traveling to Tà Xùa dangerous](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-co-nguy-hiem-khong).

## Can you combine Hà Giang and Tà Xùa in one trip?

In theory yes, but few people do. Hà Giang lies north of Hanoi and Tà Xùa northwest, and the two are not on the same road axis. Tà Xùa Booking has no route linking the two directly, so to combine them you must drive yourself or return to Hanoi and catch an overnight cabin bus to Tà Xùa.

The more sensible way is two separate trips. If you want to pair Tà Xùa with another place on a long trip, the closer direction is Trạm Tấu and Mù Cang Chải (Tà Xùa to Trạm Tấu is about 40km). A detailed comparison is in [Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/mu-cang-chai-hay-ta-xua). A table comparing Tà Xùa with other northern mountain areas is in [Where to go in the Northwest](/kinh-nghiem/tay-bac-nen-di-dau-so-sanh-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hà Giang or Tà Xùa closer to Hanoi?

Tà Xùa is closer to Hanoi, about 210 to 215km versus about 300km to Hà Giang city. In Hà Giang you still have to ride the mountain loop after reaching the city.

### How many days do you need for Hà Giang?

The Đồng Văn, Mèo Vạc, Mã Pì Lèng loop usually takes 3 days 2 nights to 4 days 3 nights. With 2 days, most of the time is spent in vehicles.

### Is 2 days 1 night enough for Tà Xùa?

Yes. The cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 21h to 22h, reaches Tà Xùa around 6h, and returns at 12h the next day. A sample plan is in [Tà Xùa 2 days 1 night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

### In October or November, Hà Giang or Tà Xùa?

In October and November both places are in season: Hà Giang has buckwheat flowers and Tà Xùa starts its cloud season. With 2 days, choose Tà Xùa; with 3 to 4 days and a love of riding, choose Hà Giang.

### Should you go to Hà Giang if you can't ride a motorbike?

Hà Giang is mostly done by motorbike or car over many long passes, so non-riders should go with a driver who knows the roads. In Tà Xùa you can take the cabin bus and then call a local xe ôm to each spot.

### Is there a direct bus from Tà Xùa to Hà Giang?

Tà Xùa Booking has no direct route between Tà Xùa and Hà Giang. The two lie in different directions from Hanoi, so people usually return to Hanoi and make a separate trip.

### Does Tà Xùa have buckwheat flowers like Hà Giang?

Tà Xùa is not known for buckwheat flowers. Its strengths are the sea of clouds, tớ dày and plum blossoms, and the Xím Vàng terraced fields in harvest season.
