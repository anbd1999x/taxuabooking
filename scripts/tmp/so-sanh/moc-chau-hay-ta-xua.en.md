**Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa: which should you visit?** Both are in Sơn La province, but they suit two different kinds of trip. Mộc Châu is about 180 to 200km from Hanoi on the easy National Highway 6, and its strengths are tea hills, plum and mustard flower seasons, and resorts. Tà Xùa is further, about 210 to 215km, with a final 15km mountain pass, but in return you get a sea of clouds right below your homestay at around 1,500m.

**Quick summary:**

- **Distance:** Mộc Châu is closer to Hanoi, about 4 hours by car on Highway 6. Tà Xùa is usually reached by overnight cabin bus: board in the evening and arrive in the centre around 6am.
- **Road:** Mộc Châu sits on a major highway, easy for coaches and family cars. Tà Xùa has a 15km pass up from Bắc Yên, steep with many bends.
- **Main scenery:** Mộc Châu is a plateau of tea hills, flower fields, waterfalls and pine forest. Tà Xùa is clouds, mountain ridges and sunrise viewpoints.
- **Best season:** Mộc Châu is at its best in the mustard and plum flower season, roughly November to February. Tà Xùa's cloud season starts around October and lasts until April of the following year.
- **Where to stay in Tà Xùa:** the system currently lists 38 homestays; 17 have cloud-view rooms and 14 have bathtubs. The lowest listed price starts at 200.000đ per night.
- **Combining both:** possible if you have 4 days or more and drive yourself. The system has no direct coach from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa.

## How are Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa different?

Both are "Sơn La", but the experience in each place is very different.

**Mộc Châu** is a wide plateau with an average elevation of about 1,000m. Its scenery spreads out horizontally: rolling tea hills, plum orchards, white mustard fields, Dải Yếm waterfall, the Bản Áng pine forest. Towns, restaurants and resorts are plentiful. Getting between sights by car is fairly easy.

**Tà Xùa** is a highland commune on the mountain range of the same name. The centre sits at about 1,500m, higher than the surrounding valleys. In cloud season, you look down from the homestay yard and see clouds filling the valleys. Its scenery is vertical: ridges, rocky outcrops, grassy hills up high.

In short: Mộc Châu is for strolling, flower photos and relaxing. Tà Xùa is for getting up early to hunt clouds, walking along ridges and sleeping somewhere with a valley view.

## Is it easier to get to Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa?

Mộc Châu is easier, especially by family car. Highway 6 is a big road with few steep sections, and about 4 hours gets you there. People prone to car sickness or older travellers find it quite comfortable.

To get to Tà Xùa, most people take an overnight sleeper cabin bus. The bus leaves Hanoi around 21:00 or 22:00, passengers sleep on board, pass Bắc Yên at nearly 4am and reach the centre around 6am. The return trip departs at 12:00 noon and gets back to Hanoi around 19:00. There are currently 5 cabin bus operators, with return tickets ranging from 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person; the cheapest is [Khánh Châu bus](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau) at 500.000đ, the most expensive is Quang Nghĩa at 650.000đ. All 5 operators are compared in the article [buses to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/nha-xe-di-ta-xua).

If you plan to drive up to Tà Xùa yourself, read [driving to Tà Xùa by car](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai) first.

## Which is better for cloud hunting?

Choose Tà Xùa. Mộc Châu has morning mist over the tea hills, but a sea of clouds filling the valleys is Tà Xùa's clearest strength.

In Tà Xùa, the clouds usually look best around sunrise. The viewpoints are all close to where you stay:

- **Right from your room:** almost half of the homestays on the system (17/38) have rooms facing the clouds. For example, [Bản Mây Tà Xùa Retreat](/homestay/ban-may-ta-xua-retreat) in the centre has cloud-view rooms with bathtubs, listed from 500.000đ; [Thác Mây Tà Xùa](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua) in Đỉnh Gió is 1.300.000đ to 1.600.000đ.
- **Đỉnh Gió:** about 2km from the centre, a motorbike taxi costs 100.000đ per ride per person. Details in the article [Đỉnh Gió Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/dinh-gio-ta-xua).
- **Dinosaur Spine:** further, 12 to 15km from the centre, a motorbike taxi costs 300.000đ to 350.000đ per ride per person. Directions and the best hours in the article [Dinosaur Spine Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/song-lung-khung-long-ta-xua).

Clouds depend on each day's weather, and nobody can promise them. To guess which days will have clouds, read [Tà Xùa cloud hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

## Which months are best for Mộc Châu, and which for Tà Xùa?

The two places have seasonal calendars that almost complement each other.

| Time | Mộc Châu | Tà Xùa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September to October | Green tea hills, drier weather | Ripe rice at Xím Vàng, clouds start returning |
| November to December | White mustard flowers | Thick clouds, cold nights |
| January to February | Plum and peach blossoms, crowded at Tết | Tớ dày blossoms, plum blossoms, nights 5 to 10°C |
| March to April | Ban flowers | End of cloud season, gradually warmer |
| May to August | Rainy season, fruit season | Rainy season, days 20 to 26°C, few visitors |

If you travel between November and February, both places are in season. Then decide based on whether you want flower photos or clouds. Tà Xùa's 12-month calendar is in the article [best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

## Who suits Mộc Châu, and who suits Tà Xùa?

### Choose Mộc Châu if

- You travel with grandparents, small children, or someone in the group gets badly car sick.
- You only have 1 weekend day and want a day trip or a single night.
- You love photographing flowers and tea hills, and want a resort with a pool and restaurant.

### Choose Tà Xùa if

- Your main goal is cloud hunting, sunrise and sunset in the mountains.
- You want to use Friday night: board in the evening, be in the mountains on Saturday morning, no leave days needed.
- You enjoy walking along ridges and sitting in cafés watching the clouds.

Older parents can still go to Tà Xùa: choose a place in the centre with car access to the door and heated rooms. A list of suggestions is in the article [taking your parents to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cho-bo-me).

## Should you combine Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa in one trip?

You can, if your trip is 4 days or longer and you travel by car or motorbike. There are two ways across to Tà Xùa:

- **Vạn Yên ferry route:** take Highway 43 to Phù Yên, Bắc Yên and then up to Tà Xùa, about 90 to 110km, 3 to 4 hours. It is shorter but depends on ferry times.
- **Tạ Khoa bridge route:** via Cò Nòi and Highway 37, no ferry, about 135km to the centre of Tà Xùa.

The sensible order is Hanoi, 1 to 2 days in Mộc Châu, then 2 days in Tà Xùa, and finally the bus back to Hanoi. Ending in Tà Xùa leaves you one more morning of cloud hunting before heading down.

Without your own vehicle, the simplest option is two separate trips. To link them, go back to Hanoi and catch the overnight cabin bus up to Tà Xùa. Route details are in the articles [from Mộc Châu, Sơn La to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua) and [how to get to Tà Xùa from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/duong-di-ta-xua-tu-ha-noi).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa in the same province?

Yes. Mộc Châu and Tà Xùa are both in Sơn La province. Tà Xùa used to belong to Bắc Yên district, and its centre is at about 1,500m.

### Which is closer to Hanoi, Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa?

Mộc Châu is closer to Hanoi, about 180 to 200km and about 4 hours by car. Tà Xùa is about 210 to 215km away, and visitors usually sleep on the overnight cabin bus and reach the mountain early in the morning.

### How far is it from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa?

From Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa is about 90 to 110km via the Vạn Yên ferry route, taking 3 to 4 hours. The Tạ Khoa bridge route has no ferry and is about 135km. The system has no direct coach between the two places.

### Can you see clouds in Mộc Châu?

Mộc Châu has morning mist over the tea hills in the cold season, but people who want to see a sea of clouds filling the valleys usually choose Tà Xùa. Tà Xùa has 17 homestays on the system with rooms facing the clouds.

### How many days are enough for Tà Xùa?

Tà Xùa suits a 2D1N or 3D2N schedule. Taking the Friday night bus gives you all of Saturday and Sunday morning in the mountains. An hour-by-hour plan is in the article [Tà Xùa 2-day 1-night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

### With small children, should you choose Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa?

Families with small children usually find Mộc Châu gentler because the roads are easy and there are many places to play. If you choose Tà Xùa, stay in the centre and avoid the narrow outcrops at the Dinosaur Spine. How to choose the bus and room for kids is in the article [Tà Xùa with small children](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cung-gia-dinh-tre-nho).
