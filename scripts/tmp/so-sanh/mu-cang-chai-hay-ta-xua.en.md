**Should you go to Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa?** Pick Mù Cang Chải for ripe rice or the water-filled terraces; pick Tà Xùa for winter cloud hunting. Tà Xùa is about 210 to 215km from Hanoi, and the overnight cabin bus gets you there around 6am; Mù Cang Chải is farther, about 280 to 300km, and needs at least 3 days so the whole trip is not spent on the road.

**Quick summary:**

- **Different provinces, different best seasons:** Tà Xùa is in Sơn La and is known for its sea of clouds from roughly November to March. Mù Cang Chải now belongs to Lào Cai province (Yên Bái before July 2025) and is known for its rice terraces in the water season of May to June and the ripe-rice season from mid-September to early October.
- **Road from Hanoi:** Tà Xùa about 210 to 215km; Mù Cang Chải about 280 to 300km, crossing the Khau Phạ pass.
- **Days you need:** Tà Xùa fits neatly into 2 days 1 night by night bus; Mù Cang Chải deserves 3 days or more.
- **Want both:** ride the Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải loop over 4 days by motorbike or private car. Tà Xùa Booking has no bus route linking the two places directly.
- **Bus and room costs in Tà Xùa:** each guest pays 500.000đ to 650.000đ for a return cabin ticket; the cheapest room on the system is listed at 200.000đ a night on weekdays.
- **Tà Xùa has ripe rice too:** Mâm Xôi hill in Xím Vàng, about 19km from the centre, ripens at the same time as Mù Cang Chải.

## How are Mù Cang Chải and Tà Xùa different?

Both get lumped together as "the Northwest", but what visitors come to see is completely different.

**Mù Cang Chải is rice terrace country.** The terraces in the three communes of La Pán Tẩn, Chế Cu Nha and Dế Xu Phình were ranked a special national relic in 2019. The scenery depends on the farming calendar: fields shimmering with water at planting, lush green in summer, bright gold at harvest. Outside the season, the fields are bare stubble and Mù Cang Chải is quiet.

**Tà Xùa is cloud country.** The centre sits at around 1.500m, and many homestays are built on the mountain edge looking down into the valley. On winter mornings, clouds can fill the valley below and spill over the ridges. Tà Xùa has few rice terraces; to see rice you ride over to neighbouring Xím Vàng commune.

In short: choose by **the month you travel** first, then by taste.

## Which months suit Mù Cang Chải and which suit Tà Xùa?

This is the deciding question. In the same month, one place may be in season while the other is "resting".

| Period | Tà Xùa | Mù Cang Chải | Suggestion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November to March | Cloud season, cold, some nights below 10°C | Fields resting after harvest | Choose Tà Xùa |
| April | End of cloud season | Fields being prepared | Tà Xùa if you still want to try your luck with clouds |
| Late May to June | Start of rainy season, few visitors | Water season | Choose Mù Cang Chải |
| July to early September | Rainy season, cool | Green rice, roads prone to landslides after rain | Go when the forecast is dry |
| Mid-September to early October | Ripe rice in Xím Vàng, clouds start returning | Ripe rice, busiest season | Mù Cang Chải, or combine both |
| Mid-October | More clouds | Fields gradually harvested | Lean towards Tà Xùa |

Ripe rice in both places can shift by one to two weeks depending on that year's weather. Clouds are weather, and no day is guaranteed. Tà Xùa's month-by-month calendar of clouds, flowers and rice is in [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat); how to read cloud signs from the previous afternoon is in [Tà Xùa cloud hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

## How do you get there, and how long does it take?

### To Tà Xùa: sleep on the bus, arrive in the morning

Most people take a sleeper cabin bus. The outbound trip leaves Hanoi at 9pm to 10pm and reaches the centre around 6am; the return trip departs at 12 noon and is back in Hanoi around 7pm. You do not lose a day off on the way up, and you step off the bus right in the cloud-watching window.

| Return fare per guest | Bus company |
| --- | --- |
| 500.000đ | [Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau) |
| 550.000đ | Xuân Trường |
| 600.000đ | Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành |
| 650.000đ | Quang Nghĩa |

Compare pickup points and cabin types of each company in [Bus companies to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/nha-xe-di-ta-xua); check seats by date on the [cabin bus](/xe-cabin) page. The last stretch is a 15km pass, so if you get carsick read [tips against carsickness on the Tà Xùa pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua) first.

### To Mù Cang Chải: farther, over the Khau Phạ pass

Mù Cang Chải is about 70 to 90km farther from Hanoi than Tà Xùa. The road runs through Nghĩa Lộ and Tú Lệ, then climbs the Khau Phạ pass, one of the long and famous passes of northern Vietnam. There are sleeper buses from Hanoi, and many people ride a motorbike or drive so they can stop for photos along the way. At this distance, a 2-day trip leaves time to see the terraces for only about one session.

## Should you combine Tà Xùa and Mù Cang Chải in one trip?

Yes, if you have 4 days and your own wheels. The two places connect via Trạm Tấu:

- **Tà Xùa to Trạm Tấu:** about 40km of small, quiet mountain road with narrow cliffside stretches.
- **Trạm Tấu, Nghĩa Lộ to Mù Cang Chải:** about 100km, over the Khau Phạ pass.
- **Whole loop from Hanoi:** about 600km.

The sensible order is Tà Xùa first for a morning of cloud hunting, then Mù Cang Chải for the terraces, returning to Hanoi via Tú Lệ. A 4-day 3-night itinerary, overnight stops and road notes are in [The Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải loop](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai).

Three things to remember on this route:

1. Tà Xùa Booking has no bus route linking Tà Xùa with Trạm Tấu or Mù Cang Chải. You must drive yourself or hire a private vehicle; if you cannot drive, make separate trips, each returning to Hanoi before taking another bus.
2. Ride the Bắc Yên, Trạm Tấu stretch only in daylight and dry weather. After heavy rain, ask your homestay host about road conditions before setting off.
3. Fill up before leaving Tà Xùa; there are few petrol stations along the passes.

For a short trip from Hanoi, Tà Xùa is usually lighter on the wallet because it is closer and you can sleep on the bus the first night, saving a night's room. ## Who suits Mù Cang Chải, who suits Tà Xùa?

**Choose Tà Xùa if:**

- You only have a weekend. Night bus on Friday, on the mountain by Saturday morning, back in Hanoi on Sunday evening.
- You want to watch clouds from your bedroom. On Tà Xùa Booking, 17 properties (out of 38) have rooms looking out over the clouds and 14 have rooms with a bathtub. For example, [Thác Mây Tà Xùa](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua) in the Đỉnh Gió area lists weekday prices of 1.300.000đ to 1.600.000đ; the full list is in [Tà Xùa homestays with sea-of-clouds views](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-view-may-dep).
- You travel between November and March, when Mù Cang Chải's fields are already harvested.
- You do not drive. Once there, [local motorbike taxis](/xe-om) and [taxis](/taxi) take you to the viewpoints.

**Choose Mù Cang Chải if:**

- Your main goal is photos of the terraces in the water season or at harvest.
- You have 3 days or more and can handle the long road over the Khau Phạ pass.
- You ride a motorbike or drive and like stopping for photos along the way.

**Want rice but only have 2 days?** Go to Tà Xùa between mid-September and early October: clouds in the morning, then ride over to Mâm Xôi hill in Xím Vàng in the afternoon. Directions, best hours and bike rental are in [Xím Vàng in the ripe-rice season](/kinh-nghiem/xim-vang-mua-lua-chin); room prices around the 2/9 holiday are in [Tà Xùa in September](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-9). Staying in Làng Chếu, for example at [La Chếu Homestay](/homestay/la-cheu-homestay), puts you closer to Xím Vàng than staying in the centre.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I go to Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa in September?

Mid-September to early October is the ripe-rice season, Mù Cang Chải's most beautiful time of year. If you only have 2 days, going to Tà Xùa and riding over to Xím Vàng for the rice still works, because Xím Vàng ripens at the same time.

### Is Mù Cang Chải or Tà Xùa farther from Hanoi?

Mù Cang Chải is farther, about 280 to 300km. Tà Xùa is about 210 to 215km, with overnight cabin buses arriving around 6am.

### How far is it from Tà Xùa to Mù Cang Chải?

Via Trạm Tấu, the Tà Xùa to Trạm Tấu stretch is about 40km, and Trạm Tấu, Nghĩa Lộ to Mù Cang Chải is about 100km. The mountain roads are small, so ride in daylight when it is dry.

### Is there a bus from Tà Xùa to Mù Cang Chải?

Tà Xùa Booking has no bus route linking Tà Xùa directly with Mù Cang Chải. To combine the two, drive yourself or hire a private vehicle; otherwise, make separate trips from Hanoi.

### In December, should I go to Tà Xùa or Mù Cang Chải?

Go to Tà Xùa in December. It is the middle of Tà Xùa's cloud season, while Mù Cang Chải's fields were harvested in October and are resting.

### How many days do Mù Cang Chải and Tà Xùa need?

Tà Xùa is fine in 2 days 1 night thanks to the night bus. Mù Cang Chải deserves 3 days or more. Combining both via Trạm Tấu takes about 4 days and your own vehicle.
