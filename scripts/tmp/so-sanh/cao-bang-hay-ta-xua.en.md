Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa: choose **Tà Xùa** if you have 2 days and want to hunt clouds right from your homestay, choose **Cao Bằng** if you have 3 days or more and want to see Bản Giốc waterfall, rivers and limestone mountains. Tà Xùa is about 210 to 215km from Hanoi and an overnight bus gets you there; Cao Bằng is about 280 to 300km away, and Bản Giốc waterfall is another 80 to 90km or so from the city.

**Quick summary:**

- **Different regions:** Cao Bằng is in the Northeast, Tà Xùa (Sơn La) is in the Northwest. The two lie on opposite sides of Hanoi and cannot be combined in a short trip.
- **Time needed:** Tà Xùa fits neatly into 2 days 1 night by overnight cabin bus. Cao Bằng needs 3 days 2 nights because the distance is long and the main sights are far apart.
- **Scenery:** Tà Xùa means sea of clouds, mountain ridges and tea hills. Cao Bằng means waterfalls, green rivers and limestone valleys.
- **Best season:** Tà Xùa usually has clouds from October to April. Bản Giốc has the most water in the rainy season and looks best when the rice around it ripens in autumn.
- **Tà Xùa costs:** a round-trip cabin ticket Hanoi – Tà Xùa costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person; the cheapest homestay room on the system is listed at 200.000đ/night.

## How are Cao Bằng and Tà Xùa different?

Many people lump all the northern mountain provinces together as "the Northwest". In fact Cao Bằng is in the **Northeast**, on the northern border, reached via Thái Nguyên. Tà Xùa is in the **Northwest**, in Sơn La province, reached via Phú Thọ and then Bắc Yên.

Different directions bring completely different scenery:

- **Tà Xùa** is high, with many grassy earth ridges and tea forests. What visitors come for is the early-morning sea of clouds, seen from a hilltop or from a room balcony.
- **Cao Bằng** is limestone mountains, rivers and waterfalls. The most famous sight is Bản Giốc waterfall in Trùng Khánh, followed by Ngườm Ngao cave, the Pác Bó relic site and Mã Phục pass on the way up.

If you have been to Tà Xùa and want a change, Cao Bằng offers completely different scenery. If you have never hunted clouds, Tà Xùa is the easier place to start because it is closer and simpler to get around.

### Cao Bằng: a long road, and the main sight is far from the city

Hanoi to Cao Bằng city is about 280 to 300km, taking around 6 to 7 hours. From the city to Bản Giốc waterfall is another 80 to 90km or so. That is why a 2-day trip to Cao Bằng usually only leaves time for the drive and the waterfall. With 3 days you can add Ngườm Ngao cave, Pác Bó or a slow afternoon in the city.

## Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa: choose by purpose

### For cloud hunting: Tà Xùa

In Tà Xùa the clouds sit right below where you sleep. The system currently lists 38 homestays; 17 of them have rooms facing the clouds and 14 have rooms with a bathtub. A few examples: [Khèn Coffee House](/homestay/khen-coffee-house-ta-xua) at Đỉnh Gió is listed from 300.000đ, [Bản Mây Tà Xùa Retreat](/homestay/ban-may-ta-xua-retreat) in the centre from 500.000đ, [Thác Mây Tà Xùa](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua) from 1.300.000đ. See the full list in the article [Tà Xùa homestays with sea-of-clouds views](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-view-may-dep).

From the centre up to Đỉnh Gió is only about 2km, and a motorbike taxi costs 100.000đ per ride per person. Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) is much further, with motorbike taxis at 300.000đ or 350.000đ per ride per person. Clouds depend on the weather and no day is guaranteed; how to read the clouds is covered in [Tà Xùa cloud hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

![A waterfall of clouds spilling through a mountain gap into the valley, seen from Khèn Coffee at Đỉnh Gió, Tà Xùa](/uploads/1794461285231-5449d3ea20d0.jpg)

### For waterfalls and rivers: Cao Bằng

Bản Giốc waterfall is the main reason to go to Cao Bằng. It is wide, with water pouring over several rock tiers, surrounded by rice fields and limestone peaks. Tà Xùa has no large waterfall like that; the "cloud waterfall" in Tà Xùa is clouds spilling through a mountain gap, not water.

![A lit walkway inside Ngườm Ngao cave near Bản Giốc waterfall, Cao Bằng](/uploads/1795799017908-160de4a3c175.jpg)

### A weekend without taking leave: Tà Xùa

Board the bus on Friday night, be in Tà Xùa on Saturday morning, take the bus back at Sunday noon and reach Hanoi on Sunday evening. A 2-day schedule is told in detail in the article [48 hours in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/48-gio-o-ta-xua-nhat-ky). For Cao Bằng, a 2-day weekend is quite rushed.

### Family trips with older people: think carefully about both

Tà Xùa has a final 15km mountain pass up from Bắc Yên; choose a homestay in the centre labelled "Easy road". A list of homes suited to older travellers is in the article [Tà Xùa for parents](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cho-bo-me). Cao Bằng involves longer roads and more hours in the car each day, so break the journey into stages.

## Which season for Cao Bằng, which for Tà Xùa?

The two places are at their best in different seasons, so you can visit both in the same year instead of choosing one.

| Period | Tà Xùa | Cao Bằng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October to April | Cloud season, cold mornings, often clear skies | Waterfall gradually drops, dry weather |
| May to August | Rainy season, cool, fewer clouds | Rainy season, waterfall at its fullest |
| September to October | Ripe rice in Xím Vàng, clouds start returning | Rice around the waterfall ripens, still plenty of water |

Every mountain area carries a landslide risk in the rainy season, so check the forecast before you go. For a month-by-month look at Tà Xùa, read [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat); for a year-end trip, also see [Tà Xùa in December](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-12).

## Should you combine Cao Bằng and Tà Xùa in one trip?

Not with fewer than 6 days. Cao Bằng is northeast of Hanoi and Tà Xùa is northwest; to go from one to the other you almost have to return to Hanoi first. Tà Xùa Booking has no route linking the two directly.

The more sensible option is two separate trips. If you still want to go back to back, rest one night in Hanoi and catch the cabin bus to Tà Xùa the following evening. To add a stop near Tà Xùa, see [Mộc Châu or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-hay-moc-chau) or the [Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai) route. To compare with another Northeast route, see [Hà Giang or Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ha-giang-hay-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Cao Bằng part of the Northwest?

No. Cao Bằng is in the Northeast, on the northern border. Tà Xùa is in Sơn La province, in the Northwest.

### Is Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa further from Hanoi?

Cao Bằng is further. Cao Bằng city is about 280 to 300km from Hanoi, and Bản Giốc waterfall is another 80 to 90km or so. The road from Hanoi to Tà Xùa is only about 210 to 215km.

### For a 2-day trip, which is better, Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa?

Tà Xùa suits 2 days better. The cabin bus runs overnight, arrives around 6am, and returns at noon the next day. On a 2-day Cao Bằng trip most of the time is spent on the road.

### Where is cloud hunting better, Cao Bằng or Tà Xùa?

Cloud hunters usually choose Tà Xùa, since 17 of the 38 homestays on sale have rooms facing the clouds. Clouds tend to appear from October to April, but still depend on the weather each day.

### Which month is best for Bản Giốc waterfall?

Bản Giốc has the most water in the rainy season, around mid-year. In autumn there is still plenty of water and the rice around the falls ripens, so many people choose that time.

### Can I go from Cao Bằng straight on to Tà Xùa?

You can, but it takes a lot of time because the two are on opposite sides of Hanoi. The easiest way is to return to Hanoi and take the overnight cabin bus up to Tà Xùa.

### When should I go to Tà Xùa for clouds and fewer crowds?

In Tà Xùa, clouds usually appear from October through April of the next year. For fewer crowds, go on weekdays rather than weekends and avoid holidays.
