**Your Tà Xùa itinerary and your Tà Xùa budget** are best worked out together: how many days you go, which bus you take and which sights you visit add up to one exact figure. This article breaks down **every expense, hour by hour** for the 4 most popular options from Hà Nội: 2 days 1 night and 3 days 2 nights, by overnight sleeper cabin bus or morning limousine van. All the timings, stops and prices come from the [Tà Xùa itinerary & cost planner](/lich-trinh) by Tà Xùa Booking, updated November 2026.

> **Quick answer:** For a trip to Tà Xùa from Hà Nội, a group of 2 sharing one room and one motorbike and visiting every stop in the itinerary spends about **1.76–1.95 million VND per person for 2 days 1 night** and **2.47–2.71 million VND per person for 3 days 2 nights**. Of that, the round-trip bus is 500.000–700.000đ per person, a homestay room starts from 400.000đ/night (weekday) or 600.000đ/night (weekend) for 2 people, a motorbike is 180.000đ/day, entrance tickets to the check-in spots come to about 210.000–240.000đ, and the rest is food and drink. Skip a few coffees and a couple of ticketed spots and the 2D1N trip comes down to about 1.5 million VND per person.

## Where is Tà Xùa and how long does it take to get there?

Tà Xùa is a highland commune in Sơn La province; the commune centre sits at about 1,500 m, 215–220 km from Hà Nội. The cloud hunting season runs from October to April and is at its best from November to March (see [the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat)).

There are two main ways to get there from Hà Nội, and the one you choose shapes both the itinerary and the cost:

| | Overnight sleeper cabin bus | Morning limousine van |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Departure | Around 21:30 the evening before | Pick-up at 6:30 in Hà Nội |
| Arrival | 5:00 in Bắc Yên, 5:30–6:30 in the centre of Tà Xùa | Around 12:30 in Tà Xùa |
| Round-trip price | 500.000đ (standard), 650.000đ (premium double cabin) | 700.000đ |
| Strengths | Cloud hunting on the very first morning, saves a day of leave, private double cabins available | A full night's sleep at home, arrives in time for sunset, suits families and older travellers |
| Return | Pick-up at 12:00–13:00 in Tà Xùa, back in Hà Nội around 19:00 | Shuttle down to Bắc Yên at 12:00, back in Hà Nội around 19:00 |

Both options switch to a **shuttle** in Bắc Yên town for the final 15 km of mountain pass. Compare each operator in our [comparison of bus operators to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/so-sanh-nha-xe-di-ta-xua), or check prices by date on the [sleeper cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/xe-cabin) page.

## How does the cost change with group size?

- **Travelling solo:** there is no one to split the room and motorbike with. The 2D1N sleeper cabin bus trip comes to about 2.080.000đ on weekdays, roughly 320.000đ more than going as a pair. To cut that, choose a bed in a dorm room or take motorbike taxis leg by leg instead of renting a bike for the whole day.
- **Group of 2:** the figures in the tables above, with 1 room and 1 motorbike.
- **Group of 4:** with 2 rooms and 2 motorbikes the cost per person stays the same. Taking one family room for 4 is usually cheaper than two double rooms.
- **Children and older travellers:** take the morning limousine van and replace the motorbike with a [motorbike taxi](/xe-om) or a [taxi by the day](/taxi), then split the cost across the family.

The bus and the room (marked "included in the combo" in the tables) can be booked together in one go on the [sleeper cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo) page: enter your departure date, return date and number of people, and the system pairs round-trip tickets with available rooms and quotes a price per person.

## 6 ways to cut costs without losing the main experiences

1. **Go on weekdays.** The room alone is 100.000đ per person per night cheaper than at weekends, and there are no queues at the check-in spots. The difference for each period is covered in [homestay prices on weekdays, weekends and holidays](/kinh-nghiem/gia-homestay-ta-xua-ngay-thuong-cuoi-tuan-le).
2. **Pick one ticketed spot per half-day.** Day 1 has 5 ticketed spots totalling 209.000đ. If you keep only Thảo nguyên and Cây cô đơn, you save 105.000đ.
3. **Combine coffee with the view.** The itinerary has three coffee stops on day 1, about 190.000đ. One sunset cafe at Đỉnh Gió is enough.
4. **Have dinner as a group.** Chicken hotpot with lá é is about 200.000đ per person, 50.000đ cheaper than barbecue and warmer on a cold night.
5. **Go cloud hunting where it's free.** Neither Sống lưng khủng long nor the veranda of a cloud-view homestay is among the ticketed items in the itinerary. Choose a room facing the valley and you get a cloud hunting session every morning.
6. **Book the bus and the room together.** The combo price per person is lower than booking each part separately, and you won't end up with bus tickets but no room.

Apply tips 2 and 3 and the 2D1N sleeper cabin bus trip comes down to about **1.5 million VND per person**. On an even tighter budget, see [cheap homestays in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-gia-re).

## Things to note before you go

- A thick warm jacket, a light raincoat and shoes with good grip.
- Bring cash: many cafes and ticket booths don't accept bank transfers yet.
- Follow the cloud forecast 1–2 days ahead so you can move your cloud hunting session to the right morning.
- Don't ride up to Sống lưng khủng long yourself in the rain; hire a local motorbike taxi if you're not used to mountain roads.
- The sleeper cabin bus arrives at 5:30–6:30 but check-in isn't until 14:00: leave your bags at the homestay and head straight out (ideas in [what to do after getting off the night bus in the early morning](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a 2 days 1 night trip to Tà Xùa cost?

About 1.76–1.95 million VND per person from Hà Nội for a group of 2 visiting every stop: round-trip bus 500.000–700.000đ, room 200.000–300.000đ per person, motorbike and fuel 115.000đ, tickets about 170.000–210.000đ, food and drink 600.000–735.000đ. Skip a few coffees and a couple of ticketed spots and it comes down to about 1.5 million VND.

### How much does a 3 days 2 nights trip to Tà Xùa cost?

About 2.47–2.71 million VND per person, including the round-trip bus, 2 nights in a double homestay room, a motorbike for 2.5 days, entrance tickets and 5–7 meals. Weekdays are 200.000đ per person cheaper than weekends because room prices are lower.

### Should I take the overnight sleeper cabin bus or the morning limousine van to Tà Xùa?

The overnight sleeper cabin bus is cheaper (500.000đ versus 700.000đ round trip) and arrives in the early morning, so you get an extra cloud hunting session. The morning limousine van suits people who struggle to sleep on a bus and families with small children or older travellers; it reaches Tà Xùa around 12:30 and still leaves time for sunset.

### How much are entrance tickets to the sights in Tà Xùa?

Thảo nguyên Tà Xùa 79.000đ, Thôn Bụa A 45.000đ, Hồ mắt Ngọc 30.000đ, Amei Hill 30.000đ, Núi đôi thiếu nữ 30.000đ, Cây cô đơn 25.000đ, Mỏm cá heo 5.000đ parking. Tà Xùa Farm and Tiệm trà Kiên Nông Dân don't charge for entry. Visiting all of them costs about 240.000đ per person.

### How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Tà Xùa?

A manual motorbike is about 180.000đ/day, picked up in the commune centre. Including fuel, the 2D1N itinerary comes to about 230.000đ per bike and the 3D2N one about 370.000đ per bike; two people sharing split that in half.

### How much does food and drink cost per day in Tà Xùa?

About 400.000–500.000đ per person for three meals and one or two coffees: breakfast 50.000đ, lunch 100.000–150.000đ, a hotpot or barbecue dinner 200.000–250.000đ, coffee 45.000–90.000đ each time.

### How much more expensive is Tà Xùa at the weekend than on weekdays?

At the room prices used in the itinerary, a room for 2 starts from 600.000đ/night at weekends versus 400.000đ/night on weekdays, so each person pays 100.000đ more per night. Bus fares, entrance tickets and food and drink barely change.

### How much more does it cost to visit Tà Xùa solo than as a pair?

About 320.000đ for a 2 days 1 night trip, because there is no one to split the room (400.000đ) and the motorbike plus fuel (230.000đ) with. The total for a solo 2D1N sleeper cabin bus trip is about 2.08 million VND on weekdays.
