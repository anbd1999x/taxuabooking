**Cabin sleeper buses to Tà Xùa pick up in Hanoi at 4 places: 68 Mạc Thái Tông, 24 Đinh Núp, Mễ Trì (Mỹ Đình) and opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital**, leaving between 21:00 and 22:00 and reaching Tà Xùa at about 6:00 the next morning. Outside Hanoi, 4 of the 5 bus operators on Tà Xùa Booking list fares from Nội Bài Airport and 12 northern provinces. A round-trip ticket costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person from Hanoi and 600.000đ to 1.050.000đ from the provinces.

**Quick summary:**

- **Boarding points in Hanoi:** Khánh Châu at 68 Mạc Thái Tông, Thành Mỹ at 24 Đinh Núp, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa in the Mễ Trì area, Xuân Trường opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital.
- **Departure times:** 21:00 (Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành), 21:30 (Xuân Trường), 22:00 (Thành Mỹ, Quang Nghĩa). All 5 operators list a 6:00 arrival in Tà Xùa.
- **Provincial pickups:** Khánh Châu has fares for 10 provinces, Tiến Thành for 8, Thành Mỹ for 4 provinces plus Nội Bài Airport, Xuân Trường for 3. Quang Nghĩa only lists the Hanoi leg.
- **Round-trip fares from the provinces:** the lowest is Vĩnh Phúc at 600.000đ (Xuân Trường), the highest is Hải Phòng at 1.050.000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành).

## Where does the bus stop along the way?

The outbound routes of the 5 operators differ in the number of stops:

- **Phú Thọ, about 2:00:** Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành take a rest stop. The other three operators do not list this stop.
- **Bắc Yên, about 4:00:** Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa. Xuân Trường lists arrival in Bắc Yên town at 5:15.
- **Tà Xùa, 6:00:** the final point of all 5 routes.

Going by the listed times, the outbound trip takes 8 to 9 hours. From Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa there are about 15km of steep mountain pass left; Quang Nghĩa's route marks Bắc Yên as a "Trung chuyển" (transfer) point. To find out how far the last leg takes you, read [do cabin buses drop you at your homestay in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

## Where does the return bus drop off, and when does it reach Hanoi?

All 5 operators list the same return schedule:

- **12:00:** passengers are collected in Tà Xùa centre.
- **13:00:** the bus leaves Bắc Yên town.
- **About 16:00:** Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành stop at Ba Vì. Xuân Trường and Quang Nghĩa do not list this stop.
- **About 19:00:** arrival in Hanoi.

The operators label the 19:00 mark a "deadline", meaning an expected time, not a guaranteed one.

Passengers from the provinces still have a shuttle leg from Hanoi back home after 19:00. The fare lists do not give arrival times for each province, so ask the operator before booking, especially for Hải Phòng, Quảng Ninh and Nam Định. If you want to get off somewhere other than where you boarded, write it in the drop-off field.

## Tips for choosing a pickup point

- **Compare fares for your own province.** In Bắc Ninh, Khánh Châu is 250.000đ cheaper than Tiến Thành. In Thái Bình, the two differ by only 50.000đ.
- **Consider getting to Hanoi yourself.** The gap between a provincial ticket and a Hanoi ticket is the cost of the shuttle both ways. If you already have business in Hanoi, a Hanoi ticket is cheaper.
- **Ask three questions when your ticket is confirmed:** where the pickup is, what time, and where the return trip drops you.
- **Book your room first, then your ticket.** Give the homestay's name so the operator can plan your drop-off.
- **Arriving at 6:00 before check-in:** see the suggestions in [what to do after getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## How do you book a Hanoi to Tà Xùa cabin bus ticket?

Book on the [cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/xe-cabin) page in 4 steps:

1. Choose your **departure date** and **return date**. The page only lists operators still selling tickets for both dates.
2. Click an operator and choose the number of single or double cabin tickets for each direction.
3. Fill in the **pickup and drop-off points**. Passengers from the provinces should state the province and area.
4. Leave your name and phone number. The website does not take online payment; you get a call back to confirm your seat, the pickup time and how to pay the deposit.

If you need a room too, use the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo) page. In the departure point field, choose your province: the combo's ticket price follows the fare lists above, and operators that do not pick up in that province will not appear. Each guest gets 25.000đ off compared with booking separately. Read more in [cabin bus and homestay combos in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/combo-xe-cabin-homestay-ta-xua).

The steps for holding a seat and paying a deposit are in [booking a bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/dat-xe-di-ta-xua). ## Frequently asked questions

### Which Tà Xùa bus operator departs from 68 Mạc Thái Tông, Hanoi?

68 Mạc Thái Tông is the departure point of the Khánh Châu bus. It leaves at 21:00 every day, and a round-trip ticket costs 500.000đ per person.

### What time do Hanoi to Tà Xùa cabin buses depart?

Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành leave at 21:00, Xuân Trường at 21:30, Thành Mỹ and Quang Nghĩa at 22:00. All 5 operators reach Tà Xùa at about 6:00 in the morning.

### How much is the bus to Tà Xùa from Hải Phòng?

A round-trip ticket from Hải Phòng costs 950.000đ with Khánh Châu and 1.050.000đ with Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành. Thành Mỹ notes that it offers home pickup.

### Where is the pickup from Hải Dương, Bắc Ninh or Bắc Giang to Tà Xùa?

Thành Mỹ picks up in Hải Dương at BigC, the 25-storey building, 559 and Ghẽ; in Bắc Giang at 19:30 at BigC, Đình Trám, Vân Trung and Quang Châu. Other operators agree the pickup spot by phone.

### Are there cabin buses to Tà Xùa from Nam Định, Thái Bình or Ninh Bình?

Yes. Tiến Thành picks up in Nam Định and Ninh Bình for 1.000.000đ and in Thái Bình for 950.000đ. Khánh Châu picks up in Ninh Bình for 850.000đ and in Thái Bình for 900.000đ.

### Is there a pickup at Nội Bài Airport for Tà Xùa?

Yes. Thành Mỹ lists a round-trip fare of 800.000đ for pickup at Nội Bài, with the note "đón từ 2" (pickup from 2).

### Which operator serves Thái Nguyên, Quảng Ninh, Hưng Yên, Hà Nam and Vĩnh Phúc?

Khánh Châu picks up in Thái Nguyên for 700.000đ, Hà Nam for 850.000đ and Quảng Ninh for 950.000đ. Hưng Yên has Khánh Châu at 700.000đ and Tiến Thành at 1.000.000đ. Vĩnh Phúc has Xuân Trường at 600.000đ.
