The Hanoi – Tà Xùa route currently has 5 overnight cabin bus operators on sale on Tà Xùa Booking: **Khánh Châu, Xuân Trường, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa**. A round-trip ticket from Hanoi costs **500.000đ to 650.000đ per person**; buses leave Hanoi between 21:00 and 22:00 and reach Tà Xùa at about 6:00 the next morning. The differences are mainly the pickup point, the departure time, the fares from other provinces and the type of cabin. This article puts the 5 operators side by side so you can choose quickly.

**Quick summary:**

- **[Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau):** 500.000đ, leaves at 21:00 from 68 Mạc Thái Tông, shuttle pickup in 10 provinces.
- **[Xuân Trường](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-xuan-truong):** 550.000đ, leaves at 21:30 from opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital, has a listed fare from Vĩnh Phúc.
- **[Thành Mỹ](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-thanh-my-2):** 600.000đ, leaves at 22:00 from 24 Đinh Núp, picks up at Nội Bài airport.
- **[Tiến Thành](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-tien-thanh):** 600.000đ, leaves at 21:00 from Mễ Trì, shuttle pickup in 8 provinces.
- **[Quang Nghĩa](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2):** 650.000đ, leaves at 22:00 from CC2 Mễ Trì (Mỹ Đình), has both single and double cabins.

## What do all 5 operators have in common?

Before comparing the differences, a few things are nearly the same on every bus, so you do not need to weigh them:

- **All are overnight sleeper cabin buses.** Each passenger gets a private compartment with a curtain, blanket and pillow. The word "limousine" in bus names on this route refers to the premium cabin bus class, not a 9-seat van.
- **The outbound trip takes 8 to 9 hours.** The bus reaches Bắc Yên town at about 4:00 (Xuân Trường lists 5:15), then covers the last 15 km or so of mountain pass up to the centre of Tà Xùa, arriving at 6:00, right on time for cloud hunting.
- **The return runs in the same time slot.** Passengers are gathered in the centre of Tà Xùa at 12:00, the bus leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and reaches Hanoi at about 19:00. Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành also list a rest stop around Ba Vì at about 16:00.
- **They run every day.** The bus does not wait to fill up before leaving; on a day when tickets are sold out, that operator is automatically hidden from the search results.

So what is worth comparing is: **where you board, at what time, for how much, and what the compartment is like**. The table below is sorted by fare, lowest first:

## Khánh Châu: the lowest fare, pickup in the most provinces

- **Round trip from Hanoi:** 500.000đ (250.000đ for a single one-way ticket).
- **Departure:** 21:00, boarding at 68 Mạc Thái Tông.
- **Night route:** rest stop in Phú Thọ at about 2:00, Bắc Yên at 4:00, Tà Xùa at 6:00.
- **Pickup from the provinces (round trip, shuttle included):** Bắc Ninh, Hưng Yên, Thái Nguyên 700.000đ; Bắc Giang, Hải Dương 800.000đ; Hà Nam, Ninh Bình 850.000đ; Thái Bình 900.000đ; Hải Phòng, Quảng Ninh 950.000đ.

![A Khánh Châu cabin bus parked next to the shuttle van in the early morning, with mountains behind](/uploads/1793442934896-a1f6dfa021f8.jpg)

**Pros:** the cheapest on the route at most pickup points. For the same province, Khánh Châu is 50.000đ to 300.000đ lower than Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành, for example Bắc Ninh at 700.000đ against 950.000đ, or Hưng Yên at 700.000đ against 1.000.000đ. Only from Bắc Giang is it level with Xuân Trường, at 800.000đ. Its list of provinces is also the longest, and it is the only operator with a published fare for Thái Nguyên, Quảng Ninh and Hà Nam. The bus sells both single and double cabins.

**Things to note:** the bus leaves at 21:00, so anyone who finishes work late or lives far from Cầu Giấy needs to allow time to reach the pickup point. Because the fare is low, Friday-night trips in cloud season tend to fill up early.

**Best for:** budget travellers, groups of friends, and passengers from the provinces east and south of Hanoi. Separate review: [review of Khánh Châu bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-khanh-chau-ta-xua).

## Xuân Trường: the second-lowest fare, with pickup in Vĩnh Phúc

- **Round trip from Hanoi:** 550.000đ.
- **Departure:** 21:30, boarding opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital.
- **Night route:** Bắc Yên town at 5:15, Tà Xùa at 6:00.
- **Pickup from the provinces (round trip):** Vĩnh Phúc 600.000đ; Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang 800.000đ.

![The aisle between two rows of cabins on a Xuân Trường bus, with blue LED lighting and a screen in every compartment](/uploads/1793520816457-e2b65a297478.jpg)

**Pros:** from Hanoi it is only 50.000đ more than Khánh Châu, and it is the only operator with a published fare for **Vĩnh Phúc**. Passengers from Bắc Ninh pay 800.000đ, 150.000đ less than with Thành Mỹ or Tiến Thành. The 21:30 departure sits between the 21:00 group and the 22:00 group. The operator's photos show two-level compartments on both sides of the aisle, each with a screen.

**Things to note:** Hồng Ngọc Hospital has more than one branch in Hanoi and the route does not say which one, so you must ask when they call to confirm your ticket. The operator has also not published the on-board amenities, whether there are double cabins, or whether it drops passengers at their homestay.

**Best for:** budget travellers already used to night buses, and passengers from Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang. See the [review of Xuân Trường bus](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-xuan-truong-ta-xua).

## Thành Mỹ: a late 22:00 departure, pickup at Nội Bài

- **Round trip from Hanoi:** 600.000đ.
- **Departure:** 22:00, boarding at 24 Đinh Núp (Trung Hoà area, Cầu Giấy).
- **Night route:** Bắc Yên at 4:00, Tà Xùa at 6:00.
- **Pickup elsewhere (round trip):** Nội Bài airport 800.000đ; Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang, Hải Dương 950.000đ; Hải Phòng 1.050.000đ.

![A double cabin on a Thành Mỹ bus: a wide mattress for two, a screen and its own window](/uploads/1794209452709-2e7185a20f0a.jpg)

**Pros:** the only operator that publishes a fare for pickup at **Nội Bài airport**, handy for travellers flying in from central or southern Vietnam and heading straight to the mountains. Pickup points in the provinces are listed in some detail: Bắc Giang at 19:30 around BigC, Đình Trám, Vân Trung and Quang Châu; Hải Dương at BigC and the 25-storey building; Hải Phòng with pickup at home. The photos show compartments with their own entertainment screen; the operator has double cabins, but double tickets are not yet sold online, so add a note when you book.

**Things to note:** fares from the provinces are higher than Khánh Châu's. The published route does not list a rest stop in Phú Thọ like the two operators leaving at 21:00, so if you need a toilet stop in the middle of the night, ask in advance.

**Best for:** travellers flying into Nội Bài, people who finish work late, and passengers from Bắc Giang and Hải Dương who want clearly stated pickup points. See the [review of Thành Mỹ bus](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-thanh-my-ta-xua).

## Tiến Thành: private compartments with entertainment screens

- **Round trip from Hanoi:** 600.000đ.
- **Departure:** 21:00; the route lists the starting point as Mễ Trì.
- **Night route:** rest stop in Phú Thọ at about 2:00, Bắc Yên at 4:00, Tà Xùa at 6:00.
- **Pickup from the provinces (round trip, shuttle included):** Bắc Ninh, Thái Bình 950.000đ; Hải Dương, Bắc Giang, Ninh Bình, Hưng Yên, Nam Định 1.000.000đ; Hải Phòng 1.050.000đ.

![Inside a cabin on a Tiến Thành bus: a private screen, a fully closing curtain, a fleece blanket and space for a bag](/uploads/1793518540434-d0047cc1c5f1.jpg)

**Pros:** this is the operator with the most real photos of the inside of the compartment, so you know fairly well where you will be lying: a leather-covered mattress, a screen at the end of the compartment, a curtain that closes off the aisle side, a tray for small items and a control panel within reach. The compartment is wide enough to keep a bag beside you and still stretch your legs. It is the only operator with a published fare for **Nam Định**.

**Things to note:** the same 600.000đ as Thành Mỹ, but its fares from the provinces are among the highest on the route, for example Hải Dương and Bắc Giang at 1.000.000đ against 950.000đ with Thành Mỹ. The route only says "Mễ Trì" with no street number, so settle the exact waiting spot when your ticket is confirmed.

**Best for:** couples, solo travellers who want privacy, and light sleepers who want an enclosed compartment. See the [review of Tiến Thành bus](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-tien-thanh-ta-xua).

## Quang Nghĩa: many double cabins, drop-off at homestays in the centre

- **Round trip from Hanoi:** 650.000đ.
- **Departure:** 22:00 daily, boarding at CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình (Nam Từ Liêm).
- **Route:** Hanoi – Phù Yên – Bắc Yên – Tà Xùa; Bắc Yên at 4:00, the centre of Tà Xùa at 6:00.
- **Fleet as described by the operator:** a VIP bus with 24 double cabins and a bus with 34 single cabins.

![The aisle between two rows of two-level cabins on a Quang Nghĩa bus, with a white interior and wood trim](/uploads/1794213958838-7b6f4c6137de.jpg)

**Pros:** it has a dedicated bus made up entirely of **double cabins**, good for couples or parents travelling with a small child, while double cabins on other buses are usually few. The operator says passengers are dropped at its office in Tà Xùa or at homestays in the commune centre, which saves dragging a suitcase at 6:00 in the morning. The Mỹ Đình pickup point is convenient for passengers arriving by coach from the provinces at Mỹ Đình bus station and connecting from there.

**Things to note:** the fare from Hanoi is the highest of the five operators. It accepts connecting passengers from Hải Phòng, Quảng Ninh, Bắc Ninh, Thái Nguyên, Nam Định and Thanh Hoá but **has not published a fare for each province**, so you need to state your pickup point clearly to get a quote.

**Best for:** couples who want a double cabin, families with small children, and travellers who want to be dropped close to where they stay. See the [review of Quang Nghĩa bus](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-quang-nghia-ta-xua).

## Which operator should you choose?

- **Price first:** Khánh Châu, 500.000đ round trip from Hanoi; next is Xuân Trường at 550.000đ.
- **Finishing work late, worried about missing the bus:** Thành Mỹ or Quang Nghĩa, both leaving at 22:00. Xuân Trường at 21:30 is the middle option.
- **Living near Cầu Giấy:** Khánh Châu (Mạc Thái Tông) or Thành Mỹ (Đinh Núp).
- **Living near Mễ Trì or Mỹ Đình, or connecting from Mỹ Đình bus station:** Tiến Thành or Quang Nghĩa.
- **Flying into Nội Bài and leaving straight away:** Thành Mỹ.
- **In Thái Nguyên, Quảng Ninh or Hà Nam:** Khánh Châu. **In Nam Định:** Tiến Thành. **In Vĩnh Phúc:** Xuân Trường.
- **Two people who want to lie together:** Quang Nghĩa, or a Khánh Châu double cabin if one is still free.
- **Prone to motion sickness:** every operator crosses the same mountain pass; what matters is choosing a lower-level compartment in the middle of the bus. See also [tips against motion sickness on the Tà Xùa pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua).

If you are not sure which criteria to judge an operator by, read [8 criteria for choosing a sleeper or limousine bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/xe-giuong-nam-limousine-di-ta-xua).

## How do you book a ticket?

Open the [Hanoi – Tà Xùa cabin bus](/xe-cabin) page and choose your outbound and return dates; the system lists the operators that still have seats on exactly those two days, with fares. Click an operator, choose the number of single or double cabin tickets, the pickup and drop-off points, then leave your name and phone number. The website does not collect payment online; you get a call back to confirm your seats and explain how to pay the deposit.

If you also need a place to stay, use the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo): choose your departure point, dates and number of people, and the system pairs round-trip tickets with rooms that are still free and takes 25.000đ off per person compared with booking separately. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which bus operators run the Hanoi – Tà Xùa route?

Tà Xùa Booking currently has 5 cabin bus operators on sale for the Hanoi – Tà Xùa route: Khánh Châu, Xuân Trường, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa. All 5 run overnight every day and include the leg up to the centre of Tà Xùa.

### Which operator to Tà Xùa is the cheapest?

For a round-trip single cabin ticket from Hanoi, Khánh Châu is the cheapest at 500.000đ. Xuân Trường is 550.000đ, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành are both 600.000đ, and Quang Nghĩa is 650.000đ.

### What time and where do buses to Tà Xùa leave?

Khánh Châu leaves at 21:00 from 68 Mạc Thái Tông, Tiến Thành at 21:00 from Mễ Trì. Xuân Trường leaves at 21:30 from opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital. Thành Mỹ leaves at 22:00 from 24 Đinh Núp. Quang Nghĩa leaves at 22:00 from CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình. All arrive in Tà Xùa at about 6:00 the next morning.

### Which operators pick up in the provinces?

Khánh Châu picks up in 10 provinces, Tiến Thành in 8 provinces, including Nam Định. Thành Mỹ picks up at Nội Bài, Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang, Hải Dương and Hải Phòng. Xuân Trường picks up in Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang. Round-trip fares from the provinces run from about 600.000đ to 1.050.000đ.

### What time do the operators run the return trip?

All 5 operators gather passengers in the centre of Tà Xùa at 12:00, leave Bắc Yên at 13:00 and reach Hanoi at about 19:00.

### How far ahead should you book?

On weekdays, booking 1 to 2 days ahead still gets you a seat. For Friday night and Sunday midday in cloud season (November to March), book 5 to 7 days ahead, and for public holidays and Tết, 2 to 3 weeks ahead.
