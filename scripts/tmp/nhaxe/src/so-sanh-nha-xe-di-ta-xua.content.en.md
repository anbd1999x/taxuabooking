**Quick answer:** As of November 2026, the Hanoi – Tà Xùa (Son La) route has 4 overnight cabin bus operators running daily and selling tickets on Tà Xùa Booking: **Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa**. A return ticket from Hanoi costs **500,000đ – 650,000đ per person**. Buses leave Hanoi at 9 – 10 pm and reach central Tà Xùa around 6 am; on the way back they leave Tà Xùa at noon and arrive in Hanoi around 7 pm. **Cheapest:** Khánh Châu. **Daytime buses:** Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành (limousine). **Beds to rest in before check-in:** Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành, Quang Nghĩa. **Drop-off at remote or hillside homestays:** Tiến Thành, Quang Nghĩa, Thành Mỹ (by pickup truck).

Our [review of the 4 cabin bus operators to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/nha-xe-di-ta-xua) covers each operator in turn. This article puts all 4 into **one table** and compares them criterion by criterion: fares, departure times, berths, waiting rooms, door-to-door drop-off and provincial pick-up, so you can choose at a glance.

## 1. Fares: 150,000đ apart from Hanoi, up to 300,000đ from the provinces

## 2. Departure times: working late or travelling by day

- **9:00 pm:** Khánh Châu. Leave early for the pick-up point if you live far from Cầu Giấy or Nam Từ Liêm.
- **9:30 – 10:15 pm:** Tiến Thành (24-cabin bus).
- **10:00 pm:** Thành Mỹ and Quang Nghĩa, the easiest option for office workers who finish late.

Two operators also run **daytime limousines with reclining seats**, good for people who dislike night buses, older travellers, or anyone who wants to see the mountain pass:

- **Thành Mỹ:** limousine departs 6 – 7 am (and 10 pm), returns at 1 pm or 6:30 pm.
- **Tiến Thành:** limousine departs 7 am (and 10:15 pm), returns at 1:30 pm. Seats recline up to 137 degrees and it **drives straight to central Tà Xùa with no transfer**.

Note: travelling by day means arriving in the afternoon, so you miss the first morning of cloud hunting. A night bus arriving at 6 am gets you there right at the best time for the cloud sea.

**On the way back**, all 4 operators follow nearly the same schedule: they collect passengers in central Tà Xùa at noon, leave Bắc Yên town at 1 pm, stop around Ba Vì at about 4 pm and reach Hanoi at about 7 pm. Each direction takes about **8 hours** for roughly 215 – 220 km.

## 3. Berths and on-board amenities

- **Khánh Châu:** single berths and double cabins; leather berths, roller blinds on the windows, blankets and pillows. It is the only operator currently **selling double-cabin tickets directly** on Tà Xùa Booking.
- **Thành Mỹ:** single and double cabin buses **built in 2024**, each cabin with its own screen and window.
- **Tiến Thành:** 24-cabin leather bus with a **massage function**, Wi-Fi, free water and cold towels, charging ports, reading lights, TV and full curtains.
- **Quang Nghĩa:** room-style cabins with curtains, leather mattresses, Wi-Fi and USB charging; the operator says buses are cleaned and disinfected before every trip.

![Inside a Tiến Thành cabin to Tà Xùa: screen, closed curtain, fleece blanket and a free bottle of water](/uploads/1793518540522-845c25d7ec37.jpg)

Every operator takes the same 15 km mountain pass from Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa. If you get carsick, choosing a **lower berth in the middle of the bus** matters more than choosing the operator; see also [tips against motion sickness on the Tà Xùa pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua) and [single or double cabin](/kinh-nghiem/trai-nghiem-xe-cabin-di-ta-xua).

## 4. Arriving in Tà Xùa at 6 am: waiting rooms and homestay drop-off

This is the criterion where operators **differ the most**, and it is often overlooked. The bus arrives at 6 am, while homestays usually check in at 2 pm, which leaves about 8 hours in between.

- **Thành Mỹ:** its Tà Xùa office has a **rest room with beds, free until noon**, a place to brush your teeth, wash, change and leave luggage, and **motorbike rental right at the office**. It drops passengers at central homestays; for homestays up steep slopes it uses a **pickup truck**; luggage is carried to your homestay free of charge.
- **Tiến Thành:** has a **shared rest house with beds** for sleeping until check-in. It takes passengers and luggage **to the homestay gate, even homestays far from the centre**.
- **Quang Nghĩa:** has a **shared rest house with beds** and shared bathrooms. Door-to-door pick-up and drop-off, **even at remote or steep homestays**.
- **Khánh Châu:** has a waiting room but **no beds**. It drops you at the door if the road is easy; for steep homestays it carries your luggage up and you go by motorbike or motorbike taxi; very remote areas such as **Dinosaur Spine are not served door-to-door**.

Every operator recommends **booking your homestay first, then the bus**, and giving the homestay's name and location when booking so they can plan the drop-off. Details for each remote area are in [do cabin buses drop off at homestays](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua); for the 8 hours before check-in, see [what to do after arriving in Tà Xùa at 6 am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## 5. Travelling solo, as a couple, or with a big suitcase

- **Solo:** with Khánh Châu, on quiet weekdays you may get a whole double cabin to yourself **at no extra charge**; at peak times you may share with another passenger; if double cabins are sold out you move to a single berth and **the price stays the same**.
- **Two people wanting to share:** a Khánh Châu double cabin (bookable online) or Thành Mỹ (add a note when booking).
- **Suitcases:** Khánh Châu and Thành Mỹ recommend a **medium suitcase or small bag** because there is a transfer between Bắc Yên and Tà Xùa. Tell them in advance about large suitcases; bulky or excessive luggage may need a separate vehicle at extra cost.
- **Reconfirm:** Khánh Châu asks passengers to reconfirm **1 day before departure**. It is a good habit with all 4 operators.

## How to book a bus to Tà Xùa

Open the [Hanoi – Tà Xùa cabin bus](/xe-cabin) page and choose your outbound and return dates: the system lists only operators **with seats on exactly those two days**, with prices. Click an operator, choose the number of single or double cabins, pick-up and drop-off points, then leave your name and phone number. The website does not take payment online; you will get a call to confirm your seats and explain the deposit.

If you also need a place to stay, use the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo): it pairs a return ticket with an available room, 25,000đ per person cheaper than booking separately. Coming from the provinces? See [cabin bus pick-up points by province](/kinh-nghiem/diem-don-xe-cabin-cac-tinh).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which bus operator to Tà Xùa is the best?

There is no single best operator for everyone. Khánh Châu is the cheapest (500,000đ return from Hanoi) and picks up in the most provinces. Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa are strongest at dropping passengers at remote homestays and have beds for resting before check-in. Thành Mỹ has a free rest room with beds until noon, daytime limousines and pick-up at Noi Bai Airport.

### How much is a bus ticket from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

A return single-cabin ticket from Hanoi costs 500,000đ (Khánh Châu), 600,000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành) and 650,000đ (Quang Nghĩa). Pick-up from northern provinces costs about 700,000đ to 1,050,000đ return, including the transfer.

### Is there a daytime bus to Tà Xùa?

Yes. Thành Mỹ has a limousine leaving Hanoi at 6 – 7 am, and Tiến Thành has a limousine at 7 am that drives straight to central Tà Xùa. Cabin sleeper buses all run overnight, 9 – 10:15 pm.

### Do buses to Tà Xùa drop passengers at their homestay?

Yes, depending on the operator. Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa drop you at the door even at homestays far from the centre. Thành Mỹ drops at central homestays and uses a pickup truck for steep ones. Khánh Châu drops at homestays with easy road access, but not in very remote areas such as Dinosaur Spine. Give your homestay's name when booking.

### Where can I rest after arriving in Tà Xùa at 6 am before check-in?

Thành Mỹ has a rest room with beds, free until noon. Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa have shared rest houses with beds. Khánh Châu has a waiting room without beds. Many homestays also allow early check-in for a surcharge if a room is free.

### How long does it take to get from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

About 8 hours each way for roughly 215 – 220 km. Night buses leave Hanoi at 9 – 10 pm, reach Bắc Yên town around 4 am and central Tà Xùa around 6 am. On the way back they leave Tà Xùa at noon and reach Hanoi around 7 pm.

### Which operator is cheapest from Hải Phòng, Bắc Ninh or Hải Dương?

Khánh Châu is the cheapest from these provinces: Bắc Ninh 700,000đ, Hải Dương 800,000đ, Hải Phòng 950,000đ return, including the transfer. Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành also pick up there for 950,000đ to 1,050,000đ.
