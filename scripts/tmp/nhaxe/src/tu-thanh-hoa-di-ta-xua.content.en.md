**Getting from Thanh Hóa to Tà Xùa takes two legs: first to Hanoi by coach or train (about 150 to 160km, roughly 3 hours), then the overnight sleeper cabin bus from Hanoi to Tà Xùa, with round-trip fares from 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person.** No cabin bus operator currently lists a pickup fare in Thanh Hóa. Leave Thanh Hóa in the early afternoon and you will make the 21:00 to 22:00 departures, reaching Tà Xùa around 6:00 the next morning.

**Quick summary:**

- **No pickup in Thanh Hóa:** the route fare tables of the 5 cabin bus operators on the Tà Xùa route have no Thanh Hóa row; the southernmost pickup point is Ninh Bình.
- **Leg 1, Thanh Hóa to Hanoi:** about 150 to 160km on the North–South expressway, around 3 hours by coach, or about 3 to 4 hours by train from Thanh Hóa station. Check fares for your date on the coach company's site and the railway ticket site.
- **Leg 2, Hanoi to Tà Xùa:** 5 overnight cabin bus operators, departing at 21:00, 21:30 or 22:00 from the Cầu Giấy and Mỹ Đình area, 8 to 9 hours on the road.
- **Round-trip cabin fares from Hanoi:** Khánh Châu 500.000đ, Xuân Trường 550.000đ, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 600.000đ, Quang Nghĩa 650.000đ.
- **Thọ Xuân airport:** no flights to Nội Bài, so flying is not an answer for Thanh Hóa travellers.
- **Return trip:** the cabin bus reaches Hanoi around 19:00 (estimated), so check the evening trains or coaches back to Thanh Hóa in advance.

## Why can't you book a cabin bus pickup in Thanh Hóa yet?

All cabin buses to Tà Xùa start in Hanoi. For travellers from other provinces, some operators send a small vehicle to bring them to Hanoi and list a fare for each province. That list currently only covers the northern delta and midland provinces. Thanh Hóa is not on the list of any of the 4 operators that offer provincial pickups, and Quang Nghĩa only lists the Hanoi leg.

The nearest priced pickup point to Thanh Hóa is Ninh Bình: Khánh Châu 850.000đ and Tiến Thành 1.000.000đ round trip, both marked "Trung chuyển" (shuttle transfer). If you live in Bỉm Sơn, Hà Trung or Nga Sơn, right next to Ninh Bình, you can ask the operator where the Ninh Bình pickup point is and make your own way there. At 850.000đ the fare is 350.000đ above Khánh Châu's Hanoi fare, so it only makes sense if the meeting point is really close to home.

If you live in Thanh Hóa city, Sầm Sơn, Nghi Sơn or Thọ Xuân, the sensible route is still to get to Hanoi yourself. The full list of pickup points and fares is in our article on [where the cabin bus picks up](/kinh-nghiem/diem-don-xe-cabin-cac-tinh).

## Should you take a coach or the train from Thanh Hóa to Hanoi?

| Transport | Departs from in Thanh Hóa | Arrives in Hanoi at | Approximate time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coach, limousine van | North, South or West bus station, or the coach company's office | Giáp Bát, Nước Ngầm or Mỹ Đình, depending on the company | About 3 hours |
| Train | Thanh Hóa station | Hanoi station, Lê Duẩn street | About 3 to 4 hours |

**Coach.** Coaches on the Thanh Hóa to Hanoi route leave all day. They take the North–South expressway through Ninh Bình and Hà Nam, then enter Hanoi on the Pháp Vân – Cầu Giẽ section. When buying your ticket, ask one question: which station does it drop off at? All 5 cabin bus boarding points are in the west of the city, so a coach ending at Mỹ Đình saves you a long taxi ride.

**Train.** Thanh Hóa station sits on the North–South railway, and northbound passenger trains stop here. Train times change with the season, so check the railway's ticket site. Pick a train that reaches Hanoi station before 19:00 so you have time for dinner and the transfer.

## How do you get from a Hanoi bus or train station to the cabin bus?

The five operators have five meeting points, all around Cầu Giấy and Mỹ Đình:

- [Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau): 68 Mạc Thái Tông, departs 21:00.
- [Tiến Thành](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-tien-thanh): Mễ Trì area, departs 21:00.
- [Xuân Trường](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-xuan-truong): opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital, departs 21:30. The hospital has more than one branch in Hanoi, so ask which one.
- [Thành Mỹ](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-thanh-my-2): 24 Đinh Núp, departs 22:00.
- [Quang Nghĩa](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2): CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình, departs 22:00.

From Mỹ Đình bus station these points are only a few km away. From Hanoi station it is about 8 to 10km. Giáp Bát and Nước Ngầm are farther, over 10km, and you have to cross Ring Road 3, which is packed at rush hour. Roughly 17:00 to 19:30 is peak time in Hanoi, so allow at least an extra hour for this city stretch.

## Does Thọ Xuân airport shorten the trip to Tà Xùa?

No. Thọ Xuân airport is about 45km west of Thanh Hóa city, and in late 2026 its main remaining route is to Ho Chi Minh City, with no flights to Nội Bài. The Thanh Hóa to Hanoi distance is far too short to be worth flying.

## What time should you leave Thanh Hóa to catch the cabin bus?

Take the latest safe target as being at the meeting point 20 minutes before departure, then count backwards:

| Milestone | 21:00 bus (Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành) | 22:00 bus (Thành Mỹ, Quang Nghĩa) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At the boarding point | 20:40 | 21:40 |
| Reach a Hanoi bus or train station, allowing 1.5 hours for the city and dinner | Before 19:00 | Before 20:00 |
| Leave Thanh Hóa, allowing 3.5 hours on the road | Before 15:30 | Before 16:30 |

This is our own backwards calculation, not the time of any coach or train. On Friday afternoons and the day before a public holiday, the expressway into Hanoi often jams, so leave an hour earlier than the table.

The rest of the night is the same for everyone: a rest stop in Phú Thọ around 2:00 (Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành), arrival in Bắc Yên town around 4:00, or 5:15 for Xuân Trường, and Tà Xùa around 6:00. The large cabin bus stops in Bắc Yên. The final 15km mountain stretch is covered by the operator's transfer vehicle. Give your homestay name when booking and ask about this leg at the same time. Our article on [cabin buses dropping off at your homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua) explains this part in detail.

## Back in Hanoi at 19:00: can you get home to Thanh Hóa the same night?

On the way back, the bus collects passengers in central Tà Xùa at 12:00, leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and reaches Hanoi around 19:00. That 19:00 is an estimate, and on Sunday evenings it can be later.

You have two options:

- **Head home the same night.** You need a coach or train leaving Hanoi after 20:30. Add 3 hours on the road and you reach Thanh Hóa close to midnight at the earliest. Check the last departure before buying your cabin ticket, and choose a changeable ticket if one is offered.
- **Spend a night in Hanoi.** Go home on an early departure the next morning. This is safer when travelling with small children or older relatives.

## Driving yourself from Thanh Hóa: via Hanoi or via Mộc Châu?

By private car or motorbike, you have two directions.

**Via Hanoi.** Take the expressway to Hanoi, then continue about 200 to 240km up to Bắc Yên. The roads are easy with plenty of rest stops, but the total distance is long.

**Via Mộc Châu.** On the map, you could take National Road 15 from Thanh Hóa up to Mai Châu, join National Road 6 to Mộc Châu and then turn towards Bắc Yên, without detouring through Hanoi. This sounds appealing for people in the western highlands such as Quan Hóa or Bá Thước. We have no verified data on road conditions, the Đà River crossing or real driving times. Check the map again and ask someone who has driven it recently before deciding. We also know of no coach route running directly from Thanh Hóa to Bắc Yên.

Two articles to read before you take the wheel: [driving yourself to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai) for the steep passes and parking, and [from Mộc Châu to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/tu-moc-chau-son-la-sang-ta-xua) for the connecting road.

## Tips for Thanh Hóa travellers

- **Stay 2 nights if you can.** Each direction costs you almost a full day of travel. A [3-day, 2-night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem) gives you two mornings to watch for clouds instead of one, and clouds are never guaranteed on any given day.
- **Book the cabin bus first, the coach second.** Cabin buses have a limited number of cabins, while Thanh Hóa to Hanoi coaches run far more often.
- **Bring warm clothes and cash.** December nights on the mountain drop to only 5 to 10°C. See the [Tà Xùa packing checklist](/kinh-nghiem/chuan-bi-gi-khi-di-ta-xua) and our article on [ATMs, wifi and phone signal](/kinh-nghiem/song-dien-thoai-atm-wifi-ta-xua).
- **Getting around the mountain:** [rent a motorbike](/thue-xe-may) if you are a confident rider, otherwise call a [taxi](/taxi). Reference prices are in our article on [motorbike taxi and taxi fares in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/gia-xe-om-taxi-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### How far is it from Thanh Hóa to Tà Xùa?

Via Hanoi, the total distance is about 350 to 400km: Thanh Hóa to Hanoi is about 150 to 160km, and Hanoi to Tà Xùa about 200 to 240km depending on the route.

### Is there a direct coach from Thanh Hóa to Tà Xùa?

No cabin bus operator on Tà Xùa Booking lists a pickup fare in Thanh Hóa yet. You need to get to Hanoi and board one of the 5 cabin buses leaving between 21:00 and 22:00.

### How long does it take from Thanh Hóa to Tà Xùa?

About 3 hours to Hanoi, a few hours of waiting, then 8 to 9 hours on the night bus. Leave Thanh Hóa around 15:00 and you reach Tà Xùa around 6:00 the next morning.

### How much is the cabin bus from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

A round-trip single cabin ticket for one passenger is 500.000đ (Khánh Châu), 550.000đ (Xuân Trường), 600.000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành) and 650.000đ (Quang Nghĩa).

### Can you fly from Thọ Xuân to Hanoi?

No, Thọ Xuân airport has no route to Nội Bài. Thanh Hóa travellers get to Hanoi by coach, train or private car.

### Do you have to transfer money right away when booking on the website?

No. The website does not take online payment; we call you back to confirm your seat, the time to be at the boarding point and how to pay the deposit.
