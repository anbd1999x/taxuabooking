**Tiến Thành** runs the Hanoi – Phù Yên – Bắc Yên – Tà Xùa (Sơn La) route with two types of vehicle: a **24-cabin sleeper bus with massage seats** and a **VIP limousine van**. A return cabin ticket from Hanoi costs **600.000đ per person**; the night bus leaves between 21:00 and 22:15 and reaches Tà Xùa at around 6 a.m. The two things the bus company stresses most: it **takes passengers and their luggage right to the homestay gate, including places far from the centre**, and it has a **shared rest house (dorm-style) with beds** where you can sleep until check-in when the bus arrives early in the morning.

## What Tiến Thành offers: 24 massage cabins and a reclining-seat limousine

### The 24-cabin sleeper bus

The bus has 24 cabins. According to the bus company's description, the cabins are spacious and come with:

- Leather-upholstered reclining berths with a **massage function**.
- Clean blankets and pillows.
- **Wi-Fi**, free drinking water and cold towels.
- Charging ports for phones and tablets.
- A reading light, a TV and a **full privacy curtain** for each compartment.

The bus company's own photos show a screen mounted at the foot of each compartment, a pleated curtain that closes off the aisle, and soft blue LED lighting so the whole bus can sleep.

![Inside a Tiến Thành cabin: a screen, a fully closing curtain and room to stretch your legs out](/uploads/1793518540522-845c25d7ec37.jpg)

### The VIP limousine van

- Wide seats that **recline to 137 degrees**.
- **Drives straight up to central Tà Xùa, with no shuttle transfer** at Bắc Yên. With a full-size sleeper bus, passengers usually have to switch to a smaller vehicle for the final 15km of mountain pass; the limousine skips that step.
- There is a daytime departure, which suits people who can't sleep on a night bus.

## Tiến Thành timetable

**Hanoi to Tà Xùa:**

- **24-cabin sleeper bus:** the bus company says it departs between **21:30 and 22:15**. The schedule currently on sale on the website gives **21:00 at 68 Mạc Thái Tông**.
- **Limousine van:** **7:00** in the morning or **22:15**.

The two sources differ by 30 minutes to over an hour. The safe approach: be there for 21:00, and confirm the exact time when you get the call confirming your ticket.

**Outbound itinerary according to the published schedule:**

- 21:00: departure (the route sheet says Mễ Trì).
- Around 2:00: rest stop in Phú Thọ.
- Around 4:00: stop in Bắc Yên.
- Around 6:00: arrival in Tà Xùa.

**Tà Xùa back to Hanoi:**

- **Cabin bus:** passengers are collected in central Tà Xùa at 12:00, the bus leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00, makes a rest stop in Ba Vì at around 16:00 and reaches 68 Mạc Thái Tông at around 19:00.
- **Limousine van:** 13:30.

Each leg takes about 8 hours. The outbound trip has two rest stops (Phú Thọ and Bắc Yên), which is less tiring than driving straight through.

## Arriving in Tà Xùa at 6 a.m.: sleeping at the shared rest house

The night bus gets in at around 6 a.m., while homestays usually don't check guests in until 14:00. Tiến Thành has a **shared rest house (dorm-style) with proper beds** where its passengers can catch up on sleep and wait for check-in time.

This is a real difference from bus companies that only have a waiting room with seats. After 8 hours on the bus, two or three more hours lying on a real bed is what leaves you with the energy to head to Đỉnh Gió or Khe Cải in the afternoon. The bus company has not published a closing time for this rest area, so ask when you confirm your ticket. ## How far does Tiến Thành's homestay drop-off really go?

- **Right to the homestay gate:** you don't have to find your own way just after getting off the bus while it's still dark.

**Strengths:**

- **Passengers and luggage dropped at the homestay gate, even far from the centre.**
- **A shared rest house (dorm-style) with beds** to sleep in until check-in.

**Who is Tiến Thành a good fit for?**

## Frequently asked questions about Tiến Thành

### How much is a Tiến Thành ticket to Tà Xùa?

A return cabin ticket from Hanoi is 600.000đ per person. From the provinces with a shuttle transfer: Bắc Ninh and Thái Bình 950.000đ; Hải Dương, Bắc Giang, Hưng Yên, Ninh Bình and Nam Định 1.000.000đ; Hải Phòng 1.050.000đ.

### Where does Tiến Thành pick passengers up in Hanoi?

The schedule on the website gives 68 Mạc Thái Tông as the boarding point, while the bus company's route sheet lists Mễ Trì as the starting point; confirm the pick-up point when your ticket is confirmed. On the way back, the bus drops passengers at 68 Mạc Thái Tông.

### What time does the Tiến Thành bus leave?

The schedule on the website says 21:00; the bus company says the cabin bus leaves between 21:30 and 22:15, and the limousine at 7:00 or 22:15. On the return, the cabin bus collects passengers in Tà Xùa at 12:00 and the limousine leaves at 13:30. It's best to confirm the time when you get the confirmation call.

### Does Tiến Thành drop passengers at their homestay?

Yes. The bus company takes passengers and their luggage right to the homestay gate, including places far from the centre; it depends on the location, so give the name of your homestay in advance for the bus company to confirm.

### Where can I rest if I arrive in Tà Xùa early in the morning?

Tiến Thành has a shared rest house (dorm-style) with beds where its passengers can sleep and wait until homestay check-in time.

### What amenities does the Tiến Thành bus have?

The 24-cabin sleeper bus has leather berths with a massage function, Wi-Fi, free drinking water and cold towels, charging ports, reading lights, a TV and a separate curtain for each compartment.

### Does the Tiến Thành limousine have to change vehicles at Bắc Yên?

No. According to the bus company, the limousine drives straight up to central Tà Xùa with no shuttle transfer.

### Which provinces does Tiến Thành pick up from?

Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang, Hải Dương, Hải Phòng, Hưng Yên, Thái Bình, Ninh Bình and Nam Định, all with a shuttle transfer to Hanoi.
