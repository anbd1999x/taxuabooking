There is no direct bus from Hà Tĩnh to Tà Xùa. **The practical route: take a sleeper bus or the train to Hanoi (about 340km, roughly 6–7 hours by road), board a sleeper cabin bus that evening at 21:00–22:00, and you reach Tà Xùa around 06:00 the next morning.** A return cabin ticket Hanoi – Tà Xùa costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person; the Hà Tĩnh – Hanoi leg is paid separately to whichever operator you choose.

**Quick summary:**

- None of the 5 sleeper cabin bus operators on the Tà Xùa route lists a pickup price in Hà Tĩnh; travellers from Hà Tĩnh make their own way to Hanoi and board there.
- Hà Tĩnh is about 340km from Hanoi; the province has no airport (the nearest is Vinh, in Nghệ An), and its main passenger stations are Yên Trung and Hương Phố.
- Cabin buses leave Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 every night, reach Bắc Yên town around 04:00 and Tà Xùa around 06:00.
- Return fares from Hanoi: Khánh Châu 500.000đ, Xuân Trường 550.000đ, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 600.000đ, Quang Nghĩa 650.000đ.
- On the way back the bus collects passengers in Tà Xùa at 12:00 and is expected in Hanoi around 19:00, so book a late-night or next-morning ride home to Hà Tĩnh.

## How far is Tà Xùa from Hà Tĩnh, and how long does it take?

Tà Xùa commune (former Bắc Yên district, Sơn La) lies northwest of Hanoi, while Hà Tĩnh sits in the North Central region, south of the capital. Every route passes through Hanoi.

Adding up the two legs:

- **Hà Tĩnh – Hanoi:** about 340km via Highway 1A or the eastern North–South expressway; buses take roughly 6–7 hours depending on how often they stop for passengers.
- **Hanoi – Tà Xùa:** about 200–240km depending on the road, 8–9 hours on the night bus.

That comes to about 540–580km and 14–16 hours of riding, not counting the wait in Hanoi. See also [where Tà Xùa is and how far it is from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km).

## Getting to Hanoi: sleeper bus, train or a flight from Vinh?

**Sleeper bus.** Buses leave from Hà Tĩnh bus station and pick up along Highway 1A at places like Hồng Lĩnh and Nghi Xuân. Most North Central buses end at the southern Hanoi stations (Nước Ngầm, Giáp Bát), some at Mỹ Đình. Before you buy, ask the operator one question: which station do you drop off at, and roughly what time.

**Reunification Express train.** The North–South railway does not run through Hà Tĩnh city. The two stations where passenger trains stop are Yên Trung (Đức Thọ area, in the northwest of the province) and Hương Phố (Hương Khê), both well away from the city centre. The train suits you if you live in Đức Thọ, Hương Sơn or Hương Khê. From Hà Tĩnh city, many people go to Vinh station instead because it has more trains. Check timetables and stops on the railway's ticketing site.

**Flying from Vinh.** Hà Tĩnh has no airport. Vinh airport (Nghệ An) is about 60km from Hà Tĩnh city. There are direct flights from Vinh to Nội Bài, but only a few a day; the airport closed for about 6 months for upgrades and reopened at the end of 2025, so check the airline schedule before planning around it. Once you add the trip to the airport and check-in, flying is rarely faster than the sleeper bus.

## How do I get from the bus drop-off in Hanoi to the cabin pickup point?

The cabin pickup points are in western Hanoi, around Cầu Giấy and Mỹ Đình. Xuân Trường picks up opposite the Hồng Ngọc Hospital gate; the hospital has more than one branch, so ask which one. Nước Ngầm and Giáp Bát stations are in the south. The two areas are about 10–15km apart through the city, by taxi or ride-hailing app. The ring roads jam in the late afternoon and evening, so allow about an hour.

If your bus from Hà Tĩnh drops you at Mỹ Đình station, it is simpler: Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa pick up in Mễ Trì, in the same area.

Train passengers arrive at Hanoi station in the centre, about 10km from Mỹ Đình. If you fly from Vinh you land at Nội Bài, where Thành Mỹ offers an airport pickup at 800.000đ return. The price list notes "đón từ 2" (pickup from 2), so if you travel alone, ask about the conditions.

## When should I leave Hà Tĩnh to catch the 21:00 bus?

A sample weekend plan with one night in the mountains. The Hà Tĩnh times are suggestions worked back from the cabin bus departure, not the schedule of any particular bus; match them to the ticket you actually get.

- **Friday morning:** take a morning sleeper bus from Hà Tĩnh. After 6–7 hours you reach Hanoi in the afternoon, with time for dinner and the transfer to the pickup point.
- **Friday, 21:00–22:00:** board the cabin bus. Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành stop for a break in Phú Thọ around 02:00.
- **Saturday, around 04:00:** the bus reaches Bắc Yên town. The large cabin bus stops here and passengers switch to the operator's smaller vehicle for about 15km of mountain pass.
- **Saturday, around 06:00:** you are in Tà Xùa. Homestay check-in is from 14:00; for ideas, see [what to do after arriving on the night bus at 6am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).
- **Sunday, 12:00:** the bus collects passengers in central Tà Xùa, leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and is expected in Hanoi around 19:00.
- **Sunday night:** take a sleeper bus back to Hà Tĩnh, arriving Monday morning.

If you take a night bus from Hà Tĩnh, you reach Hanoi at dawn and wait until evening. For what to do over two days and one night, see [the Tà Xùa 2-day 1-night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

## If I'm back in Hanoi at 19:00, can I still get a bus to Hà Tĩnh?

19:00 is only an estimate; fog on the pass or traffic at the city gates can make the bus later. Travellers from Hà Tĩnh should check three things when booking the trip home:

1. **Night sleeper bus:** ask the operator when the last bus leaves and where it picks up. We suggest one leaving Hanoi after 21:00, at least two hours after the 19:00 mark.
2. **Night train:** early-evening departures from Hanoi station are very hard to make, since you still have to cross from Mỹ Đình to the centre. If you prefer the train, stay a night in Hanoi and take a morning one.
3. **Changeable tickets:** choose fares that allow a change of departure in case the bus from Tà Xùa runs late.

Groups with older people or small children should stay a night in Hanoi. Tips for the winding pass are in [how to avoid motion sickness on the road to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua).

## Tips for travellers from Hà Tĩnh

- **Lock in the cabin ticket first, then the Hà Tĩnh bus.** Each cabin operator has only one departure a night, while buses from Hà Tĩnh to Hanoi run more often.
- **From Kỳ Anh or Cẩm Xuyên, add extra time.** Both lie south of Hà Tĩnh city, so the road to Hanoi is longer than 340km.
- **Bring real warm clothes.** Central Tà Xùa sits at about 1.500m and December nights drop to 5–10°C, much colder than Hà Tĩnh. A packing list is in [what to bring to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/chuan-bi-gi-khi-di-ta-xua).
- **Rent a motorbike locally.** Hire one from [the motorbike rental shops in Tà Xùa](/thue-xe-may) once you arrive; there is no need to bring your own from home.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does transport from Hà Tĩnh to Tà Xùa cost?

A return cabin ticket Hanoi – Tà Xùa is 500.000đ–650.000đ per person depending on the operator. The sleeper bus or train from Hà Tĩnh to Hanoi is extra, and the price changes by date, so check where you buy the ticket.

### Is there a direct bus from Hà Tĩnh to Tà Xùa?

No. All 5 cabin operators on the Tà Xùa route start in Hanoi and none lists a pickup price in Hà Tĩnh. Get to Hanoi first, then board the cabin bus at 21:00–22:00.

### How long does the whole trip from Hà Tĩnh to Tà Xùa take?

About 14–16 hours on the road: 6–7 hours Hà Tĩnh – Hanoi and 8–9 hours Hanoi – Tà Xùa. Leave home in the morning and you arrive around 06:00 the next day.

### Does the cabin bus take you all the way to your homestay in Tà Xùa?

The large cabin bus only goes as far as Bắc Yên town, around 04:00. The operator covers the last 15km of pass in a smaller vehicle, so give your homestay name as soon as you book.

### Do I have to pay online when booking a cabin ticket from Hà Tĩnh?

No. The booking page only records your request; we then call you to confirm availability, pickup time and how to pay the deposit. You can look up your booking code on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page.
