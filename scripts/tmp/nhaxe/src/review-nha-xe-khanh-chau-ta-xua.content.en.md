Khánh Châu is the cabin bus operator with **the lowest fares on the Hanoi – Tà Xùa route** right now: 500,000đ return per person, 250,000đ each way. Buses run overnight daily, leaving 68 Mạc Thái Tông at 9pm and reaching Tà Xùa around 6am. This article goes into detail: the route leg by leg, what the compartments are really like, fares from the provinces, strengths and things to consider.

**Khánh Châu at a glance:**

- **Route:** Hanoi – Tà Xùa (Bắc Yên, Sơn La) and back, daily.
- **Bus type:** cabin sleeper (the bus reads "Phòng Limousine"), with single and double cabins.
- **Fare from Hanoi:** 500,000đ return, 250,000đ one way (single cabin).
- **Outbound:** 9:00pm from 68 Mạc Thái Tông, arriving in Tà Xùa around 6:00am.
- **Return:** 12:00pm from Tà Xùa centre, arriving in Hanoi around 7:00pm.
- **Provincial pick-ups:** 10 provinces, with shuttles, return 700,000đ to 950,000đ.
- **Check availability:** the [Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau) page.

## Outbound route: board at 9pm, see clouds at 6am

According to the operator's published route:

- **9:00pm, 68 Mạc Thái Tông (Cầu Giấy):** passengers board, stow luggage and take their compartments. Arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure.
- **Around 2:00am, Phú Thọ:** rest stop for toilets and drinks.
- **Around 4:00am, Bắc Yên:** another short break before the last 15 km of pass.
- **Around 6:00am, Tà Xùa:** arrive right in the best cloud window of the day.

Total time is about 8 hours, so you sleep almost the whole night on the bus and don't use any days off for travel. One thing to remember when booking your room: board on Friday evening and you arrive on Saturday morning, while homestays usually only allow check-in at 2pm. How to use that time is in the article [getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa at 6am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## Return route: home in time for dinner in Hanoi

- **12:00pm:** depart from Tà Xùa centre, matching most homestays' check-out time.
- **1:00pm:** pass through Bắc Yên.
- **Around 4:00pm:** rest stop at Ba Vì.
- **Around 7:00pm:** arrive in Hanoi, drop-off at 68 Mạc Thái Tông.

The return runs in daylight, so anyone prone to car sickness will notice the descent more than on the way up. Eat lightly before 11:30am rather than a big meal right before departure.

## What's in a Khánh Châu cabin compartment?

From the operator's real photos, each compartment is a private pod with a partition from the next one:

- **A mattress where you can lie with your legs stretched out**, with a thick blanket and pillow. Mountain nights are cold and the bus's air conditioning is too, so the thick blanket is worth a lot.
- **A private window** for each compartment, with a curtain to close it off.
- **An aisle between two rows** of two-deck compartments, with dim blue LED lights so everyone can sleep.

![The bed in a Khánh Châu cabin compartment with a thick blanket and private window](/uploads/1793442945574-3636c341eef1.jpg)

The bus sells two kinds of berth:

- **Single cabin:** one person per compartment. This is the 250,000đ-each-way option.
- **Double cabin:** a wide compartment for two people side by side, 500,000đ per compartment each way, so still 250,000đ per person. There are fewer double compartments than singles, so they sell out first at weekends.

If you're unsure which type or deck to choose, read the article [a night on the cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/trai-nghiem-xe-cabin-di-ta-xua).

## Assessment: strengths and things to consider

**Strengths:**

- **The best fares on the route**, both from Hanoi and the provinces.
- **The widest shuttle network:** 10 provinces, and the only operator with published fares for Thái Nguyên, Quảng Ninh and Hà Nam.
- **A rest stop in Phú Thọ at 2am** as well as Bắc Yên, less tiring than driving straight through.
- **Sells both single and double cabins** on the same trip.
- **The 12pm return matches check-out time**, so there's no waiting around.

**Things to consider:**

- **Departs at 9pm**, an hour earlier than Thành Mỹ and Quang Nghĩa. If you finish work at 7pm and live far from Cầu Giấy, it's quite tight.
- **Provincial passengers must get to the shuttle early**; shuttle times are always a few hours before the cabin bus departs. Ask for the exact time and place when you get the confirmation call.
- **No publicly posted change/cancellation policy yet.** Ask when paying the deposit, especially if you're planning around the cloud forecast.
- **Cloud-season weekend tickets sell out early** because of the low price.

**Who is Khánh Châu for?**

- Budget travellers, students, large groups of friends.
- Passengers in provinces around Hanoi who want a shuttle from their area.
- People in the Cầu Giấy and Mỹ Đình areas, convenient for Mạc Thái Tông.

If you need a later departure or a pick-up from Nội Bài Airport, see the other operators in the comparison article above.

## How to book Khánh Châu tickets

1. Open the [Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau) page and choose your outbound and return dates.
2. Choose the number of single or double cabin tickets for each direction.
3. Choose your pick-up and drop-off points; provincial passengers should state their province and area.
4. Leave your name and phone number and send the request.

The website doesn't take payment online. After sending, you'll be called back to confirm your seats and given deposit instructions; use your booking code to check on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page. To add a room as well, try the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo) — Khánh Châu fares are calculated for your exact departure province and each person gets a further 25,000đ off.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a Khánh Châu ticket to Tà Xùa?

From Hanoi, a single cabin is 500,000đ return per person, 250,000đ one way. From provinces with shuttles, return tickets are 700,000đ to 950,000đ.

### Where does Khánh Châu pick up in Hanoi?

The boarding point is 68 Mạc Thái Tông, Cầu Giấy district. The bus departs around 9pm daily.

### What time does Khánh Châu run and how long to Tà Xùa?

The bus departs at 9pm, rests in Phú Thọ around 2am and Bắc Yên around 4am, arriving in Tà Xùa around 6am. Total time about 8 hours.

### Which provinces does Khánh Châu pick up from?

Bắc Ninh, Hưng Yên, Thái Nguyên, Bắc Giang, Hải Dương, Hà Nam, Ninh Bình, Thái Bình, Hải Phòng and Quảng Ninh. Return fares include the shuttle to Hanoi.

### Does Khánh Châu have double cabins?

Yes. The bus sells both single and double cabins. Double cabins are priced per compartment for two people and are fewer than singles, so book early for weekends.

### What time does the Khánh Châu return bus run?

The bus leaves Tà Xùa centre at 12pm, passes Bắc Yên at 1pm, rests at Ba Vì around 4pm and reaches Hanoi around 7pm.
