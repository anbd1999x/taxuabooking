**Quick answer:** As of November 2026, **4 sleeper cabin bus operators run daily on the Hanoi – Tà Xùa (Sơn La) route** and are on sale on Tà Xùa Booking: **Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa**. A round trip from Hanoi costs **500.000đ – 650.000đ per person**. Buses leave Hanoi at 21:00 – 22:00 and reach the centre of Tà Xùa at around 6:00, just in time for cloud hunting; on the way back they leave Tà Xùa at 12:00 and reach Hanoi at around 19:00. The cheapest is Khánh Châu (500.000đ); if you would rather travel by day, take the limousine run by Thành Mỹ or Tiến Thành.

All 4 operators run **overnight sleeper cabin buses**, where each passenger gets a private compartment with a curtain, blanket and pillow. The journey is about 215 – 220 km and takes roughly **8 hours each way**: the bus reaches Bắc Yên town at around 4:00, then covers the last 15 km of mountain pass up to the centre of Tà Xùa.

## Choosing an operator by what you need

- **You want the cheapest:** Khánh Châu.
- **You finish work late:** Thành Mỹ or Quang Nghĩa, both leaving at 22:00.
- **You don't want an overnight bus:** the seated limousine from Thành Mỹ (6:00 – 7:00) or Tiến Thành (7:00). Travelling by day means arriving in the afternoon and missing the first morning of cloud hunting.
- **Your homestay is far from the centre or up a steep slope:** Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa drop you at the door; Thành Mỹ uses a pickup truck to reach homestays on steep slopes.
- **You arrive at 6:00 and want to catch up on sleep:** Thành Mỹ has a free rest room with beds until 12:00; Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa have a shared guesthouse with beds.
- **Two of you want to lie side by side:** Khánh Châu's double cabin can be booked right on the website.

## Besides the sleeper cabin bus, how else can you get to Tà Xùa?

- **Taxi or private car from Hanoi:** some local drivers run the Hanoi – Tà Xùa route, a good fit for families with elderly relatives or young children, or for groups of 4 – 6 who want to set their own schedule. See the list on the [Tà Xùa taxi](/taxi) page and state your pickup point clearly to get a quote.
- **Regular coach to Bắc Yên:** cheap, but you have to find your own motorbike taxi or taxi for the remaining 15 km of mountain pass up to Tà Xùa.
- **Driving yourself by car or motorbike:** flexible, but the final stretch of pass is steep and often foggy. Detailed directions are in the article on [the road to Tà Xùa from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/duong-di-ta-xua-tu-ha-noi).

## How do you book a bus ticket to Tà Xùa?

1. Book your homestay first, because the operator needs the homestay's name and location to arrange your drop-off.
2. Open the [Hanoi – Tà Xùa sleeper cabin bus](/xe-cabin) page and choose your outbound and return dates. The system only shows operators that **still have seats on exactly those two days**.
3. Choose the operator, the number of single or double cabin tickets, and your pickup and drop-off points; leave your name and phone number.
4. Wait for a call back confirming your seats and explaining how to pay the deposit. The website does not take online payments.

If you also need accommodation, use the [sleeper cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo), which is 25.000đ per person cheaper than booking separately. For weekends in cloud season, book 5 – 7 days ahead.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which bus operators go to Tà Xùa?

The Hanoi – Tà Xùa route currently has 4 sleeper cabin bus operators running daily and on sale on Tà Xùa Booking: Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa. Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành also run seated limousines during the day.

### How much is a bus ticket to Tà Xùa?

A single cabin round trip from Hanoi is 500.000đ (Khánh Châu), 600.000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành) and 650.000đ (Quang Nghĩa). From the northern provinces it is around 700.000đ – 1.050.000đ round trip, shuttle transfer included.

### What time and where do buses to Tà Xùa depart?

Sleeper cabin buses leave Hanoi at 21:00 (Khánh Châu, 68 Mạc Thái Tông), 21:30 – 22:15 (Tiến Thành, Mễ Trì – Mạc Thái Tông area) and 22:00 (Thành Mỹ at 24 Đình Núp, Quang Nghĩa at CC2 Mễ Trì). They reach the centre of Tà Xùa at around 6:00.

### Is there a direct bus up to Tà Xùa, or do you have to change in Bắc Yên?

All the operators take passengers all the way to the centre of Tà Xùa. Depending on the operator and the type of vehicle, the 15 km from Bắc Yên town up to Tà Xùa may be covered by shuttle transfer; Tiến Thành's limousine runs straight to the centre with no change of vehicle.

### Which bus operator picks up at Nội Bài airport for Tà Xùa?

Thành Mỹ is the operator that publishes a fare for pickup from Nội Bài airport: 800.000đ round trip per person.

### How many days ahead should you book a bus ticket to Tà Xùa?

On weekdays, booking 1 – 2 days ahead is enough. For Friday and Saturday nights in cloud season and for public holidays, book 5 – 7 days ahead; on any day an operator is sold out, that operator is automatically hidden from the search results.
