**Thành Mỹ** is a bus company running the Hanoi – Tà Xùa (Sơn La) route with 2024-model sleeper cabin buses and limousine vans. A return cabin ticket from Hanoi costs **600.000đ per person**; the bus leaves 24 Đinh Núp at 22:00 and reaches the centre of Tà Xùa at around 6:00 in the morning. What sets it apart most from the other operators on the route: its office in Tà Xùa has a **rest room with beds, free to use until 12:00 noon**, and it has a **pickup truck** to take guests up to homestays perched on steep slopes.

## Which route does Thành Mỹ run, and where does it pick up?

According to the information the company publishes, Thành Mỹ specialises in the **Tà Xùa – Bắc Yên – Hanoi** route and also connects a few places around Hanoi:

- **Hanoi:** the boarding point for the cabin bus is **24 Đinh Núp**, Cầu Giấy.
- **Nội Bài Airport:** a good fit for travellers flying in from central or southern Vietnam and heading straight up to the mountains. The price list carries the note "pickup from 2", so ask about the pickup conditions if you are travelling alone.
- **Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang, Hải Dương, Hải Phòng:** each has its own return fare.
- **Hưng Yên:** the company does pick up here but has not listed a price; state your pickup point to get a quote.

The pickup notes currently shown on the company's price list:

- **Bắc Giang:** pickup at 19:30 at BigC, Đình Trám, Vân Trung, Quang Châu.
- **Hải Dương:** pickup at BigC, the 25-storey building, 559, Ghẽ.
- **Hải Phòng:** two slots, 5:00 and 19:30, with pickup at your door.

Travellers from the provinces should check the exact pickup time when they get the confirmation call, because the shuttle transfer always sets off a few hours before the cabin bus departs. Pickup points by province for each operator are listed in the article [where sleeper cabin buses to Tà Xùa pick up](/kinh-nghiem/diem-don-xe-cabin-cac-tinh).

Thành Mỹ is not the cheapest choice; what you get for the extra 50.000đ to 100.000đ is a later departure and a rest room with beds when you arrive.

Two notes on pricing:

- The website currently sells single-cabin tickets. If you want a **double cabin**, add a note when booking so the company can arrange it and quote you a price.

## Thành Mỹ cabin bus schedule: leave at 22:00, arrive at 6:00

**Outbound (Hanoi up to Tà Xùa):**

- **22:00, 24 Đinh Núp:** board and settle into your cabin. Aim to arrive 15 to 20 minutes early.
- **Around 4:00, Bắc Yên:** a rest stop before the final 15km of mountain pass.
- **Around 6:00, Tà Xùa:** arrival, right when the clouds are usually at their best for the day.

**Return (Tà Xùa back to Hanoi):**

- **12:00:** passengers are picked up in central Tà Xùa.
- **13:00:** the bus leaves Bắc Yên town. The company's own introduction gives the return time as 13:00, so settle the pickup time when your ticket is confirmed.
- **Around 16:00:** rest stop in Ba Vì.
- **Around 19:00:** arrival in Hanoi.

Each leg takes about 8 hours. The 22:00 departure is a real advantage for people with day jobs: finish work at 18:30, have dinner, go home for your bags and still make it, whereas the buses leaving at 21:00 cut it rather fine.

## Thành Mỹ limousine vans: an option if you'd rather not travel overnight

Besides the cabin bus, Thành Mỹ runs **seated limousine vans**:

- **Hanoi to Tà Xùa:** 6:00 – 7:00 in the morning, or 22:00.
- **Tà Xùa to Hanoi:** 13:00 or 18:30.

The morning run suits older travellers, people who struggle to sleep on a bus, or anyone who wants to see the mountain pass in daylight. The 18:30 return is the only late return currently advertised on the route, giving you one more full afternoon in Tà Xùa.

The trade-off is that the morning run gets you there in the early afternoon, so you miss the first morning of cloud hunting. The limousine schedule is not yet on sale directly on the website; write "limousine" and your preferred time slot in the notes when you send your request.

## Arriving in Tà Xùa at 6:00: a free rest room with beds until 12:00

This is the part of Thành Mỹ we rate most highly. The night bus arrives at 6:00, while homestays usually don't allow check-in until 14:00. At the company's office in Tà Xùa, passengers can use the following for free:

- **A rest room with beds** to catch up on sleep after the overnight ride.
- **Somewhere to brush your teeth, wash your face and change clothes.**
- **Luggage storage** while you go out exploring.

These facilities are available **until 12:00 noon**. You can also rent a motorbike (for a fee) right at the office. A sample morning: arrive at 6:00, sleep until 8:00, leave your suitcase, rent a bike to Đỉnh Gió and the cloud-hunting cafés, come back at 12:00 for your things and have lunch, then check in at 14:00. More detailed suggestions are in the article [what to do after getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa at 6:00](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## Does Thành Mỹ drop you off at your homestay?

Yes, in the three cases the company describes:

- **Homestays in the centre with easy road access:** guests are taken to the door.
- **Homestays on steep slopes:** the company uses a **pickup truck** as a shuttle transfer up the hill, depending on the location and actual road conditions.
- **Luggage:** staff take it to your homestay free of charge, under the company's policy.

The company asks guests to **give the name and location of their homestay in advance** so it can check the road and arrange a vehicle. So the sensible order is to choose a [Tà Xùa homestay](/homestay) first and book the bus afterwards. If you are staying in Khe Cải, Chung Trinh or further out, also read [do sleeper cabin buses drop you at your homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

## Luggage rules

The company recommends bringing a **medium-sized suitcase or a small bag** because the Bắc Yên to Tà Xùa stretch involves a shuttle transfer; large suitcases need to be declared in advance, and bulky luggage may have to go in a separate vehicle at an extra charge.

## Thành Mỹ and two homestays under the same owner

A detail few people know: the owner of Thành Mỹ also runs two places to stay in Tà Xùa, [Thơ Garden](/homestay/tho-garden-ta-xua) in the centre (rooms from 450.000đ on weekdays) and [Mây Hạ Retreat](/homestay/may-ha-retreat) in Mống Vàng (rooms from 550.000đ). If you travel with Thành Mỹ and stay at either one, pickups, drop-offs and luggage storage tend to be easier to arrange because there is a single point of contact. ## Thành Mỹ review: strengths and things to weigh up

**Strengths:**

- **A free rest room with beds until 12:00**, plus somewhere to freshen up and store your bags. Only three operators on the route advertise beds for the wait.
- **A 22:00 departure**, later than Khánh Châu and Xuân Trường, so less of a rush for office workers.
- **2024-model cabin buses**, with both single and double cabins, each with its own screen and window.
- **Daytime limousine vans and an 18:30 return.**
- **Pickup at Nội Bài Airport**, a point the other operators on our platform have not listed.
- **A pickup truck up to homestays on steep slopes**, with luggage taken to your homestay for free.

**Things to weigh up:**

- **100.000đ more expensive than Khánh Châu** per return ticket from Hanoi; from Bắc Ninh and Hải Dương the gap is 150.000đ to 250.000đ.
- **Fewer provincial pickup points** than Khánh Châu (10 provinces) and Tiến Thành (8 provinces). No fares for Hưng Yên, Hà Nam, Thái Bình, Ninh Bình, Nam Định, Thái Nguyên or Quảng Ninh.
- **Only one rest stop on the way up, at Bắc Yên**, according to the published itinerary; if you need a stop along the way, tell the bus attendant.
- **Double cabins and limousine vans can't yet be booked directly on the website**; you have to add a note to have them arranged.
- **No publicly posted policy on changing or cancelling tickets.** Ask clearly when you pay your deposit.

**Who is Thành Mỹ a good fit for?**

- People who finish work late and need the 22:00 departure.
- People who want a bed to catch up on sleep after arriving at 6:00.
- Travellers flying into Nội Bài and going straight on to Tà Xùa.
- Older travellers, or anyone who wants a daytime limousine van.
- Guests staying at Thơ Garden, Mây Hạ Retreat or a hillside homestay in the central area.

If price is your priority, see the [Khánh Châu bus review](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-khanh-chau-ta-xua). ## How to book a Thành Mỹ ticket

1. Open the [Thành Mỹ bus](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-thanh-my-2) page and choose your outbound and return dates.
2. Choose the number of tickets for each direction.
3. Choose your pickup and drop-off points. Give the name of your homestay, and add a note if you want a double cabin or a limousine van.
4. Leave your name and phone number, then send the request.

The website does not take online payments. You will get a call back to confirm your seats and explain how to pay the deposit; use your booking code to check it again on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page. To book a room at the same time, use the [sleeper cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo): the system pairs a return ticket with an available room and takes 25.000đ off per guest.

## Frequently asked questions about Thành Mỹ

### How much is a Thành Mỹ ticket to Tà Xùa?

A return single-cabin ticket from Hanoi is 600.000đ per person. From Nội Bài Airport it is 800.000đ; Bắc Ninh, Bắc Giang and Hải Dương 950.000đ; Hải Phòng 1.050.000đ.

### Where does Thành Mỹ pick up in Hanoi?

The boarding point for the cabin bus is 24 Đinh Núp, Cầu Giấy. The bus departs at 22:00.

### What time does the Thành Mỹ bus arrive in Tà Xùa?

The cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 22:00, stops in Bắc Yên at around 4:00 and reaches the centre of Tà Xùa at around 6:00 in the morning, about 8 hours in total.

### What time does the Thành Mỹ bus leave on the way back?

The bus picks up passengers in central Tà Xùa at 12:00, leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and gets to Hanoi at around 19:00. The limousine van has an additional return at 18:30.

### If I arrive in Tà Xùa early and can't check in yet, where can I rest?

Thành Mỹ's office in Tà Xùa has a rest room with beds, somewhere to wash your face and change, and luggage storage, free for passengers until 12:00 noon.

### Does Thành Mỹ run a daytime limousine van?

Yes. The limousine van leaves Hanoi at around 6:00 – 7:00 in the morning and returns at 13:00 or 18:30. Add a note when booking so the company can arrange your trip.

### Does Thành Mỹ pick up at Nội Bài Airport?

Yes, the return fare is 800.000đ per person. Ask the company about the pickup time and conditions when you book.

### Do Thành Mỹ buses have double cabins?

Yes, there are double cabins on the 2024-model buses. The website currently sells single cabins, so write "double cabin" in the notes when you send your request.
