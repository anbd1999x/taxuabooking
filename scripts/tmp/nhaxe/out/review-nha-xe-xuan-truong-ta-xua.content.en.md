**Xuân Trường** (Xuân Trường Travel) is a bus company running overnight sleeper cabin buses on the Hanoi – Tà Xùa (Sơn La) route. A return ticket from Hanoi costs **550.000đ per person**, the second lowest on the route. The bus leaves Hanoi at **21:30**, reaches Bắc Yên at around 5:15 and gets up to Tà Xùa at about 6:00 in the morning. Besides Hanoi, the company lists pickup fares for **Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang**; Vĩnh Phúc in particular is a pickup point no other operator on Tà Xùa Booking lists.

Where Xuân Trường sits among all the fares on the route:

- **From Hanoi:** Khánh Châu 500.000đ, **Xuân Trường 550.000đ**, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 600.000đ, Quang Nghĩa 650.000đ.
- **From Bắc Ninh:** Khánh Châu 700.000đ, **Xuân Trường 800.000đ**, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 950.000đ.
- **From Bắc Giang:** Khánh Châu and **Xuân Trường both 800.000đ**, Thành Mỹ 950.000đ, Tiến Thành 1.000.000đ.
- **From Vĩnh Phúc:** only Xuân Trường lists a fare, 600.000đ.

Xuân Trường is a budget-friendly choice: just 50.000đ more than the cheapest operator from Hanoi, and level with the cheapest if you are in Bắc Giang. These are the fares currently listed; they may change around public holidays and Tết, and the correct price is the one shown when you pick your date.

## Xuân Trường schedule: leave at 21:30, arrive at 6:00

**Outbound (Hanoi up to Tà Xùa), according to the route published by the company:**

- **21:30:** departure, pickup **opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital**.
- **Around 5:15:** arrives in Bắc Yên town for a rest stop before the final 15km of mountain pass.
- **Around 6:00:** arrives in Tà Xùa, in time for the morning clouds.

**Return (Tà Xùa back to Hanoi):**

- **12:00:** passengers are collected in central Tà Xùa and taken by shuttle transfer down to Bắc Yên.
- **13:00:** the bus leaves Bắc Yên town.
- **Around 19:00:** arrives back in Hanoi.

The outbound leg takes about eight and a half hours, the return about seven. The 21:30 departure sits between two groups: half an hour later than Khánh Châu (21:00) and half an hour earlier than Thành Mỹ and Quang Nghĩa (22:00).

Be at the pickup point 15 to 20 minutes before departure.

## What is on board a Xuân Trường bus?

The photos supplied by the company show a double-deck cabin bus with a row of cabins on either side of the aisle, **each cabin with its own screen**, a mattress long enough to stretch your legs out fully, and blue LED lights along the ceiling.

## Tips for booking with Xuân Trường

- **The return trip starts with a transfer:** at 12:00 the company gathers passengers in central Tà Xùa and takes them down to Bắc Yên, and the big bus leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00. If your homestay is far from the centre, allow extra time to reach the meeting point.
- **Give the name of your homestay when booking** so the company can arrange your drop-off and return pickup.

The bus reaches Tà Xùa at about 6:00, while homestays usually check you in from 14:00; ways to use those hours are covered in [what to do after getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa at 6:00](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi). If your homestay is far from the centre, read [do cabin buses drop you at your homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua) first.

## Xuân Trường review: strengths and points to weigh up

**Strengths:**

- **Budget-friendly fare:** 550.000đ return from Hanoi, the second lowest of the 5 operators.
- **Lists a pickup fare for Vĩnh Phúc** (600.000đ), a point no other operator lists.
- **Good fares from Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang:** 800.000đ, which is 150.000đ to 200.000đ lower than Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành.
- **21:30 departure**, less of a rush than the 21:00 bus.
- **The 12:00 return matches the check-out time** of most homestays.

**Points to weigh up:**

- **Only one rest stop on the outbound trip** (Bắc Yên, 5:15) according to the published route.
- **Few provincial pickup points:** no fares for Hải Dương, Hải Phòng, Hưng Yên, Thái Bình, Ninh Bình or Nam Định.

**Who is Xuân Trường a good fit for?**

- Budget travellers who are used to night buses and do not need many extra services.
- Passengers in Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang.
- People staying at a homestay in the central area who can get around on their own on arrival.

## How to book a Xuân Trường ticket

1. Open the [Xuân Trường bus company](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-xuan-truong) page and choose your outbound and return dates.
2. Choose the number of tickets for each direction.
3. Choose your pickup and drop-off points; passengers in Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang should state their area clearly. Put the name of your homestay in the notes.
4. Leave your name and phone number, then send the request.

The website does not take payment online. You will get a call back to confirm your seats and pickup point and to explain how to pay the deposit; you can look up your booking code on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page. To add a room in one go, use the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo): the fare is calculated from your actual departure point and each guest gets 25.000đ off. If you have nowhere to stay yet, pick a [Tà Xùa homestay](/homestay) first.

## Frequently asked questions about Xuân Trường

### How much is a Xuân Trường ticket to Tà Xùa?

A return ticket from Hanoi is 550.000đ per person. From Vĩnh Phúc it is 600.000đ; from Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang, 800.000đ.

### What time does the Xuân Trường bus to Tà Xùa leave?

The bus leaves Hanoi at 21:30, reaches Bắc Yên town at around 5:15 and gets up to Tà Xùa at about 6:00 in the morning. On the way back, passengers are collected in central Tà Xùa at 12:00, the bus leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and arrives in Hanoi at around 19:00.

### Where does Xuân Trường pick up passengers in Hanoi?

The Hanoi pickup point is opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital, with departure at 21:30. Be there 15 to 20 minutes early.

### Does Xuân Trường pick up in Vĩnh Phúc?

Yes, at a return fare of 600.000đ per person. It is the only operator on Tà Xùa Booking that lists a pickup fare for Vĩnh Phúc.

### Is Xuân Trường the cheapest on the Hanoi – Tà Xùa route?

No. Khánh Châu is the cheapest at 500.000đ return; Xuân Trường comes second at 550.000đ. From Bắc Giang, the two operators charge the same 800.000đ.

### How do I book a Xuân Trường ticket?

Choose your outbound and return dates on the Xuân Trường page on Tà Xùa Booking and leave your phone number. The website does not take payment online; you will get a call back to confirm and to explain how to pay the deposit.
