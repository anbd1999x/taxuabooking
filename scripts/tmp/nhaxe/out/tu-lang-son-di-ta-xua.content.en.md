**No sleeper cabin bus picks up in Lạng Sơn for Tà Xùa yet: you travel to Hanoi first (about 150km, roughly 3 hours by coach), then board a night cabin bus leaving at 21:00 to 22:00, with return tickets from 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person.** The bus reaches Bắc Yên town at around 4:00, where you switch to a shuttle for the final 15km of mountain pass and arrive in Tà Xùa at about 6:00. Total time on the road for both legs is around 11 to 12 hours.

**Quick summary:**

- **Operators picking up in Lạng Sơn:** none so far. None of the 5 cabin bus operators on Tà Xùa Booking lists a fare for a Lạng Sơn pickup.
- **The reliable way:** take a coach from Lạng Sơn to Hanoi on the Bắc Giang – Lạng Sơn expressway, then board the cabin bus in the Cầu Giấy or Mỹ Đình area.
- **Return cabin fares from Hanoi:** Khánh Châu 500.000đ, Xuân Trường 550.000đ, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 600.000đ, Quang Nghĩa 650.000đ.
- **An option on your way:** 4 operators list a pickup fare in Bắc Giang (now part of Bắc Ninh province), from 800.000đ to 1.000.000đ return. Every Lạng Sơn to Hanoi trip passes through Bắc Giang anyway, but the meeting point needs careful checking.
- **Coming back:** the bus leaves Bắc Yên at 13:00 and is expected in Hanoi at 19:00. If you want to reach Lạng Sơn the same evening, check for a late coach.
- **Payment:** the website takes no online payment. We call you back to hold your seat and explain the deposit.

## How far is it from Lạng Sơn to Tà Xùa, and which way do you go?

Tà Xùa is a highland commune in Sơn La province, formerly part of Bắc Yên district. Lạng Sơn lies in the northeast and Tà Xùa in the northwest, and no coach route links the two directly. Every sensible itinerary bends through Hanoi.

Adding the legs together, you cover about 350 to 390km one way:

| Leg | Distance | Transport | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lạng Sơn city to Hanoi | about 150km | coach on the Bắc Giang – Lạng Sơn expressway | about 3 hours |
| Hanoi to Bắc Yên town | part of the 200 to 240km between Hanoi and Tà Xùa | overnight sleeper cabin bus | arrives around 4:00 |
| Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa | about 15km of mountain pass | the bus operator's shuttle | arrives around 6:00 |

For why the Hanoi to Tà Xùa leg is quoted as 200 to 240km, see [where Tà Xùa is and how far it is from Hanoi](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-o-dau-cach-ha-noi-bao-nhieu-km). Full-size cabin buses stop in Bắc Yên, and the operator's shuttle covers the last stretch of pass. That is why you need to give your homestay's name when booking: the operator uses it to plan your drop-off.

## Does any cabin bus pick up passengers in Lạng Sơn?

**No.** As of 27/11/2026, the four operators that pick up outside Hanoi only list fares for 12 provinces plus Nội Bài airport, and Lạng Sơn is not among them. Quang Nghĩa only lists the Hanoi leg.

Two consequences for travelers from Lạng Sơn:

1. On the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo) page, the departure point box has no Lạng Sơn option. Choose Hanoi, or Bắc Giang if you plan to board there.
2. The Lạng Sơn to Hanoi coach ticket is something you buy separately. Our system has no fare for that leg, so check the coach company's page for your travel date.

To see all 12 provinces with a pickup fare, open [cabin bus pickup points by province](/kinh-nghiem/diem-don-xe-cabin-cac-tinh).

## How do you get from Lạng Sơn to Hanoi in time for the night bus?

**Coach is the main choice.** From Lạng Sơn city, coaches follow National Highway 1A and the Bắc Giang – Lạng Sơn expressway, then continue on the Hanoi – Bắc Giang expressway into the capital. The province's main terminal is Lạng Sơn Northern Bus Station (Bến xe phía Bắc Lạng Sơn). Coaches to Hanoi drop off at several different stations, so ask which one when you buy your ticket.

All 5 cabin bus boarding points are on the west side of Hanoi:

- **68 Mạc Thái Tông** (Khánh Châu) and **24 Đinh Núp** (Thành Mỹ) are both in Trung Hòa, in the former Cầu Giấy district.
- **Mễ Trì** (Tiến Thành) and **CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình** (Quang Nghĩa) are close to Mỹ Đình Bus Station.
- **Opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital** (Xuân Trường), departing at 21:30.

If your coach from Lạng Sơn ends at Mỹ Đình station, you only need a short taxi ride. If it ends at a station on the east or south side, you have to cross the inner city at rush hour, so add 1 hour.

**Train:** Lạng Sơn has Lạng Sơn station and Đồng Đăng station on the rail line to Hanoi. Passenger services on this line have changed timetable several times, so check the railway's ticket site before counting on it. Lạng Sơn has no airport.

**Private car:** a group of 4 to 7 can drive themselves. Read [driving to Tà Xùa by car](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai) first.

The last column is an estimate, not the timetable of any coach company. Three notes to go with it:

## Can you board in Bắc Giang instead of going into Hanoi?

On paper yes, but ask carefully before you commit. Bắc Giang sits right on the road from Lạng Sơn to Hanoi, and 4 operators list a return fare for this pickup point:

| Operator | Return from Bắc Giang | Return from Hanoi | Difference | Operator's note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Khánh Châu | 800.000đ | 500.000đ | 300.000đ | Transfer |
| Xuân Trường | 800.000đ | 550.000đ | 250.000đ | — |
| Thành Mỹ | 950.000đ | 600.000đ | 350.000đ | pickup at 19:30 at BigC, Đình Trám, Vân Trung, Quang Châu |
| Tiến Thành | 1.000.000đ | 600.000đ | 400.000đ | Transfer |

"Transfer" means a smaller vehicle run by the operator takes you from Bắc Giang to the cabin bus boarding point in Hanoi, and does the reverse on the way back.

- **Good for:** solo travelers with a lot of luggage who would rather not juggle bus stations in Hanoi.
- **Not good for:** anyone trying to save money. The ticket costs 250.000đ to 400.000đ more than boarding in Hanoi, and you still pay for the Lạng Sơn to Bắc Giang coach.

Before choosing, ask three things: the exact pickup point in Bắc Giang, the pickup time (only Thành Mỹ states 19:30, the other three confirm by phone), and what time the bus drops you in Bắc Giang on the way back. Whether the Lạng Sơn coach actually stops at those points is a question for the coach company.

## Back in Hanoi at 19:00, can you still get home to Lạng Sơn?

The 19:00 arrival is an estimate that operators do not guarantee. Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành and Thành Mỹ also take a rest stop in Ba Vì at around 16:00.

You have three ways to handle it:

- **Go home the same evening:** ask the coach company in advance whether a service leaves Hanoi after 20:00 and where it picks up. Only buy that ticket once the cabin bus is inside the city.
- **Stay one night in Hanoi:** catch an early coach back to Lạng Sơn on Monday morning.
- **Push the return back:** stay one more night in Tà Xùa and return to Hanoi on Monday evening. See the [3-day, 2-night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).

Tips for travelers from Lạng Sơn:

- Book the room first, then the bus. See [which area of Tà Xùa to stay in](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao) to pick a place the shuttle can reach.
- The last 15km is full of bends. If you get carsick easily, read [how to avoid motion sickness on the pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua) beforehand.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to get from Lạng Sơn to Tà Xùa?

About 3 hours by coach to Hanoi, plus 8 to 9 hours on the night cabin bus. Leave Lạng Sơn at around 16:00 and you are in Tà Xùa at 6:00 the next morning.

### How much is the bus from Lạng Sơn to Tà Xùa?

A return cabin ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person depending on the operator. The Lạng Sơn to Hanoi coach is charged separately, so check the coach company's page for the fare on your date.

### Is there a direct bus from Lạng Sơn to Tà Xùa?

No cabin bus operator lists a pickup fare in Lạng Sơn yet. The nearest point with a fare is Bắc Giang, at 800.000đ to 1.000.000đ return, listed by 4 operators.

### Does the cabin bus take you all the way to your homestay in Tà Xùa?

The big bus stops in Bắc Yên town, and the remaining 15km of pass is covered by the operator's shuttle. The drop-off point depends on the road into each homestay. Details are in [how far the shuttle takes you](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

### Which departure point do you choose for a combo from Lạng Sơn?

Choose Hanoi if you make your own way to the capital, or Bắc Giang if you board there. A combo saves each guest 25.000đ compared with buying the bus and the room separately.

### Do you have to transfer money right away when booking?

No. You send a request on the website, and Tà Xùa Booking calls back to tell you whether seats are available, set the pickup time and explain the deposit. You can look up your booking code on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page.
