**The most common way to get from Hanoi to Tà Xùa is an overnight sleeper cabin bus: you board in the Cầu Giấy or Mỹ Đình area between 21:00 and 22:00, sleep for 8 to 9 hours, and reach Tà Xùa at around 6:00 in the morning.** Five operators run every day, and a round-trip ticket in a single cabin costs 500.000đ to 650.000đ per person. The road from Hanoi up to Tà Xùa (Sơn La) is roughly 200 to 240km depending on the route.

**Quick summary:**

- **How to go:** 5 sleeper cabin bus operators run direct from Hanoi to Bắc Yên every night: Khánh Châu, Xuân Trường, Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa.
- **Fares from Hanoi:** round trip 500.000đ (Khánh Châu), 550.000đ (Xuân Trường), 600.000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành), 650.000đ (Quang Nghĩa), for 1 passenger in a single cabin.
- **Where to board:** 68 Mạc Thái Tông, 24 Đinh Núp, the Mễ Trì area and CC2 Mễ Trì (Mỹ Đình), and opposite the gate of Hồng Ngọc Hospital.
- **Timing:** buses leave at 21:00, 21:30 or 22:00; they reach Bắc Yên town at around 4:00, you switch to a smaller vehicle for the 15km mountain pass, and arrive in Tà Xùa at around 6:00.
- **Back to Hanoi:** pickup in central Tà Xùa at 12:00, departure from Bắc Yên at 13:00, expected arrival in the city at 19:00.
- **Booking:** send a request on the Tà Xùa Booking cabin bus page. There is no online payment; we call you back to confirm your seat and pickup time.

## How far is the pickup point from each district?

The distances below are street estimates to the Cầu Giấy and Mễ Trì area. Actual travel time depends on traffic, especially from 17:00 to 19:30 on Trần Duy Hưng, Phạm Hùng and Nguyễn Trãi.

- **Hoàn Kiếm Lake, the Old Quarter, Hanoi Station:** about 7 to 9km. Go via Kim Mã or Láng Hạ, then onto Trần Duy Hưng.
- **Hà Đông:** about 5 to 7km. Tố Hữu and Lê Văn Lương lead towards Cầu Giấy.
- **Hoàng Mai, Giáp Bát and Nước Ngầm bus stations:** about 10 to 12km along Ring Road 3.
- **Long Biên, Gia Lâm:** about 13 to 16km. You have to cross the Red River and then the inner city, so leave much earlier.
- **Đông Anh, Sóc Sơn, Nội Bài Airport:** about 25 to 30km via Nhật Tân or Thăng Long bridge.
- **Mỹ Đình bus station:** only about 1 to 3km from the points in Mễ Trì and on Mạc Thái Tông. Very handy if friends are coming in from other provinces to travel with you.

A small tip: arrive 20 to 30 minutes before departure to stow your suitcase in the hold and find your berth. Have dinner before boarding, because the first rest stop, in Phú Thọ, comes at around 2:00 in the morning.

## What is a night on the bus from Hanoi to Tà Xùa like?

Take a Friday evening for someone with an office job:

1. **18:00 to 19:30:** go home, grab your things, have dinner. Keep luggage to one backpack or a small suitcase.
2. **20:30:** be at the meeting point.
3. **21:00 to 22:00:** the bus leaves Hanoi through the western gateway and crosses into Phú Thọ.
4. **Around 2:00:** Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành stop for a break in Phú Thọ. Use the restroom now if you need to.
5. **Around 4:00:** the bus reaches Bắc Yên town. Xuân Trường lists 5:15 instead.
6. **Around 6:00:** arrival in Tà Xùa.

The last leg needs to be understood correctly. **The full-size cabin bus stops in Bắc Yên town.** The stretch from Bắc Yên up to Tà Xùa is about 15km of mountain pass, and passengers move to the operator's own shuttle, either a limousine van or a 16 or 29-seat vehicle. Ask clearly how this leg is charged when you book. Where the shuttle drops you depends on where your homestay is, so give the name of your accommodation when you book. Which cases get door-to-door drop-off and which mean walking or hiring another ride is explained in [do cabin buses drop you at your homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

For upper or lower deck, single or double cabin, read [a night on the cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/trai-nghiem-xe-cabin-di-ta-xua). If winding roads make you queasy, check the [tips for motion sickness on the pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua) first.

You arrive at 6:00, while homestays usually hand over rooms at 14:00. What to do with that gap is covered in [what to do after getting off the night bus at 6am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## What time do you get back to Hanoi, and can you make work on Monday?

Yes. The return schedule is the same for all 5 operators:

- **12:00:** pickup in central Tà Xùa. Check out and have lunch early.
- **13:00:** the cabin bus leaves Bắc Yên.
- **Around 16:00:** Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành take a break in Ba Vì.
- **Around 19:00:** arrival in Hanoi.

The 19:00 mark is an estimate that operators do not guarantee. On Sunday evenings the western gateway to the city is often crowded, so do not plan anything important before 20:30.

## How do you plan a weekend trip from Hanoi?

Two schedules Hanoi travellers usually pick:

| 2 days 1 night | Friday evening | Sunday evening | 1 night (Saturday) | None |

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

The overnight cabin bus takes about 8 to 9 hours: it leaves Hanoi between 21:00 and 22:00 and reaches Tà Xùa at around 6:00 the next morning. The distance is roughly 200 to 240km depending on the route.

### How much is a bus ticket from Hanoi to Tà Xùa?

A round-trip single cabin ticket for 1 passenger is 500.000đ (Khánh Châu), 550.000đ (Xuân Trường), 600.000đ (Thành Mỹ, Tiến Thành) and 650.000đ (Quang Nghĩa).

### Do buses to Tà Xùa leave from Mỹ Đình bus station?

None of the 5 operators lists Mỹ Đình bus station as its departure point. Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa pick up in the Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình area; Khánh Châu and Thành Mỹ pick up on Mạc Thái Tông and Đinh Núp, all a few kilometres from the station.

### Is there a daytime bus from Hanoi up to Tà Xùa?

All 5 cabin bus operators on Tà Xùa Booking run the uphill leg at night, leaving between 21:00 and 22:00. Only the downhill leg runs in the daytime, leaving central Tà Xùa at 12:00.

### Does the cabin bus go all the way up to Tà Xùa?

The full-size cabin bus stops in Bắc Yên town. The remaining 15km or so of mountain pass is covered by the operator's shuttle, arriving in Tà Xùa at around 6:00.

### Can you be picked up in Nội Bài or Đông Anh?

Thành Mỹ has a fare for pickup at Nội Bài Airport, 800.000đ round trip; the price list says "đón từ 2" (pickup from 2), so ask about the conditions if you travel alone. Other points outside the inner city are not in the price list yet, so ask when your ticket is confirmed.
