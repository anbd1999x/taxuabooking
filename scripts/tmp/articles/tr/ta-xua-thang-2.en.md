TITLE: Tà Xùa in February: plum blossom, Tết prices, best dates
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in February: white plum and peach blossom, mid-season clouds, nights 5–10°C. Tết 2027 holiday rates 06–11/02. Prices at 5 homestays, best dates.

Tà Xùa in February is the month of white plum blossom, village peach blossom and Tết, the Lunar New Year. It is still cold, 10–18°C by day and 5–10°C at night, and the clouds are still in their thick season. In 2027, the first day of Tết falls on Saturday 06/02, and the Tà Xùa Booking system charges holiday rates for 6 nights, from 06/02 to 11/02. **If you want flowers and clouds without paying Tết prices, go from 12/02 onwards, on midweek nights.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Flowers:** white plum and village peach blossom bloom from late January to the end of February, peaking around Tết.
- **Clouds:** February is still in the main cloud season (November to March), and early morning, 5:30–7:30, is when you are most likely to see them.
- **Temperature:** days 10–18°C, nights 5–10°C; a hard cold snap can drop to 2–5°C.
- **Holiday rates:** 6 nights from 06/02/2027 to 11/02/2027. The nights of 01–05/02 and from 12/02 are charged by day of the week.
- **Price gap:** at the 5 homestays in this article, the cheapest room on a holiday night costs 200.000đ–600.000đ more than on a weekday.
- **Bus tickets:** the listed return sleeper cabin bus fare from Hanoi is currently 500.000đ–650.000đ per person; ask the bus operator in advance about Tết schedules and prices.

## Which days in February are best for Tà Xùa?

February 2027 starts on Monday 01/02 and splits into three periods that differ a lot in price and crowds. The four Saturday nights are 06/02, 13/02, 20/02 and 27/02; 14/02 is a Sunday.

| Period | Calendar | Room prices | Crowds | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before Tết, 01–05/02 | Monday to Friday, the last days of the lunar year | Weekday rates, Friday rate on the night of 05/02 | Usually quiet because people are busy shopping for Tết | Travelers who want peace and have flexible days off |
| Tết, 06–11/02 | First to sixth day of Tết | Holiday rates on all 6 nights | Busiest of the month | Families who can only get away at Tết |
| After Tết, 12–28/02 | Plum and village peach blossom still out | Rates by day of the week; Saturdays are 13/02, 20/02, 27/02 | Busy at weekends, quiet midweek | Cloud hunters, flower photographers, couples |

The crowd level is what is typical for the holiday calendar, not a count. Some homestays and restaurants close for a few days around Lunar New Year's Eve, so ask before booking the nights of 04–06/02.

If your days off fall in the month before, our article [Tà Xùa in January](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-1) covers the tớ dày flower season. If you can push the trip to the month after, read [Tà Xùa in March](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-3), when it gets warmer and the clouds are still around.

## Where do plum and peach blossom bloom in February?

After the tớ dày bloom of January come white plum and village peach blossom.

- **White plum:** patches of white blossom around stilt houses and hillside fields, blooming from late January through the end of February.
- **Village peach:** peach trees planted in house yards, with double petals in pale pink, blooming at the same time as the plum.
- **Where to see them:** the flowers are scattered, not gathered into ticketed gardens. They are easiest to find along the road from the center to Háng Đồng, around the villages, and in the grounds of homestays and cafes.
- **Photo hours:** 6:30–8:30, while the sunlight is still low. Under harsh midday sun, white blossom easily blows out.

Blooming dates shift from year to year with the weather. Before you fix your dates, ask your host how far along the plum blossom in the village is. Ask permission before entering local people's yards and gardens, and do not break branches. The year-round flower calendar is in our article on [Tà Xùa flower seasons](/kinh-nghiem/mua-hoa-ta-xua).

## Weather and clouds in February

February is still winter in the mountains. Days are 10–18°C and warm when the sun is out; nights and early mornings are 5–10°C. A hard cold snap can pull temperatures down to 2–5°C.

As for clouds, February sits in the thickest period of the year. Clouds are likely when there was light rain or high humidity the night before and the sun comes out the next morning. When the monsoon wind blows hard, or after several dry sunny days in a row, the clouds usually do not rise. Drizzle and fog can also turn the whole morning white; in that case switch to the plan in our article on [what to do when cloud hunting fails](/kinh-nghiem/san-may-hut-o-ta-xua-lam-gi).

Clothing is the same as in January: a thermal top, a sweater, a windproof down jacket, a wool hat, gloves and shoes with good grip. A season-by-season checklist is in our article on [what to prepare for a trip to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/chuan-bi-gi-khi-di-ta-xua).

## What should you book ahead for a Tết trip to Tà Xùa?

Tết is when everything should be locked in earlier than usual.

- **Rooms:** cloud-view rooms and rooms with a bathtub go first. Hold your room 3–4 weeks before Tết.
- **Sleeper cabin bus:** the 5 bus operators on the system leave Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 and reach Tà Xùa around 6:00; the return trip departs at 12:00. The number of trips and ticket prices during Tết may differ from normal days, so ask before sending a deposit.
- **Food:** not every restaurant stays open through Tết. Ask whether your homestay cooks meals or hotpot, or call ahead to a place on the [Tà Xùa restaurants](/nha-hang) list.
- **Transport on the mountain:** arrange your motorbike taxi driver the evening before for a cloud hunting morning during Tết.

A tip for counting nights: the bus runs overnight, so the night you board is a night spent on the bus, with no room to pay for. Board on the evening of 11/02 (the sixth day of Tết), arrive on the morning of 12/02, and you stay the two full nights of 12/02 and 13/02 without a single holiday night.

## Is February good for couples?

Yes. 14/02/2027 is a Sunday, right after Tết week, so the weekend of 13–14/02 is likely to be busy. A few suggestions:

- Rooms with a bathtub beside a glass wall sell out first. [Tà Xùa Hill](/kinh-nghiem/review-ta-xua-hill) has 9 bathtub rooms and [Bếp Nhà Táo](/kinh-nghiem/review-bep-nha-tao-ta-xua) has 6.
- Shift to the nights of 15–18/02 for weekday rates and quiet photo spots.
- Sunset comes early in February, and by around 17:30 it is already getting dark. Pick a place in Đỉnh Gió or Khe Cải from the list of [cloud hunting cafes](/cafe) and be there before 17:00.

More ideas for bathtub rooms are in our article on [bathtubs with a sea of clouds view in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/bon-tam-view-bien-may-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa beautiful in February?

February is beautiful because you get both clouds and flowers: white plum and village peach blossom last until the end of the month, and the clouds are still in their thick season. The downsides are the cold, and a crowded Tết week with high room prices.

### Which nights are charged holiday rates at Tà Xùa homestays for Tết 2027?

On Tà Xùa Booking, holiday rates apply to 6 nights from 06/02/2027 (first day of Tết) to 11/02/2027 (sixth day of Tết). The nights of 05/02 and 12/02 are charged the Friday rate.

### Which month does plum blossom bloom in Tà Xùa?

White plum blossom in Tà Xùa usually blooms from late January to the end of February, peaking around Tết. Blooming dates shift each year, so ask your homestay before you go.

### Is Tà Xùa cold in February?

Yes. Days are 10–18°C, nights and early mornings 5–10°C, and a hard cold snap can drop to 2–5°C. Choose a room with a heated mattress or two-way air conditioning.

### Which days of Tết are less crowded in Tà Xùa?

Going by the holiday calendar, the days before Lunar New Year's Eve (01–05/02) and from the seventh day of Tết (12/02) onwards are usually quieter. The second to fifth days of Tết are when the most people are out on spring trips.

### Are there sleeper cabin buses to Tà Xùa during Tết?

Tết schedules are decided by each bus operator and can change from year to year. Check which trips are open by date on the sleeper cabin bus page and confirm with the operator before paying a deposit.
