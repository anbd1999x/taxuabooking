TITLE: Tà Xùa in August: late summer, táo mèo fruit, pre-2/9 prices
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in August: 20–26°C, rainy, few clouds, táo mèo in season. The night of 31/08 is at National Day rates. August vs September, and how to avoid them.

Tà Xùa in August is the last month of summer in the mountains: days are still a mild 20–26°C, there is still plenty of rain, and visitors are few. What sets this month apart comes down to two things. Táo mèo (wild hawthorn) starts its fruit season, and the night of 31/08 is already on the National Day holiday price calendar. **If you want a quiet trip at weekday prices, go before 30/08; from the night of 31/08 onward it is holiday rates and crowds.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Weather:** days 20–26°C, nights 17–20°C, lots of rain in the afternoon and at night, slippery dirt roads.
- **Clouds:** few, appearing after rain. The main cloud season does not start until November.
- **Táo mèo:** the fruit season runs from around August to October.
- **Xím Vàng rice:** not ripe yet. The golden rice season falls in September to early October.
- **Holiday rates:** the National Day holiday price calendar on Tà Xùa Booking covers three nights, 31/08, 01/09 and 02/09. In 2027, 02/09 is a Thursday.
- **Four Saturday nights:** 07/08, 14/08, 21/08 and 28/08.

## Which week in August should you visit Tà Xùa?

01/08/2027 is a Sunday. The month splits into three parts by the holiday calendar; the crowd level is what is typical.

| Dates | Room prices | Crowds | Suggestion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01–15/08 | Weekday rates, Saturdays are 07/08 and 14/08 | Quiet, with weekend visitors escaping the heat | Good for a relaxing break and for families going before the school year |
| 16–29/08 | Weekday rates, Saturdays are 21/08 and 28/08 | Quiet | The calmest stretch before the rice season and the holiday season |
| 30–31/08 | Night of 30/08 at normal rates, night of 31/08 at holiday rates | Rising fast | National Day holidaymakers start arriving |

Because 02/09/2027 falls on a Thursday, many people may extend their break into the weekend of 04–05/09. If your trip spans both months, see also our article [Tà Xùa in September](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-9), which has the holiday-night price table and the rice ripening calendar.

## August versus September: which is better?

Many people are torn between late August and mid-September. The two months sit side by side but differ in three ways: rice, holidays and visitor numbers.

| Criteria | August | September |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xím Vàng rice | Not ripe yet | Golden and ripe, at its best from mid-month |
| Rain | A lot | Still rainy, check the forecast close to your dates |
| Clouds | Few, appearing after rain | Cloud season has not started |
| Holiday-rate nights (2027) | 31/08 | 01/09 and 02/09 |
| Crowd level | Quiet | Moderate, busy around 2/9 and in the rice season |
| Best for | A cool getaway on a budget | Photographing the rice terraces |

In short: if you want quiet and cheap, choose August; if you want photos of golden rice, wait for mid-September. Neither month is the time to hunt for a sea of clouds yet, and the milestones of each season are in our article [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).

## What does the August táo mèo season offer?

Táo mèo, also known as sơn tra, is the tree most closely tied to Tà Xùa. The "Cây cô đơn" (Lone Tree) on the hilltop that everyone photographs is in fact a táo mèo tree. The fruit season runs from around August to October.

- **Where to buy:** ask your homestay host or buy from local people selling in the central area. Prices change with the harvest, so ask before buying a lot.
- **What to buy:** fresh fruit for steeping, or ready-made products. Pick fruit that feels firm in the hand, with no bruises.
- **Taking it home:** fresh fruit is heavy and bruises easily; wrap it separately and keep it at the top of your luggage.

More on the fruit season and how to steep it is in our article on [Tà Xùa táo mèo](/kinh-nghiem/tao-meo-ta-xua). Other gifts such as Shan Tuyết tea, with prices and where to buy, are covered in our article on [Tà Xùa specialties to take home as gifts](/kinh-nghiem/qua-ta-xua-mua-gi-lam-qua).

## August weather and the clouds

August is like July in temperature: 20–26°C during the day, 17–20°C at night. There is still a lot of rain, often falling in the afternoon and at night. After rain, the dirt roads up to Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Hồ Mắt Ngọc (Jade Eye Lake) are slippery; Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) should not be attempted in rain or strong wind.

August clouds are still summer clouds: thin, hard to predict, and most likely in the early morning after a rainy night. On a clear day, late-summer sunsets tend to be richly colored and are worth an afternoon spent sitting at Khe Cải or Đỉnh Gió. Suggested spots are in our article on [5 places to watch the sunset in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ngam-hoang-hon-ta-xua).

## August room prices and how to avoid holiday rates

August has only one holiday-rate night, 31/08. Three examples of listed prices for one room for one night:

- [Pao Homestay](/homestay/pao-homestay) in the center, 7 rooms: weekdays 550.000đ–850.000đ, holiday night 1.100.000đ–1.400.000đ.
- [Nhặt Nắng House](/homestay/nhat-nang-house-ta-xua) in the center, 17 rooms: weekdays 705.000đ–905.000đ, Saturday night 1.050.000đ–1.350.000đ, holiday night 1.100.000đ–1.600.000đ.
- [Tà Xùa Retreat](/homestay/ta-xua-retreat) in the center, 9 rooms all with breakfast: weekdays 768.000đ–1.248.000đ, Saturday night 1.056.000đ–1.632.000đ, holiday night 1.500.000đ–1.900.000đ.

At Pao Homestay, the cheapest room on the holiday night costs twice the weekday price. Two ways to plan around the night of 31/08:

- **Go on the weekend of 28–29/08.** Board the bus on Friday evening 27/08, stay the nights of 28/08 and 29/08, and head back at noon on Monday 30/08.
- **Go midweek before that.** The nights of 23–26/08 are all at weekday rates, and the photo spots are almost empty.

General tips on deposits and holding a room are in our article on [booking a Tà Xùa homestay](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-dat-phong-homestay-ta-xua).

## Getting around and what to pack in August

**Getting up there:** the sleeper cabin bus runs overnight from Hanoi, 500.000đ–650.000đ return per person. Buses leave Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 and reach the center around 6:00; the return trip leaves at 12:00 and reaches Hanoi around 19:00. A comparison table of five bus operators is in our article [comparing bus operators to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/so-sanh-nha-xe-di-ta-xua).

**In the mountains:** a manual motorbike rents for 180.000đ a day, but the road surface is wet, so it only suits riders used to mountain passes. A motorbike taxi from the center to Khe Cải costs 100.000đ one way for 1 passenger, and 150.000đ to Hồ Mắt Ngọc.

**Packing:**

- A two-piece rain suit and a waterproof bag.
- Shoes with good grip, sandals, spare socks.
- A light jacket for evenings at 17–20°C.
- Sunscreen and insect repellent.
- A cloth bag or hard box if you plan to buy fresh táo mèo.

## A suggested 2 days 1 night at the end of August

- **Friday evening:** board the sleeper cabin bus in Hanoi.
- **Saturday morning:** arrive around 6:00. If it is dry, go straight to the Grasslands and Cây cô đơn route while the road is still dry. Check in at noon and rest.
- **Saturday afternoon:** sit at a cafe in Khe Cải and wait for the sunset, then head back before the mist comes down. Have hotpot or grilled food in the evening.
- **Sunday morning:** at 5:00 check the sky for drifting clouds, stop by Đỉnh Gió, and buy táo mèo and tea. Board the bus back at 12:00.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa beautiful in August?

August is beautiful in a late-summer way: green mountains, cool air, few visitors, táo mèo in season. A thick sea of clouds is rare and the Xím Vàng rice is not ripe yet.

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in August?

Few. August clouds are usually low drifting clouds in the early morning after a rainy night. Tà Xùa's main cloud season starts in November.

### Is the rice in Xím Vàng ripe in August?

Not yet. Xím Vàng rice ripens from September to early October and is usually at its best from mid-September. Ask about the state of the rice before you go.

### Which months are táo mèo season in Tà Xùa?

Tà Xùa táo mèo is in fruit from around August to October. You can buy it from local people in the central area or ask your homestay host where to go.

### Which nights are charged holiday rates at Tà Xùa homestays for National Day 2/9?

On Tà Xùa Booking, National Day holiday rates apply to three nights: 31/08, 01/09 and 02/09. The nights of 30/08 and 03/09 are priced by day of the week.

### Is Tà Xùa cold in August?

Not cold, but cool. It is 20–26°C during the day and 17–20°C at night, so bring a light jacket for evenings and early mornings.
