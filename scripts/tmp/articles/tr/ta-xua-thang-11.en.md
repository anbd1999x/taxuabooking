TITLE: Tà Xùa in November: early clouds, no holiday rates, worth it?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in November opens the cloud season: days 10–18°C, some nights below 10°C, dry, no holiday rates. Prices by weekday at 5 homestays and a sample budget.

Tà Xùa in November is the opening month of the main cloud season: the weather turns dry and cold, daytime is 10–18°C, some nights drop below 10°C, and the whole month has no holiday on the room price calendar. Is November worth a trip to Tà Xùa? **Yes. It is the most balanced month of the year: the clouds are already here, the hard cold is not, and room prices have only two levels, weekday and weekend.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** the start of the main cloud season, which lasts until March. No day is guaranteed to have clouds, so stay 2 nights.
- **Temperature:** days 10–18°C with dry sunshine; nights and early mornings are cold, sometimes below 10°C.
- **Scenery:** dã quỳ (wild sunflowers) bloom along the roads and the grass on the Grasslands is still golden.
- **Room prices:** no holiday-rate nights. The four Saturday nights of November 2027 are 06/11, 13/11, 20/11 and 27/11.
- **Roads:** dry weather and little rain make the road up easier than in summer.
- **Bus tickets:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person.

## Why is November considered the most balanced month?

Put November next to the two peak months that follow and the picture is clear. The cloud and flower markers are typical seasonal levels.

| Criteria | November | December | January |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clouds | Start of the main cloud season | Mid-season | Mid-season |
| Night temperature | Cold, sometimes below 10°C | 5–10°C, cold snaps 2–5°C | 5–10°C, cold snaps 2–5°C |
| Flowers, scenery | Dã quỳ, golden grass | Tớ dày from late in the month | Tớ dày, plum blossom late in the month |
| Holiday-rate nights | None | 31/12 | 01/01 |
| Crowds | Busy on weekends | Very busy at the end of the month | Very busy at the start of the month |
| Road weather | Dry, little rain | Dry, often foggy | Dry, foggy, hoarfrost possible |

November loses to the two later months on how "sure" the clouds are and on tớ dày flowers, but wins in every other row. First-time visitors, people traveling with their parents and anyone who dislikes crowds should start with this month. The guide to the following month is in our article [Tà Xùa in December](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-12); for the month before, see [Tà Xùa in October](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-10).

## How cold is Tà Xùa in November, and what are the clouds like?

**Temperature.** Daytime is 10–18°C with dry sunshine, so sitting outside at midday is quite pleasant. Nights and early mornings are much colder, sometimes below 10°C. The wind on high outcrops such as Đỉnh Gió makes it feel colder than the thermometer says.

**Rain.** The weather is dry with little rain. Skin and lips dry out fast, so bring lip balm and moisturizer.

**Clouds.** Cold air arrives, early mornings are humid, and clouds start to gather thickly in the valley. Clouds are most likely on a day when light rain or high humidity the night before is followed by a sunny morning. When the monsoon wind blows hard or the weather has been dry and sunny for days, the clouds usually do not rise.

**Timing.** Getting to the viewpoint around 5:30–6:00 is right, as the sun rises at about 6:00. It gets dark fast in the afternoon, and by around 17:30 it is already dusk.

For the cheapest room at these 5 homestays, Saturday night costs 100.000đ–300.000đ more than a weekday. If you can take one day off midweek, Monday to Thursday nights are both cheaper and quieter, and early check-in is easier to get.

Two things to note when choosing a room in November:

- **Choose a room with heating.** On a night below 10°C, a room with only blankets makes it hard to sleep. Dê Homestay has heating in all 8 rooms, and Tà Xùa Hill has air conditioning and heating in all 21 rooms. See also our article on [Tà Xùa homestays with air conditioning and heated mattresses](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-co-dieu-hoa-dem-suoi).
- **Book Saturday night at least 1 week ahead.** There are no holidays yet, but weekends are still busy because it is the start of the cloud season; rooms with good views and rooms with bathtubs go first.

To watch the clouds right from your bed, see our list of [Tà Xùa homestays with a sea of clouds view](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-view-may-dep).

## How much does a November trip cost?

An example for 2 people, traveling midweek, 2 days 1 night, based on current listed prices:

- **Return sleeper cabin bus ticket:** from 500.000đ per person.
- **Room:** one room at 500.000đ per night, split in two is 250.000đ per person.
- **Discount when booking a [bus and room combo](/combo):** 25.000đ per guest for most rooms.
- **Bus and room:** about 725.000đ per person.
- **Motorbike taxi for cloud hunting:** from the center to Đỉnh Gió is 150.000đ one way for 2 passengers; the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock), Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) route is 350.000đ–400.000đ one way for 2 passengers.
- **Entrance tickets:** Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) and Mỏm cá heo are about 20.000đ per person; Tà Xùa Farm is 40.000đ.

Staying one more midweek night adds 250.000đ per person for the room, and in return you get one more morning of cloud hunting. A full breakdown of food and transport costs is in our article on [the cost of an independent trip to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/chi-phi-du-lich-ta-xua-tu-tuc).

## What should you wear in November?

November is not yet as bitterly cold as January, but the gap between midday and early morning is very large. Dress in layers:

- A thermal top underneath, a sweater or fleece in the middle, and a windbreaker or light down jacket on the outside.
- Thick long trousers, socks, and closed-toe shoes with grippy soles.
- A wool hat, scarf and gloves for cloud hunting mornings and for riding on a motorbike.

You can take layers off when the midday sun comes out, so avoid wearing one thick down jacket with only a T-shirt underneath. The detailed list is in our article [What to wear in Tà Xùa in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi).

## What to do on a November evening in Tà Xùa?

Evening comes early and cold, so the options all revolve around something hot.

- **Hotpot and grilled food.** [Trạm Mây Tà Xùa](/nha-hang/tram-may-ta-xua) in Khe Cải has set menus from 249.000đ. A list of places to eat by area is in our article [Where to eat in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/quan-an-nha-hang-ta-xua).
- **Coffee and hot cocoa.** A few cafes in Đỉnh Gió serve light food and are good for sitting a long time, for example [Mây Bay Coffee](/cafe/may-bay-coffee).
- **Stargazing.** The November sky is dry with few clouds at night; on a clear night it is worth turning off the lights and stepping out into the yard.

## Suggested 3 days 2 nights itinerary for early cloud season

- **Departure night:** the sleeper cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 and reaches the center around 6:00.
- **Day 1:** if there are clouds, head to Đỉnh Gió right away (about 2km from the center). Have breakfast, leave your bags, and check in at noon. Watch the sunset in the afternoon, then have hotpot.
- **Day 2:** at 5:30 take the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn route. In the afternoon, stop by Tà Xùa Farm or have tea in the center.
- **Day 3:** go to Sống lưng khủng long in the morning (6:00–10:00, only when it is dry and not very windy). Board the bus at 12:00 and reach Hanoi around 19:00.

The full hour-by-hour version is in our article [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem). If you can only go on a weekend, follow the [2 days 1 night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem). Because the weather is dry, November is the easiest time in the cloud season to ride a motorbike yourself; manual motorbikes [rented in the center](/thue-xe-may) start from 180.000đ per day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in November?

Yes. November is the first month of the main cloud season, which lasts until March. Clouds still depend on each day's weather, so stay 2 nights to get two mornings.

### How cold is Tà Xùa in November?

Daytime is 10–18°C with dry sunshine. Nights and early mornings are cold, sometimes below 10°C, and on high, windy outcrops it feels even colder.

### What flowers bloom in Tà Xùa in November?

In November, dã quỳ bloom yellow along the roads and the grass on the Grasslands is still golden. Tớ dày flowers do not start blooming until late December.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded in November?

Weekdays are fairly quiet. Weekends are busy because it is the start of the cloud season, but not at New Year levels because November has no holidays.

### Do Tà Xùa room prices go up in November?

There are no holiday rates. Prices only change by day of the week: at the 5 homestays in this article, the cheapest room on Saturday night costs 100.000đ–300.000đ more than on a weekday.

### Should you visit Tà Xùa in November or December?

November is less cold, has no holiday-rate nights and is quieter. December has thicker clouds and tớ dày flowers late in the month, but it is cold and crowded around New Year.
