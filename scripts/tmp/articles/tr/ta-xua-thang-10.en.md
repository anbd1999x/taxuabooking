TITLE: Tà Xùa in October: clouds return, is the rice still golden?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in October turns cooler, clouds may begin and Xím Vàng rice lasts into early month. No holiday rates. First vs second half and 5 homestay prices.

Tà Xùa in October is a month of changing seasons: the rice in Xím Vàng stays golden until early in the month, the air turns steadily cooler and the first cloudy mornings may appear. No night in October 2027 is charged holiday rates. **Go in the first half of the month if you want to catch the rice, and in the second half if you want to try your luck with the clouds before peak season begins.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** may begin on humid mornings, not yet regular. The main cloud season is November to March.
- **Rice:** Xím Vàng ripens until early October; after that the fields are harvested.
- **Weather:** cool, getting chillier toward the end of the month. You need a jacket in the early morning and evening.
- **Táo mèo (wild hawthorn):** October is the end of the fruit season.
- **Prices:** only weekday and weekend rates. The five Saturday nights are 02/10, 09/10, 16/10, 23/10 and 30/10.
- **Crowds:** building toward the end of the month, busier on weekends than on weekdays.

## Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in October yet?

Possibly, but the season has not started. A sea of clouds in Tà Xùa is most common from November to March; October is when clouds begin to reappear as the air turns chilly and early mornings get more humid.

That leads to three tips:

- **Stay 2 nights.** October clouds are not regular yet, and one morning is too few. Two nights give you two tries, three nights give you three.
- **Watch the sky close to your dates.** The kind of day when clouds are likely: light rain or high humidity the night before, then a clearing sky the next morning.
- **Choose a room that looks out over the valley.** On a day the clouds come in, you know as soon as you open the curtains.

When to head out, which way to look and how to read the forecast are in our [Tà Xùa cloud hunting guide](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua). If clouds are the only goal of your trip and you can move your dates, [Tà Xùa in November](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-11) is the safer choice.

## First half or second half of October?

01/10/2027 is a Friday. The two halves of the month make for two different kinds of trip; the rice and cloud milestones are what is typical and shift from year to year.

| Criteria | 01–15/10 | 16–31/10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xím Vàng rice | Still golden into early month, harvest under way | Harvested, stubble left |
| Clouds | May begin, still sparse | Chances rise as it gets colder |
| Weather | Cool | Chilly, nights noticeably colder |
| Saturday nights | 02/10, 09/10 | 16/10, 23/10, 30/10 |
| Crowds | Moderate | Building, busy on weekends |
| Best for | Travelers who want to catch the rice, with a chance of clouds added | Travelers going for clouds who want to avoid the November to January peak |

If you go in the first half for the rice, ask your homestay how the harvest is going before you commit. ## What to wear in October as it turns chilly?

October is not as cold as December, but early mornings at the cloud hunting spots and the evenings are already much colder than in summer.

- **Daytime:** a long-sleeved top or T-shirt, plus a light jacket.
- **Early morning and evening:** a sweater or fleece and a windbreaker. Late in the month, bring a wool hat and a light scarf.
- **Shoes:** grippy soles for the dirt paths at Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine).
- **Room:** if you feel the cold easily, choose a room with a heated mattress from the second half of the month; the list is in our article on [Tà Xùa homestays with air conditioning and heated mattresses](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-co-dieu-hoa-dem-suoi).

## Where to go in October?

The weather is getting drier, so the walking spots are more pleasant than in the rainy season.

- **Sống lưng khủng long:** the best time is 6:00–10:00, and tickets are about 20.000đ per person. Only go when it is dry and not very windy. A motorbike taxi from the center costs 300.000đ–350.000đ one way for 1 passenger.
- **The Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree):** three spots on one route; a motorbike taxi from the center costs 250.000đ–300.000đ one way for 1 passenger. The grass on the Grasslands turns golden this season.
- **Đỉnh Gió:** about 2km from the center, no ticket, and the quickest place to check for clouds each morning.
- **Tà Xùa Farm:** tickets 40.000đ per person, good for the afternoon.
- **Xím Vàng:** only worth the trip if there is still rice; about 15km, roughly 40 minutes by motorbike.

For the most convenient order and the location of each spot, see our [Tà Xùa travel map](/kinh-nghiem/ban-do-du-lich-ta-xua-diem-check-in). A comparison of the three cloud hunting routes is in our article [Which cloud hunting route to take in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/san-may-ta-xua-cung-nao).

## What to do on October evenings?

It gets dark earlier and starts to get cold, which suits activities around a fire.

- **Hotpot and grilled food.** See places by area on the [Tà Xùa restaurants](/nha-hang) page.
- **Camping.** The Tà Xùa Grasslands have tent rental for about 200.000đ. Ask ahead about blankets, wind and weather; tips are in our article on [camping in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/cam-trai-ta-xua-don-binh-minh).
- **Stargazing.** A cloudless night is the time to switch off the lights and step outside; see our article on [stargazing at night in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ngam-sao-dem-ta-xua).
- **Evening coffee.** Some cafes in the center stay open late, for example [Nhà H'Mông](/cafe/nha-h-mong), open until 23:00.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in October?

Possibly. October is when clouds begin to appear on humid mornings, but not as regularly as in the main cloud season from November to March. Stay 2 nights to get two mornings.

### Is there still ripe rice in Xím Vàng in October?

The rice in Xím Vàng usually stays golden until early October, after which locals harvest it bit by bit. From mid-month on, most fields have been harvested, so ask your homestay before you go.

### Is Tà Xùa cold in October?

October is cool and gets steadily chillier, but not as cold as December and January. You need a jacket in the early morning, in the evening and on windy outcrops.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded in October?

Crowds build toward the end of the month as the clouds start to come in. Weekdays are still quiet; for weekends, book your room 1–2 weeks ahead.

### Are any days in October charged holiday rates?

No. The holiday price calendar on Tà Xùa Booking covers 30/4 to 1/5, National Day, New Year and Tết (Lunar New Year). October only has weekday and weekend rates.

### Should I visit Tà Xùa in October or November?

October is quieter and may still have rice early in the month; November is colder and more likely to have clouds. Go in November for clouds, and in October to avoid the crowds.
