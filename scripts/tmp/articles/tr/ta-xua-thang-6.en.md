TITLE: Tà Xùa in June: escape the heat at 20–26°C with family
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in June is 20–26°C while the lowlands hit 36–38°C: afternoon rain, few clouds, no holiday rates. Homestays with a pool or AC and a 3-day plan.

Tà Xùa in June is a cool 20–26°C during the day and 17–20°C at night, right when the lowlands are going through hot days of 36–38°C. There is no holiday on the price calendar all month, visitors are few, and good rooms are easy to book. **June suits families and groups of friends heading up the mountain to escape the heat for 2–3 days best; if you are going only for the sea of clouds, wait until November.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Temperature:** days 20–26°C, nights 17–20°C. Most rooms do not need the air conditioning on, but you should still have a light jacket for the evening.
- **Rain:** June is in the middle of the rainy season, with a lot of rain and slippery dirt roads. Rain tends to fall in the afternoon and at night.
- **Clouds:** few, mostly wisps of cloud drifting through the valleys after a night of rain. Do not set your hopes on a sea of clouds.
- **Prices:** no holiday-rate nights. The four Saturday nights of June 2027 are 05/06, 12/06, 19/06 and 26/06.
- **Transport:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person, leaving at 21:00–22:00 and arriving around 6:00.
- **Note:** do not travel long distances after 16:30 because of fog and afternoon rain.

## How cool is Tà Xùa in June?

Central Tà Xùa sits at an altitude of about 1.500m. The difference in altitude makes the temperature here more than 10°C lower than in the lowlands on hot days.

| | Tà Xùa in June | Hanoi on a hot day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daytime | 20–26°C | 36–38°C |
| Night | 17–20°C | Muggy, hard to sleep without air conditioning |
| Rain | Frequent, in showers in the afternoon and at night | Thunderstorms mixed with harsh sun |
| Clothing | Short sleeves during the day, a light jacket in the evening | Summer clothes |

The mountain sun is still strong even though the air is cool, so sunscreen and a hat are must-haves. In the early morning after a night of rain, some days feel as crisp as autumn in the lowlands.

## Can you go cloud hunting in June?

You may get lucky, but you should not plan around it. Tà Xùa's thick sea of clouds is a cold-season sight, from November to March. In June clouds appear only now and then, usually as bands drifting through the mountain gaps in the early morning after a night of rain.

How to travel in tune with the season:

- Treat cooling off and enjoying the green mountains as the main goal; seeing clouds is a bonus.
- Get up at 5:00 and look at the sky. If there are clouds, head to a nearby spot like Đỉnh Gió; if not, go back to sleep.
- Choose a room with a balcony or glass walls, so you still have a view to enjoy on a rainy day.

## What should you plan for when taking young children to Tà Xùa over the summer break?

Students are already on summer break in June, so many families choose this time. A few things to settle in advance:

- **Bus:** the trip from Hanoi up to Tà Xùa takes about 8 hours, with 15km of mountain pass on the final stretch. A double cabin lets a parent lie next to the child; how to choose a berth is in our article on [visiting Tà Xùa with young children](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cung-gia-dinh-tre-nho).
- **Motion sickness:** prepare ahead with our [tips for avoiding motion sickness on the Tà Xùa pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua).
- **Room:** prioritize a homestay in the center or one with car access right to the door, with a play area, and with a kitchen or meals cooked on request.
- **Places to go:** Đỉnh Gió, Núi đôi thiếu nữ (Twin Peaks) and Hồ Mắt Ngọc (Jade Eye Lake) are short walks. Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) and Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) have no railings and are not suitable for young children, especially when the path is wet.
- **Getting around on the mountain:** a family of 4–6 should hire a [local taxi](/taxi) instead of carrying children by motorbike on slippery roads; a full-day hire costs 1.700.000đ–2.000.000đ per car.

## A relaxed 3 days 2 nights itinerary

This plan leaves plenty of time to rest and packs the travelling into the mornings.

- **Departure night:** board the sleeper cabin bus at 21:00–22:00 and sleep on the bus.
- **Day 1:** arrive around 6:00, have breakfast in the center and leave your bags. In the morning stroll around Bụa A village or sit in a café; check in at noon and catch up on sleep. Spend the afternoon in the homestay grounds and have hotpot in the evening.
- **Day 2:** look at the sky at 5:00. From 7:00–11:00 take the Grasslands and Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) route by motorbike taxi or taxi. Be back before 16:30 in the afternoon, drink Shan tuyết tea, and let the children try beeswax drawing at the indigo dyeing workshop.
- **Day 3:** in the morning visit Đỉnh Gió and Núi đôi thiếu nữ (ticket 30.000đ if you walk in), board the bus at 12:00, and reach Hanoi around 19:00.

For the 2-night version with full timings, see our [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).

June is almost certain to bring at least one rainy spell. A few things you can do indoors or under a porch roof:

- Sit in a covered café looking out over the valley; the list is on the [Tà Xùa cafés](/cafe) page.
- Have hotpot and grilled food in the evening. Suggested places by area are in our article on [where to eat in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/quan-an-nha-hang-ta-xua).

## June risks to know in advance

- **Slippery roads.** The dirt side roads get muddy after rain. If you are not a confident rider, call a [local motorbike taxi](/xe-om).
- **Afternoon fog.** Visibility sometimes drops to a few dozen meters, so you should not ride down the pass in the late afternoon or evening.
- **Thunderstorms and local power cuts.** Ask whether the homestay has rechargeable lamps or a generator, and bring a power bank.
- **Leeches and insects** on trails in the damp forest: wear long trousers and high-top shoes, and bring repellent spray.
- **Places closed on weekdays.** In the quiet season, some eateries close midweek; call ahead before riding far.

General safety notes are gathered in our article [Is it dangerous to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-co-nguy-hiem-khong).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa hot in June?

No. Tà Xùa in June is 20–26°C during the day and 17–20°C at night. The midday sun is strong but the air is cool, and you need a light jacket in the evening.

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in June?

Yes, but only now and then, usually as wisps of cloud after a night of rain. June suits a trip to cool off, not a trip made only to hunt for a sea of clouds.

### Does it rain a lot in Tà Xùa in June?

Yes, a lot. June is in the middle of the rainy season, rain usually comes in showers in the afternoon and at night, and the dirt roads are slippery. Plan your travelling for the mornings.

### Are Tà Xùa room prices cheaper in June?

Listed prices change little by season. The advantage of June is that there are no holiday-rate nights, weekday nights are easy to pick, and plenty of good rooms are still free.

### Which Tà Xùa homestay has a swimming pool?

On the Tà Xùa Booking system, Chillout Village in Khe Cải has an outdoor pool. Its weekday listed price is 650.000đ–1.950.000đ per room per night.

### Should you take young children to Tà Xùa in June?

Yes, because the weather is cool and it is the summer break. Choose a room in the center, get around by taxi and avoid the rock outcrops without railings on rainy days.
