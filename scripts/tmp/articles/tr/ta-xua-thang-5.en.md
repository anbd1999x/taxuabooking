TITLE: Tà Xùa in May: early rainy season, few crowds, worth it?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in May: mild 20–26°C, rain in the afternoon and at night, few clouds. Holiday rates on 01–02/05, normal from 03/05. Rooms from 200.000đ.

Tà Xùa in May opens the rainy season and the quiet season: daytime is 20–26°C, nights 17–20°C, and rain mostly comes in the afternoon and at night. Is Tà Xùa worth visiting in May? **Yes, if you are going to escape the heat and rest; no, if your only goal is a thick sea of clouds.** The first two nights of the month, 01/05 and 02/05, still belong to the 30/4–1/5 holiday and are charged holiday rates.

**Quick summary:**

- **Temperature:** days 20–26°C, nights 17–20°C. Sitting outside in the evening calls for a light jacket.
- **Rain:** more than in the previous months, usually in the afternoon and at night; the next morning is often dry.
- **Clouds:** few and hard to predict. Usually it is cloud threading through the mountain gaps after a rainy night.
- **Holiday rates:** the nights of 01/05 and 02/05. From the night of 03/05/2027 prices go back to the day-of-week rate.
- **Crowds:** after the holiday comes the quietest stretch since the start of the year; good rooms are easier to get than in cloud season.
- **Roads:** the dirt roads up to Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Hồ Mắt Ngọc (Jade Eye Lake) are slippery after rain. Do not go to Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) in rain or strong wind.

## Early May: which days does the 30/4–1/5 holiday still affect?

In 2027, 01/05 is a Saturday and 02/05 is a Sunday. The holiday price calendar on Tà Xùa Booking runs from the night of 29/04 through the night of 02/05, so the first two nights of May are still at the highest rate.

- **Nights of 01/05 and 02/05:** holiday rates, crowded photo spots.
- **From the night of 03/05:** weekday rates. This is when the holiday guests have left and the mountains are quiet again.
- **The remaining Saturday nights:** 08/05, 15/05, 22/05 and 29/05, charged weekend rates.

If you have to travel right on the holiday, the cost-saving tips are in our article [Tà Xùa in April](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-4). If you can be flexible, moving to 03/05 or later is the simplest way to get both lower prices and fewer people.

## May weather: how does a day unfold?

Rain in this season rarely lasts all day. It comes in bursts, with fog that rolls up very fast. The table below shows the usual rhythm of a day early in the rainy season. Use it for planning, not as a forecast.

| Time | Typical sky | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5:00–8:00 | Cool, slightly chilly; after a rainy night there may be cloud threading through | Get up and check the sky; if there are clouds, go to Đỉnh Gió or a hill near your room |
| 8:00–11:00 | Dry, sunny, long views | Visit the far spots: the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) |
| 11:00–14:00 | Harsh sun even though the air is cool | Have lunch, rest in your room, drink tea |
| 14:00–16:30 | Clouds build up, rain may start | Spots near the center, cafes with a roof |
| After 16:30 | Showers, thick fog, visibility down to a few dozen meters | No long rides; have dinner and rest |

Two practical consequences: do anything far away in the morning, and always have an indoor plan for the afternoon. Ideas for bad-weather days are in our article on [what to do when cloud hunting fails](/kinh-nghiem/san-may-hut-o-ta-xua-lam-gi).

## Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in May?

Yes, but do not count on them. The chance of a flat, thick sea of clouds like in winter is much lower. May clouds usually come in two kinds:

- **Threading clouds:** bands of cloud flowing through the mountain gaps in the early morning, often seen after a rainy night followed by a clearing sky.
- **Cumulus clouds:** tall white clouds billowing against a blue sky towards midday, which stand out very clearly in photos.

In return, on clear days after rain you can see very far and make out every layer of mountains. A rainbow after an afternoon shower is also a scene of this season. Our article [Tà Xùa in summer, May to August](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-he-thang-5-8) goes deeper into summer clouds.

## What is there to see in May besides clouds?

- **Mountains and fields turn green again.** With the rain back, the grassy hills of the Grasslands and the tea fields turn a fresh green.
- **Rice terraces in the watering season.** Xím Vàng commune is about 15km from the center, around 40 minutes by motorbike. May and June are when locals let water into the terraces; the exact timing each year depends on the rain, so ask your homestay before riding over.
- **No waiting at photo spots.** In winter you sometimes have to queue for photos at Cây cô đơn and Mỏm cá heo; in May there are usually only a few groups.
- **Sunset after rain.** When the sky clears in the late afternoon, the colors are often very deep. Pick your seat using our guide to [cloud-view cafes by area](/kinh-nghiem/quan-ca-phe-san-may-ta-xua-theo-khu).

Sống lưng khủng long is still open, with tickets at about 20.000đ per person, but you should only go on a dry morning. The route and the sections that need care are in our article [Sống lưng khủng long in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/song-lung-khung-long-ta-xua).

## Is getting around difficult in May?

The main paved and concrete roads are fine as usual. The places that need care are the dirt side roads and the mountain pass sections after rain.

- **Sleeper cabin bus:** still runs every night from Hanoi, with return tickets at 500.000đ–650.000đ per person. What the ride is like is in our article [a night on the sleeper cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/trai-nghiem-xe-cabin-di-ta-xua).
- **Self-ride motorbike:** a manual motorbike costs 180.000đ per day. Only ride yourself if you are used to steep passes and wet roads.
- **Local motorbike taxi:** from the center to the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn route costs 250.000đ–300.000đ one way for 1 passenger; a full day costs 500.000đ–600.000đ for 1 passenger.
- **Taxi:** a full-day hire costs 1.700.000đ–2.000.000đ per car, good for groups on rainy days.

The price list for each leg is in our article on [Tà Xùa motorbike taxi and taxi prices](/kinh-nghiem/gia-xe-om-taxi-ta-xua).

## What to bring in May?

- A two-piece rain suit and a waterproof pouch for your phone and camera.
- Shoes with good grip and a pair of sandals for walking around the homestay in the rain.
- A light jacket and long sleeves for evenings and early mornings.
- Sunscreen and mosquito repellent.
- A power bank, in case of a local power cut after a thunderstorm.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa beautiful in May?

It is beautiful in a green-season way: the mountains and tea fields turn green again, the sky is clear after rain, and there are few visitors. A thick sea of clouds is rare, so May suits a cool getaway more than cloud hunting.

### Does it rain a lot in Tà Xùa in May?

May is the start of the rainy season; it rains more than in the previous months, usually in the afternoon and at night. Mornings are mostly dry, so plan the far spots for the morning.

### How warm is Tà Xùa in May?

20–26°C during the day and 17–20°C at night. You still need a light jacket in the early morning and evening.

### Is the night of 01/05 in Tà Xùa charged holiday rates?

Yes. On Tà Xùa Booking, the nights of 01/05 and 02/05 are on the 30/4–1/5 holiday price calendar. From the night of 03/05 prices return to the day-of-week rate.

### Should you go to Sống lưng khủng long in May?

Only go on a dry morning without strong wind. After rain the dirt path is slippery, the ridge is narrow and there are drops on both sides, so postpone when the weather is bad.

### Do you need to book Tà Xùa rooms early in May?

Apart from the two holiday nights at the start of the month, May is the quiet season, so booking a few days ahead usually still gets you a room. For weekends you should still reserve earlier if you want a room with a bathtub or glass walls.
