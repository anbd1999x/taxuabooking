TITLE: Tà Xùa in April: are there still clouds, and 30/4 room prices?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in April: warm 15–22°C, thinner clouds, green hills. Nights of 29–30/04 cost 345.000đ–600.000đ more per room than weekdays. Week-by-week plan.

Tà Xùa in April is a month between seasons: the main cloud season has just ended in March, the weather is warm at around 15–22°C during the day, and clouds only show up on some mornings. Should you go in April? **Go between 01–28/04 if you like warm weather, green mountains and quiet photo spots; think carefully about the two nights of 29/04 and 30/04, because holiday rates already apply.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** still possible, but thinner than in winter. In April, treat clouds as a bonus, not the only goal.
- **Weather:** around 15–22°C during the day, chilly in the early morning. The end of the month is when it gradually shifts into the rainy season of May.
- **Scenery:** wild flowers, the last field mustard flowers, and grass hills and tea fields turning green again.
- **30/4 holiday rates:** the holiday price calendar on Tà Xùa Booking runs from the night of 29/04 through the night of 02/05. In 2027, 30/04 falls on a Friday.
- **Four Saturday nights:** 03/04, 10/04, 17/04 and 24/04.
- **Transport:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person; for the holiday, book tickets early.

## Are there still clouds in Tà Xùa in April?

Yes, but fewer. Clouds in Tà Xùa are most common from November to March and can linger into April. Once the weather has warmed up, a thick sea of clouds filling the valley is no longer an every-morning sight. Instead you are more likely to see thin clouds drifting across the mountainside, or clouds rising quickly after a night of rain.

Three ways to avoid disappointment:

- **Set the right expectations.** April suits people who come to rest and to walk and look at the mountains. If you are only coming for the sea of clouds, choose November to March; a month-by-month comparison table is in our article [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).
- **Stay 2 nights and check the forecast close to the date.** The morning after night rain, when the sky clears and the sun comes out, is the one most worth getting up early for.
- **Have a plan for a morning without clouds.** A list of things to do is in our article [what to do when cloud hunting fails](/kinh-nghiem/san-may-hut-o-ta-xua-lam-gi).

The month before is still part of the main cloud season; see our article [Tà Xùa in March](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-thang-3) if you can move your trip earlier.

01/04/2027 is a Thursday. Hùng Kings' Commemoration Day (the 10th day of the 3rd lunar month) falls in mid-April; at the time of writing this day is not yet on the system's holiday price calendar, so check the price shown when you pick your dates.

## How to spend less on a 30/4 trip?

The 30/4 holiday falls outside the main cloud season and is also the busiest and most expensive time of the month. If you still go, these tips help cut costs:

- **Go before the holiday.** Board the bus on the evening of 25/04 or 26/04, stay the nights of 26–28/04 at weekday rates, and head back before the holiday crowds come up.
- **Go after the holiday.** The holiday price calendar ends after the night of 02/05. From the night of 03/05, prices go back to the day-of-week rate.
- **Lock in your room early.** Rooms with a bathtub and glass rooms go first. Book 3–4 weeks ahead.
- **Book bus tickets at the same time as the room.** Book a [sleeper cabin bus and homestay combo](/combo) so the dates match and you do not end up with a room but no bus.
- **If you drive yourself, plan your parking.** The road up is 15km of steep mountain pass and traffic is heavy over the holiday; read our article on [driving to Tà Xùa by car](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai) first.

Families should also see the section on choosing rooms and transport in our article on [visiting Tà Xùa with young children](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cung-gia-dinh-tre-nho).

## What to do in Tà Xùa in April besides cloud hunting?

The weather is warm and the days are longer than in winter, so April suits activities that need time outdoors.

- **Walk at Núi đôi thiếu nữ (Maiden Twin Hills).** A grass hill in the Đỉnh Gió area; tickets are 30.000đ if you walk in and 40.000đ if you go by motorbike taxi.
- **Drink Shan tuyết tea.** In April the tea fields turn green again. How to choose and buy tea is in our article on [Tà Xùa specialties to bring home as gifts](/kinh-nghiem/qua-ta-xua-mua-gi-lam-qua).
- **Try indigo dyeing and beeswax drawing** at the workshop in the center, good for midday or a rainy day.
- **Watch the sunset** at Khe Cải or Đỉnh Gió, then have dinner; restaurant suggestions are in our article on [what to eat in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/an-gi-o-ta-xua).
- **Hồ Mắt Ngọc (Jade Eye Lake)** in Mống Vàng: open 7:00–17:00, no entrance fee; a motorbike taxi from the center costs 150.000đ one way for 1 passenger.

## What to wear in April and how to get around?

- **Clothing:** a thin long-sleeved top for the daytime and a windbreaker for early mornings and evenings. You do not need thick thermal wear like in January.
- **Rain gear:** late in the month you may get the first showers of the season, so bring a two-piece rain suit if you ride a motorbike.
- **Shoes:** soles with good grip. The dirt road up to Mỏm cá heo is slippery after rain.
- **Transport on the mountain:** a manual motorbike rents for 180.000đ a day and a PG1 for 280.000đ a day at the [motorbike rental](/thue-xe-may) shops. If you are not used to steep mountain passes, call a [local motorbike taxi](/xe-om).

A midweek 2 days 1 night outline for April: board the sleeper cabin bus in Hanoi the evening before; on the morning of day 1 arrive around 6:00, go out to Đỉnh Gió and then have breakfast, check in at noon, and in the afternoon visit Hồ Mắt Ngọc or Núi đôi thiếu nữ; on the morning of day 2 take the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) route, board the bus at 12:00 and reach Hanoi around 19:00. Timings for each leg are in our article [Tà Xùa 2 days 1 night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in April?

There may be, but they are thinner than in winter. The main cloud season ends in March, and in April clouds usually appear on mornings after a night of rain.

### What is the weather like in Tà Xùa in April?

Around 15–22°C during the day, warm and pleasant, and still chilly in the early morning. The end of the month starts to shift into the rainy season, so you may get showers.

### Which nights do Tà Xùa homestays charge holiday rates over 30/4?

The holiday price calendar on Tà Xùa Booking covers 4 nights: 29/04, 30/04, 01/05 and 02/05. From the night of 03/05, prices go back to the day-of-week rate.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded over 30/4?

Yes. It is one of the four busiest periods of the year, along with 2/9, New Year and Tết (Lunar New Year). You have to wait your turn for photos at the check-in spots, and the best rooms sell out early.

### What flowers bloom in Tà Xùa in April?

April has wild flowers and the last field mustard flowers on the hillside fields. The big flower seasons such as tớ dày, plum and peach have already passed in January and February.

### Are holiday rates charged in Tà Xùa on Hùng Kings' Commemoration Day?

At the time of writing, the system's holiday price calendar does not include Hùng Kings' Commemoration Day. The price shown when you pick your dates on the homestay page is the price that applies to that night.
