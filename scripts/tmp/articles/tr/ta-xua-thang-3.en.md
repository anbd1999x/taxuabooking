TITLE: Tà Xùa in March: late cloud season, warmer days, still clouds?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in March warms to 15–22°C, still in the main cloud season, no holiday-rate nights. 4-month table, tickets for 6 spots, cloud-view rooms from 650.000đ.

Tà Xùa in March is the last month of the main cloud season. Days warm up to around 15–22°C, and no night in the whole month is charged holiday rates. For anyone who dreads the cold but still wants to go cloud hunting, **March is the easiest month of the cloud season**: less cold than January and February, less crowded than Tết, and room prices come in just two levels, weekday and weekend.

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** still in the main cloud season (November to March), but at the tail end. Clouds depend on the weather each day.
- **Temperature:** around 15–22°C during the day. Early mornings at the cloud hunting spots are still cold, so bring a windproof jacket.
- **Flowers:** forest flowers under the canopy of ancient tea trees, and the last mustard flowers left on the hillside fields.
- **Room prices:** March 2027 has no holidays on the price calendar. The four Saturday nights are 06/03, 13/03, 20/03 and 27/03.
- **Entrance tickets:** Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) and Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) cost about 20.000đ per person; Đỉnh Gió and Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) are free.
- **Sleeper cabin bus:** a return ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person, leaving Hanoi at 21:00–22:00.

## Are there still clouds in Tà Xùa in March?

Yes, and in many years they are still beautiful. Tà Xùa's main cloud season runs from November to March, and clouds can show up as late as April. What changes is that the weather warms up and cold fronts become less frequent, so mornings with a thick sea of clouds are less regular than in mid-winter.

How to improve your odds:

- **Stay 2 nights.** Two mornings mean two tries. A sample plan is in our article [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem).
- **Watch for the day after rain.** Light rain or high humidity the night before, then a clearing sky the next morning, is the kind of day when clouds are likely.
- **Be at the spot by 5:30.** Clouds are usually thickest around 5:30–7:30 and thin out as the sun climbs.
- **Choose a room facing the valley.** On a day when the clouds roll in, you see them right from your bed without riding anywhere.

## What to wear and bring in March?

March clothing is lighter than mid-winter, but you cannot leave the warm layers at home yet.

- A windbreaker or a light down jacket for cloud hunting mornings and for the evenings.
- A sweater or fleece underneath; take it off in the midday sun and you still have a thin layer.
- Shoes with grippy soles for the dirt paths at Mỏm cá heo and Sống lưng khủng long.
- A light raincoat in case of drizzle and damp morning mist.
- Sunscreen: the highland sun is harsh even when the air is cool.

If you feel the cold easily, choose a room with a heated mattress to be safe, because nights on the mountain are still much colder than in the lowlands.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you go cloud hunting in Tà Xùa in March?

It is possible. March is the last month of the main cloud season; clouds still appear, but not as regularly as in mid-winter. Stay 2 nights and watch for a clearing day after rain to improve your odds.

### Is Tà Xùa cold in March?

Daytime in March is around 15–22°C, more comfortable than January and February. Early mornings on the high outcrops are still cold and windy, so you need a windproof jacket.

### What flowers bloom in Tà Xùa in March?

March no longer has a big flower season like tớ dày or plum blossom. Instead there are forest flowers under the canopy of ancient tea trees and the last mustard flowers left on the hillside fields.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded in March?

March 2027 has no holidays on the price calendar, so weekdays are fairly quiet. The weekends of 06–07/03, 13–14/03, 20–21/03 and 27–28/03 are busier because it is still cloud season.

### Can you visit Sống lưng khủng long in March?

Yes, if it is dry and not very windy. The best time is 6:00–10:00, and the ticket is about 20.000đ per person. On days with rain, thick fog or strong wind, you should postpone.

### How far ahead should you book a room for March?

For weekends, book 1–2 weeks ahead because it is still cloud season. On weekdays rooms are usually easier to find, and booking a few days ahead still leaves plenty of choice.
