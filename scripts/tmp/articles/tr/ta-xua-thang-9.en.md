TITLE: Tà Xùa in September: Xím Vàng rice harvest, 2/9 room prices
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in September is ripe rice season in Xím Vàng, 15km from the center. Holiday rates on 01–02/09. Week-by-week calendar, transport and room prices.

Tà Xùa in September is ripe rice season. The rice terraces in Xím Vàng commune, about 15km from central Tà Xùa, turn golden from September to early October and are usually at their best from mid-September. The cloud season has not started yet this month, so **a September trip to Tà Xùa is for the rice and the cool weather, not for a sea of clouds**. The two nights of 01/09 and 02/09 are charged National Day holiday rates; from the night of 03/09/2027 room prices go back to the day-of-week rates.

**Quick summary:**

- **Rice:** Xím Vàng ripens from September to early October. After the harvest only stubble is left in the fields, so ask how the rice is doing before you go.
- **Road to Xím Vàng:** about 15km along provincial road 112, past Đỉnh Gió, roughly 40 minutes by motorbike.
- **Clouds:** not in season yet. Clouds may start to appear from October.
- **Weather:** cool, sitting between the 20–26°C daytime of summer and the 10–18°C of November.
- **Holiday rates:** the National Day holiday price calendar covers the nights of 31/08, 01/09 and 02/09. 02/09/2027 is a Thursday.
- **Four Saturday nights:** 04/09, 11/09, 18/09 and 25/09.

## Where to see the rice in Tà Xùa in September?

In Xím Vàng. The central Tà Xùa area is mostly tea hills, grassy hills and village houses; the wide rice terraces are in Xím Vàng commune, behind Đỉnh Gió.

- **Distance:** about 15km from the center, along provincial road 112 past Đỉnh Gió, roughly 40 minutes by motorbike.
- **Main spot:** the Mâm Xôi hill of Xím Vàng, a round hill of stacked terraces in the middle of the valley. Along the road there are many bends that look down into the terraced valleys.
- **Best time of day:** the afternoon, around 15:00–17:00, when the slanting sun makes the rice color deeper.
- **Where to stay:** services in Xím Vàng are still limited. Most visitors sleep in Tà Xùa and make it a day trip.
- **Road:** some sections are steep and difficult. If you are not a confident rider, hire a motorbike taxi or a taxi.

The route and the stops have their own article: [Xím Vàng in ripe rice season](/kinh-nghiem/xim-vang-mua-lua-chin). Also in September, the grass on the Tà Xùa grasslands starts to turn golden, which suits late-afternoon photos.

## Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in September?

Not in season yet. The main cloud season starts in November; clouds may show up early on the humid mornings of October. In September there is still the occasional morning when clouds rise after rain in the night, but not regularly enough to plan around.

So the order of priorities for September should be:

1. One afternoon in Xím Vàng.
2. One morning at the Grasslands, Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree) and Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) when it is dry.
3. The remaining morning at Đỉnh Gió, where clouds are a bonus if they come.

If you are set on seeing a sea of clouds, the 12-month table in our article [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat) shows you should push the trip back to November.

## A 3 days 2 nights rice season itinerary

Stay 2 nights so you have a full afternoon for Xím Vàng without rushing.

- **Departure night:** the sleeper cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 21:00–22:00.
- **Day 1:** arrive in the center around 6:00. Have breakfast, go to Đỉnh Gió, leave your bags. Check in at noon and rest. In the afternoon, stroll around the center and drink tea.
- **Day 2:** in the morning, take the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn route. Have lunch and rest. Set off for Xím Vàng around 14:00, take photos until 17:00, then get back in time for dinner.
- **Day 3:** in the morning, go to Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) if it is dry (best time 6:00–10:00, ticket about 20.000đ), board the bus at 12:00 and reach Hanoi around 19:00.

The standard 2-night plan is in our article [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem). If you want to extend the rice trip into Yên Bái, see our article on [the Tà Xùa, Trạm Tấu, Mù Cang Chải route](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-tram-tau-mu-cang-chai).

## How do you get to Xím Vàng?

The per-route motorbike taxi price list on the system does not have a Xím Vàng route yet, so this trip is priced by the day or agreed with the driver in advance.

- **Motorbike taxi by the day:** 500.000đ–600.000đ for 1 passenger, 700.000đ–900.000đ for 2 passengers sharing one bike. Ask clearly whether the plan includes Xím Vàng when you book on the [Tà Xùa motorbike taxi](/xe-om) page.
- **Taxi for the whole day:** 1.700.000đ–2.000.000đ per car, good for families or groups of 4–6 people. See the cars on the [Tà Xùa taxi](/taxi) page.
- **Self-ride motorbike:** a manual motorbike is 180.000đ per day and a PG1 is 280.000đ per day at the [motorbike rental](/thue-xe-may) shops. Only ride yourself if you are used to mountain passes.
- **Your own car:** possible, but you need to be used to mountain roads; read our article on [self-driving to Tà Xùa by car](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-bang-o-to-tu-lai) first.

## What to pack and wear in September?

- Long sleeves and a light jacket; early mornings and evenings in the mountains are noticeably cooler than daytime.
- A compact raincoat, because you can still get rain in September.
- Shoes with grippy soles for dirt roads and field edges.
- A hat and sunscreen for the afternoon out in the fields.
- When taking photos in the fields: walk on the bunds, do not step on the rice, and ask permission if you go close to people's houses. A few more things worth doing are in our article on [responsible travel in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-van-minh).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is beautiful in Tà Xùa in September?

September has ripe rice in Xím Vàng, about 15km from the center, and the Grasslands turning golden. This is the season to go for the rice terraces, not yet the sea of clouds season.

### Which month does the rice ripen in Xím Vàng?

The rice in Xím Vàng ripens from September to early October, and the Mâm Xôi hill is usually at its best from mid-September. After the harvest, the fields are no longer golden.

### How far is it from Tà Xùa to Xím Vàng?

About 15km along provincial road 112, past Đỉnh Gió, roughly 40 minutes by motorbike. The road has steep sections, so hire a motorbike taxi or a taxi if you are not used to riding on mountain roads.

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in September?

The cloud season has not started yet. Clouds may begin in October and are thickest from November to March.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded during the 2/9 holiday?

Yes. National Day is one of the busiest periods of the year, and the nights of 01/09 and 02/09 are charged holiday rates. Moving to the week of 06–12/09 is quieter and cheaper.

### Should you sleep in Xím Vàng or Tà Xùa?

Sleep in Tà Xùa. Accommodation and food services in Xím Vàng are still limited, while the ride is only about 40 minutes, so going there and back in one afternoon is enough.
