TITLE: Tà Xùa in July: mid rainy season, can you go, what to know?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in July: cool 20–26°C, rainy, few clouds, quiet. Which spots work after rain, motorbike taxi and taxi day rates, group of 4 from 663.000đ each.

Tà Xùa in July sits in the middle of the rainy season: it is cool, 20–26°C by day and 17–20°C at night, with a lot of rain and slippery dirt roads. It is a quiet month that suits a budget trip for a group of friends, as long as you accept two things: there are few clouds, and your plan has to give way to the weather. **The July rule is short: go far in the morning, stay close in the afternoon, and ask about road conditions before every route.**

**Quick summary:**

- **Weather:** days 20–26°C, nights 17–20°C, a lot of rain; thick fog in the late afternoon.
- **Clouds:** few, though you may catch low clouds drifting through the valleys after a rainy night. Do not go in July just for cloud hunting.
- **Spots to think twice about:** do not walk Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) in rain or strong wind; the dirt roads up to Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock) and Hồ Mắt Ngọc (Jade Eye Lake) are slippery after rain.
- **Prices:** there are no holiday-rate nights. July 2027 has five Saturday nights: 03/07, 10/07, 17/07, 24/07 and 31/07.
- **Transport on the mountain:** a motorbike taxi for the whole day costs 500.000đ–600.000đ for 1 passenger; a taxi for the whole day costs 1.700.000đ–2.000.000đ per car.
- **Bus from Hanoi:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person.

## Should you visit Tà Xùa in July?

The answer depends on what you expect from the trip.

**Go if you:**

- Want to escape the lowland heat for a few days without spending much.
- Like green mountains, clear skies after rain and photo spots with no one jostling for space.
- Can be flexible with your dates and are ready to change plans during the day to suit the weather.

**Wait for another month if you:**

- Are going for the first time and your goal is a sea of clouds. In that case pick November to March, following the table in our article [When is the best time to visit Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-nao-dep-nhat).
- Plan to ride a motorbike yourself but are not used to steep passes and wet roads.
- Are travelling with older people who find it hard to get around when the roads are slippery.

1–2 days before you leave, message your homestay and the bus company to ask about the rain and the road up. In the rainy season the mountains may have landslide sections or thick fog; people on the spot know better than any general forecast.

## In July, which spots are fine and which should you postpone?

The table below ranks the spots by how much they depend on the weather. Ticket prices are the ones shown on the system's destination pages.

| Spot | Ticket | When it is dry | After rain or in thick fog |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Đỉnh Gió | Free | Good to go, about 2km from the center | Still doable, visibility may be poor |
| Núi đôi thiếu nữ (Maiden Twin Peaks) | 30.000đ on foot, 40.000đ by motorbike taxi | Good to go | Wet grass, wear shoes with good grip |
| Hồ Mắt Ngọc | Free, open 7:00–17:00 | Good to go | Slippery dirt road, take a motorbike taxi |
| Grasslands, Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree), Mỏm cá heo | Mỏm cá heo about 20.000đ | Go in the morning | Slippery dirt road; the rock outcrop has no railing, so postpone |
| Sống lưng khủng long | About 20.000đ | Go at 6:00–10:00, when it is not very windy | Do not go |

Sống lưng khủng long is a narrow ridge with a drop on both sides. In rain, strong wind or dense fog there is no reason to be out there. A section-by-section description and safety notes are in our article [Sống lưng khủng long Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/song-lung-khung-long-ta-xua); for the other route see [Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn](/kinh-nghiem/mom-ca-heo-cay-co-don-ta-xua).

## Getting around the mountain in July: which transport to choose?

Wet roads change the math. Riding yourself is the cheapest but also the riskiest; hiring a local to drive costs more but gives you peace of mind.

| Transport | Price on the system | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-ride manual motorbike | 180.000đ per day | Riders used to steep passes, on dry days |
| Self-ride PG1 motorbike | 280.000đ per day | Riders used to manual motorbikes on steep roads |
| Motorbike taxi per leg | Center to Đỉnh Gió 100.000đ, to the Grasslands cluster 250.000đ–300.000đ, one way for 1 passenger | Visiting 1–2 spots in a morning |
| Motorbike taxi by the day | 500.000đ–600.000đ for 1 passenger, 700.000đ–900.000đ for 2 passengers | Visiting many spots, with the driver waiting |
| Taxi for the whole day | 1.700.000đ–2.000.000đ per car | Groups of 4–6, rainy days |

A group of 4 hiring one taxi for the whole day at 1.700.000đ pays about 425.000đ each, sits in a closed car and does not have to worry about raincoats. The full price list for every leg is in our article on [Tà Xùa motorbike taxi and taxi prices](/kinh-nghiem/gia-xe-om-taxi-ta-xua). If you still want to ride yourself, read our article on [renting a motorbike in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/thue-xe-may-ta-xua) to learn about rental shops and bike types.

The leg from Hanoi does not change with the season: the sleeper cabin bus runs overnight, leaves Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 and reaches central Tà Xùa around 6:00, and the return bus departs at 12:00. In the rainy season, ask the bus company in advance about the drop-off point and the transfer to your homestay, so you do not have to drag luggage through the rain; details are in our article on [whether the sleeper cabin bus drops you at your homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

## How should you plan a rainy day in Tà Xùa?

- **Early morning:** if it rained overnight and the sky is clearing, this is the most likely time of day to see low drifting clouds. Head to Đỉnh Gió, a nearby spot with an easy road.
- **Morning:** a hot breakfast in the center. [Thào Coffee](/cafe/thao-coffee-ta-xua) opens from 5:00, [Nhà H'Mông](/cafe/nha-h-mong) opens from 6:30.
- **Noon:** a set rice meal, then rest in your room. [Bếp ăn Xuân Thì](/nha-hang/bep-an-xuan-thi-ta-xua) has set meals from 90.000đ.
- **Afternoon:** stay around the center, drink tea, watch indigo dyeing. No riding down the passes and no far-off spots after 16:30.
- **Evening:** hotpot or grilled food; at 17–20°C hotpot still hits the spot.

## What to pack for July?

- A two-piece rain suit, and waterproof covers for your backpack and phone.
- Shoes with good grip, plus a pair of sandals; spare socks because shoes get wet easily.
- A light jacket for the evening.
- Insect repellent, and long trousers if you walk trails in damp forest because there are leeches.
- A small flashlight and a power bank, in case a thunderstorm causes a local power cut.
- Cash. For ATMs and phone signal, see our article [Does Tà Xùa have ATMs and wifi](/kinh-nghiem/song-dien-thoai-atm-wifi-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it rain a lot in Tà Xùa in July?

Yes. July is the middle of the rainy season, with a lot of rain and slippery dirt roads. Get around in the morning and avoid long routes after 16:30.

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in July?

Few. Now and then low clouds drift through in the early morning after a rainy night, but a thick sea of clouds like in winter is very hard to come by.

### Is it dangerous to visit Tà Xùa in July?

The main risks are slippery roads, afternoon fog and wet rock outcrops. Ask about road conditions before you set off, hire a motorbike taxi or taxi for far routes, and postpone Sống lưng khủng long when it rains.

### How warm is Tà Xùa in July?

20–26°C during the day and 17–20°C at night. It is cool, and you need a light jacket in the evening.

### Should you rent a motorbike and ride yourself in Tà Xùa in July?

Only if you are used to steep passes and wet roads. A manual motorbike rents for 180.000đ per day; less confident riders should hire a motorbike taxi by the day at 500.000đ–600.000đ for 1 passenger.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded in July?

No. July is the quiet season, with no holidays on the price calendar. Weekends bring visitors escaping the heat, but far fewer than in the cloud season.
