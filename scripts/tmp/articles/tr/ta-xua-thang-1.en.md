TITLE: Tà Xùa in January: tớ dày flowers, 5–10°C nights, should you go?
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in January, the coldest month: days 10–18°C, nights 5–10°C, thick clouds, tớ dày flowers until mid-month. Weekly calendar, room prices, what to pack.

Tà Xùa in January is the coldest month of the year and sits in the middle of the cloud season: daytime is usually 10–18°C, nights and early mornings 5–10°C, and a hard cold snap can drop to 2–5°C. Should you visit Tà Xùa in January? **Yes, if you want both a sea of clouds and tớ dày flowers in one trip and can handle the cold.** What needs careful planning is the two ends of the month: the first few days fall under New Year holiday rates, and the last week is already close to Lunar New Year (Tết).

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** January is within the thickest cloud period of the year (November to March). Some mornings are still clear or completely fogged in, so stay 2 nights to get 2 tries.
- **Temperature:** days 10–18°C, nights 5–10°C, cold snaps 2–5°C. Hoarfrost may appear, without warning.
- **Flowers:** tớ dày (wild Himalayan cherry) blooms until around mid-January; late in the month white plum blossom and village peach blossom start to open.
- **Holiday rates:** on Tà Xùa Booking, the night of 31/12/2026 and the night of 01/01/2027 are charged holiday rates. From the night of 02/01, prices go back to the day-of-week rate.
- **Bus tickets:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person, depending on the operator.
- **Most comfortable window:** 04/01 to 28/01, on Monday to Thursday nights.

## What makes Tà Xùa worth visiting in January?

Three things show up in the same month, and that is why January gets asked about so often.

- **A sea of clouds in mid-season.** It is cold, early mornings are humid, and the gap between day and night temperatures is large. This kind of weather tends to create clouds at Cây cô đơn (Lone Tree), Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock), Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) and the Đỉnh Gió area.
- **Tớ dày flowers.** A tall wild cherry with deep pink flowers that blooms against the blue sky of the dry season. The first half of the month is when you are most likely to see it.
- **The year-end mood in the villages.** In late January the Hmong villages start preparing for Tết, and plum and village peach trees around the stilt houses begin to bloom here and there.

The downsides are the cold and the fog on the mountain pass. ## How cold is Tà Xùa in January, and is there ice or snow?

Central Tà Xùa is about 1.500m high and the cloud hunting spots are higher, so it is noticeably colder than Hanoi.

- **Daytime:** 10–18°C. On sunny days, sitting outside at midday is quite pleasant.
- **Nights and early mornings:** 5–10°C. Wind on the high outcrops makes it feel several degrees colder than the thermometer says.
- **Cold snaps:** can drop to 2–5°C. During these spells hoarfrost may settle white on grass and leaves in the early morning.
- **Ice and snow:** thick ice or snow is very rare. If you see it, count yourself lucky, and do not build the trip around that hope.

Cloud hunting hours this season are roughly 5:30–7:30. The sun rises later than in summer, so getting to the spot at 5:30 is about right. How to read the sky and choose where to stand is covered in our [Tà Xùa cloud hunting guide](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua).

## Where to see tớ dày flowers in January?

Tà Xùa has no ticketed tớ dày garden. The trees grow scattered along the roads, around villagers' yards and on the tea fields, most of all along the road from the center to Háng Đồng. A few things that help you avoid missing them:

- **Timing:** the flowers usually bloom from late December to around mid-January, shifting 1–2 weeks from year to year with the cold spells.
- **The surest way:** message the homestay and ask "are the flowers out yet" before you book, because the host sees the trees every day.
- **Photo hours:** 6:30–8:30, when the sun slants through the petals and there are still clouds in the background.
- **Etiquette:** do not break branches, do not shake the trees to make petals fall, and ask before entering a villager's yard.

The blooming calendar for plum, village peach and other flowers through the year is in our article on [Tà Xùa flower seasons](/kinh-nghiem/mua-hoa-ta-xua).

## What to wear in Tà Xùa in January?

Wear several thin layers instead of one very thick one:

- **Base layer:** a thermal top and thermal leggings.
- **Middle layer:** a sweater or fleece.
- **Outer layer:** a down jacket or thick windbreaker that blocks the wind.
- **Accessories:** a wool hat, scarf, gloves, thick socks, and heat patches for when you stand waiting for sunrise.
- **Shoes:** grippy soles and closed toes, because every cloud hunting spot involves walking on dirt paths.

Cold weather drains phone batteries fast, so bring a power bank. Skin and lips dry out quickly in the dry air, so add lip balm and moisturizer. Our article [What to wear in Tà Xùa in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi) has a more detailed list.

## A 3 days 2 nights itinerary for January

This plan assumes you board the bus on Sunday or Monday evening to get weekday rates.

- **Night 0:** the sleeper cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 21:00–22:00, and you sleep on the bus.
- **Day 1:** arrive in the center around 6:00. If there are clouds, head to Đỉnh Gió right away (about 2km from the center). Have breakfast, leave your bags, check in at 14:00, and stroll around the village in the afternoon looking for tớ dày.
- **Day 2:** at 5:30 take the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn route. Rest at midday to warm up, stop by a tea house or café in the afternoon, and have hotpot in the evening.
- **Day 3:** if it is dry and not very windy, go to Sống lưng khủng long from 6:00. Board the bus at 12:00 and reach Hanoi around 19:00.

For detailed timings, see our [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem). If you only have 2 days, follow the [2 days 1 night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

## How do you get around the mountain in January?

On early January mornings the roads are often foggy and the surface is damp. If you are not used to manual motorbikes, call a local motorbike taxi for the longer routes. One-way prices from the center, according to the price list on the system:

- **Đỉnh Gió:** 100.000đ for 1 passenger, 150.000đ for 2 passengers.
- **Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn:** 250.000đ–300.000đ for 1 passenger, 350.000đ–400.000đ for 2 passengers.
- **Sống lưng khủng long:** 300.000đ–350.000đ for 1 passenger, 400.000đ–450.000đ for 2 passengers.

Groups of 3–6 can ask about a [local taxi](/taxi) to sit in a car out of the wind. If you ride yourself, [motorbike rental](/thue-xe-may) starts from 180.000đ a day for a manual bike.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there clouds in Tà Xùa in January?

January is within Tà Xùa's thickest cloud period, from November to March. Clouds still depend on each day's weather, so stay 2 nights to improve your chances.

### Does it snow in Tà Xùa in January?

Thick ice or snow in Tà Xùa is very rare. Hoarfrost may appear in the early morning during hard cold snaps of 2–5°C, but it cannot be predicted.

### Until when do tớ dày flowers bloom in Tà Xùa?

Tớ dày flowers usually bloom from late December to around mid-January, shifting 1–2 weeks depending on the year. Ask the homestay about the flowers before you fix your dates.

### Which nights in January are charged holiday rates in Tà Xùa?

On Tà Xùa Booking, the night of 31/12/2026 and the night of 01/01/2027 are charged holiday rates. The Lunar New Year 2027 price calendar starts from the night of 06/02, so the remaining nights of January are charged by day of the week.

### Is it better to visit Tà Xùa in early or late January?

Early in the month suits people who want to see tớ dày, and late in the month suits those who like white plum and village peach blossom. Around 04–28/01, going midweek is the quietest and cheapest.

### Should you take young children to Tà Xùa in January?

Yes, if you choose a room with a heated mattress, travel in daylight and limit long waits on windy outcrops in the early morning. Tips on choosing a bus and a room are in our article on [visiting Tà Xùa with young children](/kinh-nghiem/du-lich-ta-xua-cung-gia-dinh-tre-nho).
