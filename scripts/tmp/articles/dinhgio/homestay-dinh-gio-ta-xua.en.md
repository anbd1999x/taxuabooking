**Homestays at Dinh Gio, Ta Xua:** 9 places currently take bookings on Tà Xùa Booking: 8 in the Dinh Gio area plus Pàng Village in Cáo A village right next to it, 118 rooms in total. Monday to Thursday prices start at 200.000đ (a Happy Town bungalow) and top out at 2.100.000đ (Cloud Forest's VIP 360° unit). If you want a bathtub and breakfast included in the price, look at Thác Mây, Cloud Forest, La Chếu, Pàng Village and Chillin House.

**Quick summary:**

- **How many:** 9 homestays, 118 rooms. La Chếu has the most rooms (20), Chillin House the fewest (5).
- **Mon–Thu prices:** the cheapest is 200.000đ for a bungalow at Happy Town; the cheapest private room with a cloud view is 650.000đ at Chạm Hoàng Hôn.
- **Saturday:** prices go up at almost every place. For example, a Thác Mây VIP room rises from 1.300.000đ to 1.700.000đ, and Cloud Forest's Mùa House from 1.800.000đ to 2.500.000đ.
- **Bathtubs:** available at 6 places; the most are at Cloud Forest (10 units) and Thác Mây (3 Lux units plus VIP rooms 1–6).
- **Breakfast included:** 5 places: Thác Mây, Cloud Forest, Chillin House, Pàng Village and La Chếu (19 of 20 rooms).
- **From the center:** the Dinh Gio viewpoint is about 3,1km by road from central Ta Xua, around 7 minutes; a motorbike taxi costs 100.000đ one way for 1 passenger.

## Stay in Dinh Gio or in the center?

It depends on whether you want convenience in the morning or in the evening.

- **Choose Dinh Gio** if cloud watching is the main goal. At the places within 300m, you walk to the viewpoint instead of getting up earlier to ride 3,1km of steep road in the fog.
- **Choose the center** if you want to walk to restaurants in the evening, or you are traveling with older relatives who need to be close to shops.
- **Dinner in Dinh Gio:** Cloud Forest has a restaurant on site, and Happy Town and Pàng Village serve food on the premises. Khèn Coffee House, Nhà của An and Chạm Hoàng Hôn all have a café on or next to the property.

A full comparison of the areas (center, Khe Cải, Mống Vàng, Chung Trinh village) is in the article [which area to stay in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao).

## How do you get from Dinh Gio to the center?

From the Dinh Gio viewpoint to central Ta Xua is about 3,1km, around 7 minutes by vehicle. The road is paved but steep with many bends. Listed motorbike taxi prices, one way:

- Center to Dinh Gio: 100.000đ for 1 passenger, 150.000đ for 2 passengers sharing.
- Dinh Gio to Khèn Coffee: 50.000đ.
- Khe Cải to Dinh Gio: 150.000đ for 1 passenger, 200.000đ for 2 passengers.
- The Grassland, Dolphin Rock and Lonely Tree cluster to Dinh Gio: 250.000đ for 1 passenger, 300.000đ for 2 passengers.
- Dinh Gio to the Dinosaur Spine: 350.000đ for 1 passenger, 450.000đ for 2 passengers.

If you stay far from the center, book a [local motorbike taxi](/xe-om) in advance for early-morning trips. Coming by coach from Hanoi, give the name of your homestay when booking the ticket so the bus company can arrange the transfer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a homestay at Dinh Gio, Ta Xua cost per night?

From Monday to Thursday, the 9 homestays in the Dinh Gio area range from 200.000đ (Happy Town bungalow) to 2.100.000đ (Cloud Forest's VIP View 360°). The highest Saturday night price is 3.000.000đ.

### Which homestay in Dinh Gio is closest to the cloud viewpoint?

Nhà của An, Cloud Forest, Chillin House and Chạm Hoàng Hôn are all less than 300m by road from the Dinh Gio viewpoint, within walking distance. Thác Mây states a distance of 0,5km.

### Which Dinh Gio homestays have a bathtub?

Six places have bathtub rooms: Thác Mây, Cloud Forest, La Chếu, Pàng Village, Chillin House and Khèn Coffee House (1 room). The cheapest is La Chếu's Căn Mây 3, 1.125.000đ from Monday to Thursday.

### Which Dinh Gio homestays include breakfast?

Thác Mây, Cloud Forest, Chillin House and Pàng Village include breakfast for every room; La Chếu includes it for 19 of 20 rooms. Happy Town, Khèn Coffee House, Nhà của An and Chạm Hoàng Hôn do not include breakfast.

### Where should a group of 10 stay in Dinh Gio?

Two rooms take up to 10 guests: Thác Mây's VIP 10 (1.300.000đ) and La Chếu's whole wooden house (1.100.000đ), plus a 200.000đ fee for each guest beyond the standard occupancy.

### Am I sure to see the sea of clouds in Dinh Gio?

No place can guarantee it. The sea of clouds depends on the weather each day; in Dinh Gio you can only choose a room with an open view over the valley. Staying 2 nights gives you 2 mornings to try.
