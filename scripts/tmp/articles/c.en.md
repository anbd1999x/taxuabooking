6am, the cabin bus stops in central Tà Xùa. It is still misty and chilly, and everyone shuffles off, each with a suitcase. Those staying in the centre walk a few hundred metres and they're there. But you booked the most beautiful bungalow in Đỉnh Gió, Khe Cải or Chung Trinh village: you open your map, the signal is weak, and you have no idea how to get the rest of the way. Plenty of people face this on their first Tà Xùa trip. This article answers three questions: **which bus companies drop you at your homestay, which homestays need an extra ride, and what to do if you arrive before check-in time**.

**Quick summary:**

- **Cabin buses reach central Tà Xùa around 6am.** Homestays usually check in at 2pm.
- **Homestay drop-off:** the [Tiến Thành](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-tien-thanh) and [Quang Nghĩa](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2) bus companies advertise taking passengers and luggage right to the door, including homestays far from the centre. Both have a shared rest area to sleep while waiting for check-in.
- **Khánh Châu, Thành Mỹ:** their published routes end in the centre. Ask when booking if you want door drop-off.
- **Going on by yourself:** a motorbike taxi from the centre to Đỉnh Gió or Khe Cải costs 100.000đ for one person, 150.000đ for two.
- **Early check-in:** many homestays allow early check-in if a room is free, with a surcharge from 100.000đ or 30–50% of the room rate depending on the time.

## Where does the cabin bus drop passengers?

All four cabin bus companies on the Hà Nội – Tà Xùa route run overnight, pass Bắc Yên around 4am and reach **the centre of Tà Xùa commune around 6am**. The centre is the main road with homestays, restaurants, cafés and motorbike rentals. If your homestay is here, just walk.

The problem lies with the beautiful but distant areas:

- **Đỉnh Gió area:** [La Chếu](/homestay/la-cheu-homestay), [Thác Mây](/homestay/thac-may-ta-xua), [Khèn Coffee House](/homestay/khen-coffee-house-ta-xua), [Chạm hoàng hôn](/homestay/cham-hoang-hon), [Happy Town](/homestay/happy-town-ta-xua).
- **Khe Cải area:** [Bếp Nhà Táo](/homestay/bep-nha-tao), [Chillout Village](/homestay/chillout-village), [Noong ơi](/homestay/noong-oi-homestay).
- **Chung Trinh village:** [Cloud Forest](/homestay/cloud-forest-ta-xua), [Sunset Clouds](/homestay/sunset-clouds-ta-xua), [Chung Chinh](/homestay/chung-chinh-homestay).
- **Cáo A village (KM18 marker, provincial road 112):** [Pàng Village](/homestay/pang-village-ta-xua).
- **Mống Vàng area, near the centre:** [Mây Bản](/homestay/may-ban-ta-xua), [Mống Vàng Retreat](/homestay/mong-vang-retreat) (about 1km from the centre).

These are also the areas with the most cloud-view rooms, bathtubs and beautiful bungalows. That's no accident: the further from the main road, the more open the view. You just need to plan the last leg.

## Which bus companies drop you at your homestay?

**[Tiến Thành bus company](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-tien-thanh).** The company clearly states it **takes passengers to the homestay gate**, including **homestays far from the centre**, with staff **carrying luggage to the door**. It has a **shared rest area** with beds for passengers to sleep while waiting for check-in. Return fare from Hà Nội 600.000đ, with transfers from 8 provinces.

![The Tiến Thành sleeper cabin bus running at night, with blue LED lights around the body](/uploads/1793518540349-3ef221d28de8.jpg)

**[Quang Nghĩa bus company](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2).** Picks up and drops off passengers and luggage at the homestay, **including homestays far from the centre or on steep roads**, within its service area. It also has a **shared rest area** with beds and shared toilets. Return fare from Hà Nội 650.000đ, departing 10pm from Mỹ Đình. Full review in [Quang Nghĩa bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/review-nha-xe-quang-nghia-ta-xua).

**[Khánh Châu](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-khanh-chau) and [Thành Mỹ](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-thanh-my-2).** Their published routes end in central Tà Xùa. They are cheaper (Khánh Châu 500.000đ, Thành Mỹ 600.000đ return from Hà Nội), good for people staying in the centre or happy to take a motorbike taxi for one more leg. If you want door drop-off, add your homestay's name to the booking request and ask when they call to confirm.

Whichever company you choose, **always give your homestay's name and send its map location in advance** when booking. The company needs to know early to arrange a small vehicle or a driver; telling them once you're already on the bus is usually too late.

## Some homestays sort out the transport themselves

A few homestays sell ready-made packages including the cabin bus, which solves the last leg too:

- **[Chung Chinh Homestay](/homestay/chung-chinh-homestay)** (Chung Trinh village): 2-day 1-night combo 950.000đ per person, 3-day 2-night 1.200.000đ per person, **including a return double-cabin bus Hà Nội – Tà Xùa with pick-up and drop-off at the homestay** (weekend surcharge 100.000đ per person). The homestay is right on the road in the village, with parking.
- **[Mây Bản Tà Xùa](/homestay/may-ban-ta-xua)** (Mống Vàng): offers a return double-cabin bus service with pick-up in Hà Nội.

Ask the homestay when booking: sometimes its package is cheaper and less hassle than booking a bus separately.

## Going on from the centre: motorbike taxi, taxi or rental motorbike?

A **motorbike taxi** is the quick, cheap option for 1–2 people. According to the rates drivers list on Tà Xùa Booking, from the centre to **Đỉnh Gió, Khe Cải or Khèn Coffee costs 100.000đ for one person, 150.000đ for two**, one way. Tell the driver in advance if you have a large suitcase; a motorbike carrying two people plus two suitcases is too much on a slope.

A **4–7 seat taxi** suits large groups or families with lots of luggage. Taxis in Tà Xùa mostly quote day rates (1.700.000đ – 2.200.000đ per car); for a short ride from the drop-off point to your homestay, call and ask first. Detailed fares for each leg are in [motorbike taxi and taxi prices in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/gia-xe-om-taxi-ta-xua); book on the [motorbike taxi](/xe-om) and [taxi](/taxi) pages.

A **rental motorbike** is cheap (usually 150.000đ – 200.000đ a day, and many homestays rent them on site) but **don't ride it yourself at 6am** straight off the night bus: thick fog, unfamiliar roads, and a suitcase in tow. Picking up the bike after you've reached your homestay and caught up on sleep is much safer.

## Arriving at 6am, check-in at 2pm: what to do?

There are three ways, depending on your budget:

**1. Early check-in with a surcharge.** Many homestays allow early check-in if the room was empty the night before. Surcharges vary quite a lot:

- **[Tà Xùa Hill](/homestay/ta-xua-hill), [Bếp Nhà Táo](/homestay/bep-nha-tao):** before 6am, 50% of the listed rate; 7am – 10am, 30%; after 10am, free.
- **[Mây Hạ Retreat](/homestay/may-ha-retreat):** 7am – 10am, 200.000đ per room; 10am – 12pm, 100.000đ.
- **[Noong ơi](/homestay/noong-oi-homestay):** before 6am, 200.000đ per room; 7am – 10am, 150.000đ; after 10am, free.
- **[Happy Town](/homestay/happy-town-ta-xua):** 7am – 10am, 200.000đ per room (standard check-in at 1pm).
- **[Chung Chinh](/homestay/chung-chinh-homestay):** on weekdays, if a room is free, **early check-in and late check-out are free** when you order at least one main meal at the homestay.

A small tip: if you absolutely want a room the moment you arrive, **book the night before as well**. For a large group, the cheapest room for one night split per head can cost about the same as the early check-in surcharge.

**2. Sleep at the bus company's rest area.** Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa have shared rest areas with beds. Sleep another 3–4 hours, freshen up, then go to your homestay.

**3. Drop your bags and start exploring.** Message the homestay in advance to ask about leaving your suitcase, then have breakfast and head to Đỉnh Gió or a cloud-watching café while the clouds are at their best. A suggested plan for that morning is in [what to do after getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa at 6am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## Pre-trip checklist for far-out homestays

1. **When booking a room:** ask the homestay whether the access road is steep and whether cars can drive right up. The [homestay](/homestay) page has an **"Easy road"** filter to quickly find places that are easy to reach by car.
2. **When booking the bus:** choose a company that drops at homestays, write your homestay's name and send its map location.
3. **Before the trip:** save the homestay's number and a motorbike taxi driver's number, in case the signal is weak.
4. **Luggage:** prefer a backpack or small suitcase. Steps up to hillside bungalows are not friendly to a 28-inch suitcase.
5. **Check-in time:** ask about early check-in surcharges in advance, or book the night before if you want a room straight away.

Still choosing an area? [Which area to stay in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao) compares the centre, Đỉnh Gió and Khe Cải. To see the most beautiful units in these far-out areas, read [cloud-view bungalows in Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/bungalow-ta-xua).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the cabin bus to Tà Xùa drop passengers at their homestay?

Some companies do. Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa advertise taking passengers and luggage right to the homestay, including ones far from the centre. Khánh Châu and Thành Mỹ publish routes ending in central Tà Xùa; ask when booking if you want door drop-off.

### What time does the cabin bus reach Tà Xùa?

Around 6am. Buses leave Hà Nội at 9pm or 10pm depending on the company and pass Bắc Yên around 4am.

### How do I get from central Tà Xùa to Đỉnh Gió or Khe Cải?

By motorbike taxi, 100.000đ for one person or 150.000đ for two, one way, according to the rates drivers list. Large groups with lots of luggage should call a taxi.

### Can I check in early?

Many homestays allow early check-in if a room is free, with a surcharge that varies: about 100.000đ – 200.000đ per room, or 30 – 50% of the listed rate if you check in before 10am. Some homestays are free after 10am.

### Where can I rest early in the morning without paying a surcharge?

Tiến Thành and Quang Nghĩa have shared rest areas with beds where passengers can sleep. Or leave your bags at the homestay and go cloud hunting and have breakfast until check-in time.
