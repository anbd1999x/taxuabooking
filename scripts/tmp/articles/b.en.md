You finish work at 7pm, go home, shower, have a relaxed dinner, and only head out to Mỹ Đình with your backpack at 9:30pm, and you still make the cabin bus to Tà Xùa. The next morning, instead of standing in the middle of the commune centre at 6am with a suitcase and a homestay several kilometres away, you are driven straight to its door. Those are the two reasons many people choose **the Quang Nghĩa bus company**: its bus leaves the latest on the route (10pm), and it offers **pick-up and drop-off at your homestay, even if it is far from the centre or up a steep road**. This review covers the details: fares, route, cabin berths, the rest area in Tà Xùa, and what to weigh up before booking.

**Quang Nghĩa at a glance:**

- **Route:** Hà Nội – Phù Yên – Bắc Yên – Tà Xùa and back, daily.
- **Bus type:** sleeper cabin bus, with single and double cabins.
- **Fare from Hà Nội:** 650.000đ return per person.
- **Outbound:** 10:00pm from CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình (Nam Từ Liêm), arriving in Tà Xùa around 6:00am.
- **Return:** 12:00pm from central Tà Xùa, back in Hà Nội in the evening.
- **In Tà Xùa:** drops passengers and luggage at their homestay, with a shared rest area to sleep while waiting for check-in.
- **Check availability:** the [Quang Nghĩa bus page](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2).

## Outbound route: board at 10pm, reach the clouds at 6am

According to the route published by the company:

- **10:00pm, CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình:** board, stow luggage, take your berth. Arrive 15–20 minutes early.
- **Around 4:00am, Bắc Yên town:** a rest stop before the final mountain pass.
- **Around 6:00am, central Tà Xùa:** the bus then drops passengers at the homestays they gave in advance.

10pm is the latest departure of the four cabin bus companies on sale on Tà Xùa Booking (the same time as Thành Mỹ; Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành leave at 9pm). One hour sounds small, but for office workers around Mỹ Đình, Cầu Giấy and Nam Từ Liêm it is the difference between "rushing" and "taking it easy".

Boarding at Mỹ Đình also suits travellers from other provinces: take a coach to Mỹ Đình bus station and connect, without crossing the city.

## Return route: check out at noon, board straight away

- **12:00pm:** passengers are collected in central Tà Xùa. If your homestay is far out, tell the company to be picked up there.
- **Through Bắc Yên town**, then down the pass towards Hà Nội.
- **Evening:** back in Hà Nội, about 8 hours in total.

The return time matches most homestays' check-out time (12pm), so you don't have to sit in a café holding your bags. The return trip runs in daylight; if you get carsick, eat lightly before boarding and read our [tips against carsickness on the Tà Xùa pass](/kinh-nghiem/meo-chong-say-xe-deo-ta-xua).

## What's inside a Quang Nghĩa cabin?

Based on the company's description and real photos of the bus:

- **Private berths with curtains**: draw the curtain and it becomes your own little room.
- **Leather mattress** with blanket and pillow. Mountain nights are cold, and a thick blanket is the most valuable thing on the bus.
- **USB charging port** in each berth, so your phone won't run out overnight.
- **Wi-Fi** on board.
- **Cleaned and disinfected** before every trip.
- **Screens** fitted in the berths, and LED lights along the aisle bright enough to move around without waking the whole bus.

![The front of a Quang Nghĩa cabin bus in black and red, with the door open just behind the driver's cab](/uploads/1794213959093-62d564b3149f.jpg)

According to the company's earlier introduction, its fleet includes **an all-double-cabin bus** as well as single-cabin buses. This is most valuable for **couples** or **parents travelling with young children**: on other buses there are usually only a few double cabins, and they sell out very early at weekends. Not sure whether to choose single or double, upper or lower deck? See [a night on the cabin bus to Tà Xùa](/kinh-nghiem/trai-nghiem-xe-cabin-di-ta-xua).

## The biggest difference: drop-off at your homestay and a place to sleep

The cabin bus reaches Tà Xùa around 6am, while homestays usually **don't allow check-in until 2pm**. For many people, those 8 hours are the most tiring part of the trip. Quang Nghĩa solves this with two services:

**Homestay pick-up and drop-off.** The company takes passengers and luggage right to the door, including **homestays far from the centre** or **on steep roads**, within its service area. The only condition: **give the homestay's name and location in advance** when booking so the company can plan. If you are staying in areas like Đỉnh Gió, Khe Cải or Chung Trinh village, this saves you a motorbike taxi ride and dragging a suitcase uphill in the fog.

**Shared rest area in Tà Xùa.** The company has a rest area with beds and shared toilets where passengers can **catch up on sleep, brush their teeth and freshen up** while waiting for check-in. You can sleep a few more hours before going cloud hunting or having breakfast, instead of wandering around with tired eyes.

For what to do between getting off the bus and checking in, read [getting off the night bus in Tà Xùa at 6am](/kinh-nghiem/xuong-xe-dem-ta-xua-sang-som-lam-gi).

## Compared with other companies on the route

- **Fare from Hà Nội:** Quang Nghĩa 650.000đ, Thành Mỹ and Tiến Thành 600.000đ, Khánh Châu 500.000đ. Quang Nghĩa is the most expensive, 50.000đ – 150.000đ more per person.
- **Departure time:** Quang Nghĩa and Thành Mỹ 10pm; Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành 9pm.
- **Boarding point:** Quang Nghĩa at Mỹ Đình; Thành Mỹ at 24 Đình Núp; Khánh Châu and Tiến Thành around Mạc Thái Tông – Mễ Trì.
- **Homestay drop-off:** Quang Nghĩa and Tiến Thành clearly advertise this service, including homestays far from the centre. The other two publish routes that end in central Tà Xùa.
- **Pick-up from provinces:** Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành and Thành Mỹ have transfer fares for each province. Quang Nghĩa only runs Hà Nội – Tà Xùa, so travellers from provinces should connect to Mỹ Đình.

## Verdict: pros and things to consider

**Pros:**

- **Latest departure on the route (10pm)**, easy for people who finish work late.
- **Drop-off with luggage at your homestay**, even far-out or steep ones.
- **A shared rest area** to sleep while waiting for check-in.
- **Plenty of double cabins**, good for couples and families with young children.
- **Boarding at Mỹ Đình**, convenient for west Hà Nội and passengers from Mỹ Đình bus station.

**Things to consider:**

- **The highest fare** of the four companies. A group of 4 pays 600.000đ more than with Khánh Châu for the whole trip.
- **No transfer fares from provinces.** Travellers outside Hà Nội have to get to Mỹ Đình themselves.
- **Door drop-off "within the service area".** If the homestay is too far or the road too narrow, the company may switch to another arrangement; ask when they call to confirm.
- **No published change or cancellation policy.** Ask before sending a deposit.

**Who is Quang Nghĩa for?**

- Couples who want to share a double cabin.
- Families with young children, and people with a lot of luggage.
- Travellers booked at homestays far from the centre or on steep roads.
- People living in Mỹ Đình, Nam Từ Liêm, Cầu Giấy or finishing work late.

## How to book Quang Nghĩa

1. Open the [Quang Nghĩa bus page](/xe-cabin/nha-xe-quang-nghia-2) and choose your outbound and return dates.
2. Choose the number of single or double cabin tickets for each direction.
3. At the pick-up/drop-off step, **write your homestay's name** to be dropped at the door.
4. Leave your name and phone number and send the request.

The website does not take online payments. You will get a call to confirm your seats and explain the deposit; use your booking code to check it again on the [booking lookup](/tra-cuu) page. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a Quang Nghĩa ticket to Tà Xùa?

From Hà Nội, a return ticket is 650.000đ per person. Double cabin prices and prices for specific dates are shown on the company's booking page.

### Where and when does Quang Nghĩa pick up?

The bus leaves daily at 10pm from CC2 Mễ Trì, Mỹ Đình (Nam Từ Liêm), and reaches Tà Xùa around 6am the next morning.

### Does Quang Nghĩa drop passengers at their homestay?

Yes. The company drops passengers and luggage at their homestay, including ones far from the centre or on steep roads, within its service area. Give the homestay's name and location when booking.

### Where can I rest after arriving in Tà Xùa at 6am?

Quang Nghĩa has a shared rest area with beds and shared toilets where passengers can sleep until homestay check-in time.

### When does the Quang Nghĩa return bus leave?

The bus leaves central Tà Xùa at 12pm, passes Bắc Yên and reaches Hà Nội in the evening, about 8 hours in total.

### Does Quang Nghĩa pick up from other provinces?

The company only runs Hà Nội – Tà Xùa and has no provincial transfer fares. Travellers from provinces can take a coach to Mỹ Đình bus station and board there, or choose Khánh Châu, Tiến Thành or Thành Mỹ, which offer provincial pick-ups.
