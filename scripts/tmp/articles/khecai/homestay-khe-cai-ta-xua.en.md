## Where is Khe Cai in Ta Xua, and how far from the centre?

Khe Cai is a village of Ta Xua commune (Bac Yen, Son La), lying along the road towards Bac Yen from the commune centre. [Amei Hill](/diem-den/amei-hill), the photo spot in the middle of the village, is 1.7km from the centre by road, about 7 minutes. The homestay cluster is a little further in: Bếp Nhà Táo is 0.7km from Amei Hill, Chillout Village 1.3km.

Road distances between the places, measured on the site's map:

- Bếp Nhà Táo to Noong ơi: 0.2km.
- Noong ơi to Chillout Village: 0.5km.
- Bếp Nhà Táo to Chillout Village: 0.7km.
- Tà Xùa Hill in the centre to Bếp Nhà Táo 2.6km (10 minutes), to Noong ơi 2.9km (11 minutes), to Chillout Village 3.3km (12 minutes).

Bếp Nhà Táo and Chillout Village have an easy access road. Noong ơi sits on the hillside and the last stretch is fairly steep, so new riders should go slowly. To compare Khe Cai with Dinh Gio, Mong Vang or the centre, read [which area to stay in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao).

## Can you see clouds and sunsets in Khe Cai?

Yes, if the weather cooperates. Clouds are a weather phenomenon, and no homestay or café can promise them. A few things to know first:

- **Rooms facing the valley:** Chillout Village's Samu category is a single room with a cloud view. If you want to see the valley from bed, say so when booking so the homestay gives you a room facing the valley.
- **Sunset:** many cafés in Khe Cai treat late afternoon as their best hour, such as Trạm Mây, Tiệm Cà Phê Hoàng Hôn and Noong Ơi Coffee. At the 3 homestays you can watch from the yard without riding anywhere.
- **Sunrise:** Khe Cai Coffee opens at 6:00, right in the morning cloud-watching window.
- **[Amei Hill](/diem-den/amei-hill):** best from 6:00 to 10:00 or at sunset. Entry is about 25.000đ to 30.000đ per person, drinks about 50.000đ. The way down is a narrow, long and very steep concrete track; less confident riders should park at the top and take a local motorbike taxi for about 40.000đ return. Weekends are usually crowded.
- **Cloud season:** usually October to April, according to Amei Hill's notes. How to guess which mornings are likely to have clouds is in [Ta Xua cloud hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua); other sunset spots are in [5 places to watch the sunset in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/ngam-hoang-hon-ta-xua).

## Should you stay in Khe Cai or in the centre?

- **Choose Khe Cai** if you stay 2 nights or more, have a motorbike or car, and want spacious grounds, a pool and a sunset view right where you sleep.
- **Choose the centre** if it is your first trip, you stay 1 night, do not drive yourself, and want to walk to restaurants in the evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Khe Cai in Ta Xua?

Weekday prices run from 550.000đ (Noong ơi) to 1.950.000đ (Shan category, Chillout Village) per room per night. On Saturday night most rooms cost 200.000đ to 400.000đ more; Shan keeps one price every night.

Trạm Mây serves set menus from 249.000đ, and Hang Chú takes bookings for hotpot and grilled platters for groups. Bếp Nhà Táo and Chillout Village have restaurants inside their grounds.
