[Omely Homestay Ta Xua](/homestay/omely-homestay-ta-xua) is a newly built homestay in central Ta Xua with 12 rooms, and every room has a glass wall facing the sea of clouds. The rooms come in three types: VIP with 3 glass walls and a bathtub, Luxury with 1 glass wall right by the cloud valley, and Double rooms for groups of 4–6. The selling price on the site starts at 800.000đ/night for a Luxury room on weekdays, and from 900.000đ on Friday and Saturday.

**Quick summary:**
- Omely is in central Ta Xua, has car parking, and shuttle buses drop guests right at the door.
- 12 rooms: 4 VIP rooms (201, 206, 301, 306), 6 Luxury rooms (202, 203, 204, 205, 302, 305), 2 Double rooms (303, 304).
- Sunday to Thursday prices: Luxury 800.000đ, VIP 1.050.000đ, Double 1.150.000đ per room per night.
- VIP rooms have a bathtub set right against the glass facing the clouds, standard 2 guests, maximum 3.
- Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00; 50% deposit, surcharge of 200.000đ/person/night above the standard occupancy.

## Where is Omely Homestay, and is it easy to get around?

Omely is in the central area of Ta Xua. From here, restaurants, cafes and the cloud-hunting spots near the centre are a short trip, so you do not have to go far to eat out in the evening. If you are torn between the centre, Dinh Gio or Khe Cai, read [which area to stay in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/o-ta-xua-nen-chon-khu-nao) to compare distances and styles of stay.

The house has car parking, handy for groups driving up themselves. If you come by bus, the shuttle drops guests right at the homestay door, so there is no need to find a motorbike taxi from the drop-off point. How cabin buses and transfer vans bring guests up to the homestay is covered in [does the cabin bus drop guests at the homestay](/kinh-nghiem/xe-cabin-tra-khach-tan-homestay-ta-xua).

The house also takes bookings for motorbike rental, double cabin buses and round-trip limousines. To combine transport and room in one booking, use the [cabin bus + homestay combo](/combo); if you only need a bus ticket, see the operators on the [cabin bus to Ta Xua](/xe-cabin) page.

## VIP rooms: 3 glass walls and a bathtub facing the clouds

The four VIP rooms 201, 206, 301 and 306 are on the 2nd and 3rd floors, with glass on three sides, so from the bed you can see both the mountains and the valley. The biggest difference is the bathtub placed against the glass wall, so you look straight out towards the clouds while soaking.

The room takes 2 guests as standard, up to 3. Sunday to Thursday the price is 1.050.000đ (list price 1.100.000đ), Friday and Saturday 1.250.000đ (list price 1.300.000đ). A third guest costs an extra 200.000đ/night. To see more homestays with this kind of bathtub in Ta Xua, read [bathtubs with a sea-of-clouds view in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/bon-tam-view-bien-may-ta-xua).

![Bathtub against the glass wall in VIP room 201 at Omely Homestay Ta Xua](/uploads/1795865445876-a2dd4ec24217.jpg)

With so much glass, VIP rooms get very bright early in the morning. If you need total darkness to sleep, close the curtains fully before bed. The room suits couples on an anniversary or two friends who want the room with the most view in the house; an itinerary idea for two is in [Ta Xua for couples](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-cho-cap-doi).

## Luxury rooms: the cheapest, still right by the cloud valley

Omely has 6 Luxury rooms: 202, 203, 204, 205, 302, 305. Each has 1 large glass wall, on the side of the house closest to the cloud valley. There is no bathtub, but the bed faces the glass directly, so you still see the valley when you wake up.

This is the lowest-priced type: 800.000đ/night Sunday to Thursday (list price 850.000đ), 900.000đ on Friday and Saturday (list price 950.000đ). For two people on a weekday, that is about 400.000đ per person per night. For three people add the 200.000đ surcharge, 1.000.000đ/night in total.

![Luxury room 202 at Omely Homestay with a large glass window facing the cloud valley](/uploads/1795865635413-bd67713c11e4.jpg)

## Double rooms: for families and groups of 4–6

The two Double rooms 303 and 304 are spacious, with 2 double beds and a glass window looking straight at the sea of clouds. Standard 4 guests, maximum 6; from the 5th guest there is a surcharge of 200.000đ/person/night.

In numbers, a Double room for 4 people on a weekday is 1.150.000đ, about 287.500đ per person per night. With a full 6 people it is 1.150.000đ plus 400.000đ in surcharges, 1.550.000đ in total, about 258.000đ per person. Groups of 8–12 can take both Double rooms plus a few Luxury rooms; how to split rooms for a big group is in [Ta Xua for groups of 10–20](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-nhom-dong-thue-nguyen-homestay).

## Will you definitely see the sea of clouds from the room?

Every room's glass wall faces the valley, so when the clouds rise you can see them from inside. The photos on the house's page include clouds covering the valley in the evening and the sun over a band of clouds.

That said, clouds are a matter of weather. Not every morning has a thick sea of clouds; some days are clear, some days are covered in fog. To improve your chances, pick your timing with [Ta Xua cloud-hunting tips](/kinh-nghiem/kinh-nghiem-san-may-ta-xua) and check the coming days on the [Ta Xua weather](/thoi-tiet-ta-xua) page before fixing your dates.

## Things to know before booking

- **Air conditioning:** the house is installing two-way air conditioners; for now the rooms have fans. In winter bring enough warm clothes, see [how cold Ta Xua gets in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi); for homestays with air conditioning or heated mattresses, see [Ta Xua homestays with air conditioning and heated mattresses](/kinh-nghiem/homestay-ta-xua-co-dieu-hoa-dem-suoi).
- **Children:** under 6 free; from 6 years old counted as adults, meaning they count towards capacity and a surcharge applies above the standard.
- **Deposit and payment:** 50% of the room price as a deposit when booking, the remaining 50% paid at check-in.
- **Cancellation:** 20 days' notice refunds 70% of the deposit; 15 days refunds 50%; 10 days allows postponement; within 7 days no postponement and no refund. If you cannot go, you can pass the room to someone else yourself or ask the house to do it.
- **Food:** the house is right in the centre, so restaurants are easy to reach; places to eat are in [Ta Xua restaurants](/kinh-nghiem/quan-an-nha-hang-ta-xua) and dishes to try in [what to eat in Ta Xua](/kinh-nghiem/an-gi-o-ta-xua).
