## How is a bungalow different from a regular homestay room?

In Tà Xùa, a "bungalow" is usually a **small standalone house** that shares no wall with other rooms. The differences you notice right away:

- **Quieter:** you won't hear the next room dragging suitcases at 5am to go cloud hunting.
- **Private view:** many have one to three glass walls, so you can see the clouds from bed.
- **Private balcony or porch:** brew a pot of tea and watch the sunset without sharing the space.
- **Slightly pricier** than rooms in the shared building of the same homestay, with a big jump on Saturday nights.

In return, bungalows are usually scattered across a hillside, so expect to walk up and down steps. If you travel with older relatives, ask which unit is closest to the main path.

## 4 questions to ask before paying a bungalow deposit

1. **Which unit faces the clouds?** Within the same bungalow category, the first and last unit in a row can have completely different views.
2. **Is there heating or an electric blanket?** Winter nights in Tà Xùa can drop below 10°C, and wooden houses hold heat less well than brick ones.
3. **What is the Saturday price?** Many places charge 30–70% more than on weekdays. Going on Thursday or Friday night is the easiest way to save.
4. **Is the access road easy?** This matters more than you think. Some of the most beautiful bungalow clusters are in Chung Trinh village or the Đỉnh Gió area, far from where the cabin bus drops passengers. Getting off the bus at 6am, you will need to know how to get there, especially with a suitcase.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a bungalow in Tà Xùa per night?

From 200.000đ (Happy Town) to about 2.800.000đ (the VIP Tree House at Cloud Forest on a Saturday night). Most air-conditioned bungalows in the centre are around 750.000đ on weekdays and 950.000đ – 1.250.000đ on Saturday.

### Which bungalows in Tà Xùa have a bathtub?

The Mây Hill Bungalow and Đồi Mây unit at Tà Xùa Hill, VIP Bungalows 1 and 4 at Mon's House, the Căn Mây units at La Chếu, the Lux and VIP units at Thác Mây, and most of the houses at Cloud Forest.

### Which bungalows include breakfast?

La Chếu, Thác Mây, Cloud Forest and Tà Xùa Retreat include breakfast in the room price.

### Which bungalow is best for 4 people?

The 4-guest bungalow at Tà Xùa Retreat (1.248.000đ on weekdays, breakfast included), Căn Mây 4 at La Chếu, the 2-bedroom Bungalow Duplex at Lao Xan, or a house at Cloud Forest.

### Should I stay in a bungalow in winter?

Yes, but choose one with heating, an electric blanket or two-way air conditioning. Tà Xùa Hill, Mon's House, La Chếu and Cloud Forest all have heating or electric blankets.
