TITLE: Tà Xùa in December: thick clouds, 5–10°C nights, room prices
EXCERPT: Tà Xùa in December, mid cloud season: days 10–18°C, nights 5–10°C, tớ dày flowers late in the month. Room prices, New Year nights and itineraries.

Tà Xùa in December is the middle of the cloud season and also a cold month: daytime is usually 10–18°C, nights and early mornings 5–10°C, and a hard cold snap can drop to 2–5°C. If you are asking whether December is a good time to visit Tà Xùa, the answer is **yes, and it is one of the months when you are most likely to see a sea of clouds**, as long as you pack warm enough and avoid booking rooms at the last minute for the two holiday nights of 31/12/2026 and 01/01/2027.

**Quick summary:**

- **Clouds:** Tà Xùa's cloud season runs from October to April and is thickest from November to March. December sits right inside that window, but clouds are still weather, and no day is guaranteed.
- **Temperature:** days 10–18°C, nights 5–10°C, cold snaps 2–5°C. Hoarfrost may appear in December and January, and no two years are alike.
- **Flowers:** tớ dày flowers (wild Himalayan cherry) usually start blooming in **late December** and last until mid-January.
- **Room prices:** weekdays are cheapest, Saturday nights cost more, and the **nights of 31/12/2026 and 01/01/2027** are charged holiday rates on the Tà Xùa Booking system.
- **Getting there:** a return sleeper cabin bus ticket from Hanoi costs 500.000đ–650.000đ per person; buses leave Hanoi at 21:00–22:00 and reach central Tà Xùa around 6:00.
- **Sleeping warm:** many homestays have heated mattresses or air conditioning in every room, for example Tà Xùa Hill, Bản Mây Tà Xùa Retreat and Mống Vàng Retreat.

## Is Tà Xùa beautiful in December?

Yes, if what you are after is clouds. December is cold, humidity is high in the early morning, and the gap between day and night temperatures is large. That is exactly the kind of weather that tends to create a sea of clouds at spots like Cây cô đơn, Mỏm cá heo (Dolphin Rock), Sống lưng khủng long (Dinosaur Spine) or Đỉnh Gió. On some days the clouds even spill through a mountain gap as a "cloud waterfall" seen from the Đỉnh Gió area, which is more likely after light rain the night before and a sunny morning the next day.

Three downsides to know in advance:

- **It is genuinely cold.** Standing on a rock outcrop at 5:30 in the morning, the wind makes it feel several degrees colder than the thermometer says.
- **Fog.** On some mornings everything is white: instead of clouds below your feet, the cloud sits right where you are standing. The Tà Xùa grasslands also tend to be foggy in December and January.
- **Crowds at the end of the month.** The last week of December runs into the New Year holiday, so the best rooms sell out early.

## Weather, clouds and hoarfrost in December

December is mid-winter in the high mountains of Tà Xùa. It rarely rains and the midday sun is dry, but early mornings and evenings are noticeably cold.

- **Cloud hunting hours:** roughly 5:30–7:30 is when the clouds are thickest. The sun rises later than in summer, so there is no need to get up too early and then stand waiting in the wind.
- **Signs that clouds are likely:** light rain the night before, a clearing sky the next morning, high humidity.
- **Hoarfrost:** may form on grass and leaves in the early morning during the cold snaps of December and January. It cannot be predicted, so treat it as a bonus if it happens and do not build the whole trip around it.
- **Ice and snow:** thick ice or snow in Tà Xùa is very rare. Do not go expecting snow like photos of colder countries.

The way to improve your odds of seeing clouds is to stay 2 nights so you get 2 mornings. ## How do room prices change in December?

Homestay prices on Tà Xùa Booking are charged per night, based on the check-in date of that night. December has three levels:

- **Weekdays (Monday to Thursday):** the lowest level. Examples of weekday listed prices: [Tà Xùa Hill](/homestay/ta-xua-hill) from 375.000đ, [Thơ Garden](/homestay/tho-garden-ta-xua) from 450.000đ, [Bản Mây Tà Xùa Retreat](/homestay/ban-may-ta-xua-retreat) from 500.000đ.
- **Saturday night:** usually at least 100.000đ more than weekdays, and at some places the gap reaches about 1.000.000đ per room; check the exact price for each night when you pick dates on the homestay page. Some homestays may also charge more on Friday night, so check when booking.
- **Holiday nights of 31/12/2026 and 01/01/2027:** each room's own holiday rate applies. According to the table compiled in our article on [Tà Xùa homestay prices for Tết 2027](/kinh-nghiem/di-ta-xua-dip-tet-2027), holiday nights are usually 300.000đ–700.000đ per room higher than weekdays.

The table comparing weekday, Saturday and holiday prices for each homestay is in our article on [weekday, weekend and holiday homestay prices](/kinh-nghiem/gia-homestay-ta-xua-ngay-thuong-cuoi-tuan-le). The fastest way to check is to pick your dates on the [Tà Xùa homestay](/homestay) page: the prices shown already match each night of your stay.

## New Year 2027 in Tà Xùa: which days cost less?

01/01/2027 is a **Friday**, followed by Saturday and Sunday. The system's holiday price calendar only covers **the night of 31/12/2026 and the night of 01/01/2027**; the night of 02/01 (Saturday) goes back to the Saturday rate.

The money-saving trick is the overnight bus. If you board the sleeper cabin bus on Thursday evening 31/12, you sleep on the bus that night instead of paying for a room. You arrive on the morning of 01/01, stay the night of 01/01 (holiday rate) and the night of 02/01 (Saturday rate), and take the bus back at noon on Sunday 03/01. The whole trip includes **only one holiday-rate night** and you still get 2 mornings of cloud hunting.

Booking a [sleeper cabin bus and homestay combo](/combo) follows exactly the same logic: the departure date is the night you board the bus, and the check-in date is the following day. For New Year, lock in your room and tickets before mid-December. For each homestay's holiday-night prices, see the Tết 2027 article mentioned above.

## What to wear in December and where to stay warm?

### What to wear

The rule is layers: a thermal top underneath, a sweater or fleece in the middle, and a down jacket or thick windbreaker on the outside. Add a wool hat, scarf, gloves, thick socks and shoes with good grip for the walks out to the rock outcrops. The full list and tips for beating the cold are in our article [What to wear in Tà Xùa in winter](/kinh-nghiem/ta-xua-mua-dong-lanh-mac-gi).

## Suggested December weekend itineraries

### 2 days 1 night (leave Friday evening)

- **Friday evening:** the sleeper cabin bus leaves Hanoi at 21:00–22:00.
- **Saturday morning:** arrive in the center around 6:00. If there are clouds, head straight to Đỉnh Gió (about 2km from the center, motorbike taxi 100.000đ one way for 1 passenger). Have breakfast and leave your bags at the homestay.
- **Saturday afternoon:** check in, rest to warm up, and watch the sunset in the late afternoon at Tà Xùa Farm or around Đỉnh Gió.
- **Sunday morning:** at 5:30 take the Grasslands, Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn route. A motorbike taxi from the center costs 250.000đ–300.000đ one way for 1 passenger, depending on the driver.
- **Sunday 12:00:** bus back, arriving in Hanoi around 19:00.

An hour-by-hour plan is in our article [Tà Xùa 2 days 1 night itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-2-ngay-1-dem).

### 3 days 2 nights (leave Thursday evening)

- **Friday morning:** arrive, rest, stroll around Bụa A village in the afternoon, and have hotpot and grilled food in the evening.
- **Saturday morning:** Sống lưng khủng long (6:00–10:00 is the best time; only go when it is dry and not very windy). A motorbike taxi from the center costs 300.000đ–350.000đ one way for 1 passenger.
- **Sunday morning:** the Mỏm cá heo, Cây cô đơn or Đỉnh Gió route, then board the bus back at 12:00.

Staying 2 nights gives you 3 mornings to bet on the clouds. See also our [Tà Xùa 3 days 2 nights itinerary](/kinh-nghiem/lich-trinh-ta-xua-3-ngay-2-dem). Mountain passes are often foggy in December; if you are not used to manual motorbikes, take a [local motorbike taxi](/xe-om) instead of [renting a motorbike](/thue-xe-may) and riding yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tà Xùa beautiful in December?

Tà Xùa is beautiful in December if you want to go cloud hunting, because it is in the middle of the thickest cloud period (November to March). Late in the month you also get tớ dày flowers. The downsides are the cold and the crowds in the last week of the month.

### Is there ice or snow in Tà Xùa in December?

Thick ice or snow in Tà Xùa is very rare. Hoarfrost may appear in the early morning during the cold snaps of December and January, but it cannot be predicted, so you should not go just to hunt for frost.

### How cold is Tà Xùa in December?

Tà Xùa in December is usually 10–18°C during the day and 5–10°C at night and in the early morning. Cold snaps can drop to 2–5°C, and on high, windy outcrops it feels even colder.

### What flowers bloom in Tà Xùa in December?

Tà Xùa in December has tớ dày flowers (wild Himalayan cherry), which usually start blooming in late December and last until mid-January. The timing varies from year to year.

### Is Tà Xùa crowded in December?

In early and mid-December, weekdays are fairly quiet and weekends busier. The last week of December runs into the New Year 2027 holiday, so it is the busiest, and rooms with good views sell out early.

### Is the night of 31/12 in Tà Xùa charged holiday rates?

Yes. On Tà Xùa Booking, the night of 31/12/2026 and the night of 01/01/2027 are charged holiday rates; the night of 02/01/2027 goes back to the Saturday rate. If you board the sleeper cabin bus on the evening of 31/12, you sleep on the bus that night and only have one holiday-rate night.
